# Shots — Episode 3 (version 2)

One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (90 sentences). All stills NEW in the Bakunawa
look; writing in pictures is added in code. Nobody just standing; never the same framing twice in a row.

**Must not resemble Episode 1 (v2) or Episode 2 (v2):** from Ep 1: the queue, the pawnshop, the paresan, the
shrine in night rain, torches, a blade at a neck, tied wrists, dogs at his feet, papers torn in the air, a
woman under a lamp post. From Ep 2: a rampart at dusk, a phone screen, a stone kitchen, a wall bursting, rats,
a drain, a river bank, an old woman carried on his back. In particular Indio is NOT seized or held here: he
is pushed out by a crowd's open hands and left alone outside a fence. This episode's world is a bright dusty
afternoon, a wooden house, a window, a drum.
