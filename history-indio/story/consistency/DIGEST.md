## Episode 1 — Ang Unang Talon (ep01-ang-unang-talon)
- **Real moment**: Sigaw ng Pugad Lawin, August 1896 (official: 23 Aug, Pugad Lawin, since 1963; date and place disputed); Bonifacio and Katipuneros tear their cedulas.
- **What happens now**: Jobless Indio in Quezon City is turned away for lacking a cedula (s6), fails to pawn "ang lumang medalyon ng Lolo ko" ("tanso lang 'yan", s9), and shelters from rain at the Pugad Lawin Shrine. He reads the marker aloud, the medallion heats, a black dog from the light says "Kumapit ka, Indio" (s18), and he is in August 1896 watching the cedulas torn. Back in the rain he has a tattoo, the dog is gone, and he names it Usman.
- **How it ends**: s39 "sa kabilang kalye, may babaeng nakatingin sa akin, na parang kilala niya ako." Question s40: "pupunitin mo rin ba ang cedula mo, o dehins?" Follow s41: "At mag-follow na kayo, mga repapips, para sa dagdag werpa namin ni Usman sa susunod na talon."
