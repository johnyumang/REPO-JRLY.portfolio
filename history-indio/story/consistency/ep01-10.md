## Episode 1 — Ang Unang Talon (ep01-ang-unang-talon)
- **Real moment**: Sigaw ng Pugad Lawin, August 1896 (official: 23 Aug, Pugad Lawin, since 1963; date and place disputed); Bonifacio and Katipuneros tear their cedulas.
- **What happens now**: Jobless Indio in Quezon City is turned away for lacking a cedula (s6), fails to pawn "ang lumang medalyon ng Lolo ko" ("tanso lang 'yan", s9), and shelters from rain at the Pugad Lawin Shrine. He reads the marker aloud, the medallion heats, a black dog from the light says "Kumapit ka, Indio" (s18), and he is in August 1896 watching the cedulas torn. Back in the rain he has a tattoo, the dog is gone, and he names it Usman.
- **How it ends**: s39 "sa kabilang kalye, may babaeng nakatingin sa akin, na parang kilala niya ako." Question s40: "pupunitin mo rin ba ang cedula mo, o dehins?" Follow s41: "At mag-follow na kayo, mga repapips, para sa dagdag werpa namin ni Usman sa susunod na talon."
- **Form**: 42 sentences, one voice (Indio); two quoted lines inside narration (pawnshop s9, dog s18). All narration, no played dialogue scene. Fairness block s28–s33 ("Ang tiyak" s30, "Ang pinagtatalunan" s31); no "kathang-isip" line spoken anywhere. No trivia break, no multispeaker file.
- **Ledger after this episode**: tattoos 1 (s36); medallions: Lolo's bronze medallion, worn by Indio (unnamed); gear none; creatures/allies: Usman, black talking dog, appears only in the jump; powers: reading a marker aloud triggers the jump, clothes change (s20), rain freezes (s16), Usman "naaamoy... kapag hindi tiyak ang kuwento" (s29); hologram not stated; wishes not stated; money: short of fare, one bread for dinner, no vehicle; age/year not stated; Usman: gone in present (s37); M: unnamed woman across the street (s39); family: Lolo (medallion's owner) only, "Mag-isa lang ako, sanay na" (s11); villains none; new characters: Indio, Usman (invented); real people: Bonifacio and Katipuneros, given "Mabuhay ang Pilipinas!" (s26, in facts.md as Valenzuela's version).
- **Problems**:
  - s13 "Pugad Lawin Shrine sa Bahay Toro" versus s32–s33, which list "Bahay Toro" as a rival to "Pugad Lawin". The viewer hears the same place on both sides of the dispute.
  - s11 "Mag-isa lang ako, sanay na" versus Ep 3 s4–s6 (a Lola who calls and cooks sinigang) and Ep 10 s12 (a Lolo who raised him).
  - s8–s9: he tries to pawn the medallion as worthless; Ep 7 s27 "kinakausap ni Lolo ang medalyon" and Ep 10 s16 "Basahin mo nang malakas, apo" say he grew up watching it matter.
  - s14 "Wala akong magawa, kaya binasa ko nang malakas" (boredom) versus Ep 10 s17 "Kaya pala binabasa ko pa rin nang malakas ang bawat marker" (lifelong habit). Ep 10 s15 also puts the boy at this same Bahay Toro marker; Ep 1 treats the place as new.
  - s38 Indio chooses the name "Usman... gaya ng aso ni Rizal"; Ep 4 s18 makes the dog literally that Usman. The name is both his whim and the dog's real name.
  - History he could not know while standing in 1896: s22 "Ilang araw pa lang mula nang mabunyag ang Katipunan", s32 "ang opisyal mula 1963".
  - Threads never picked up in the block: the missing cedula and the job requirement (he simply has a job in Ep 2 s3).
- **Strongest**: s27 "'yung papel na kulang sa akin kaninang umaga, pinupunit nila para sa kalayaan." **Weakest**: s30–s33, four flat lines of dispute with nothing on screen happening.

## Episode 2 — British sa Maynila (ep02-british-sa-maynila)
- **Real moment**: British siege and capture of Manila, Intramuros, fall on 6 October 1762; occupation to April 1764.
- **What happens now**: Indio is now a delivery rider; his first order, pancit for "M", goes to Baluarte de San Diego where nobody waits. He reads the sign aloud, jumps to October 1762, sees the wall breached and the redcoats storm in. He then recites the surrender, the ransom, Anda in Bacolor, the Paris peace and the 1764 departure. Back at dusk he has a second tattoo, cold pancit and a phone ping.
- **How it ends**: s42–s44 "Tumunog ang phone ko. Delivered na raw, may tip pa. Galing kay M." Question s46: "susuko ka ba, o sasama ka kay Anda?" Follow s47: "At mag-follow na kayo, guys, para sa dagdag lakas namin ni Usman, at pambili ng bagong pancit!"
- **Form**: 48 sentences; Indio 47, Usman one clause ("ayan ka na naman", s11). Narration throughout; no dialogue scene. Fairness s34–s36 ("Ang tiyak", "Ang hindi malinaw", "ayon sa tradisyon"); no fiction line spoken. No trivia break.
- **Ledger**: tattoos 2 (s39); medallion: Lolo's, with Indio; gear none; allies: Usman; powers: same jump; hologram/wishes not stated; money: delivery job, tip from M (vehicle not named); age not stated; Usman gone in present ("mag-isa na ulit", s38); M: customer name on the order, pays and tips remotely; family not mentioned; villains none; new characters none; real people: Archbishop Rojo, Simón de Anda, no words.
- **Problems**:
  - Talking-head: s13–s17 (5 sentences) and s27–s36 (10 sentences) are pure recital; the only action is s18–s19.
  - He stands in October 1762 but narrates "Pebrero 1763" (s31), "Abril 1764" (s33) and the Cainta tradition (s36): knowledge with no source in the story.
  - s28 "lumaban kasama ang mga Pilipino": anachronistic label for 1762.
  - Repeats from Ep 1: "binasa ko nang malakas" (s9), "Uminit ang medalyon" (s10), "Lumamig ang medalyon" (s37), new tattoo (s39), alone again (s38), a last-second woman/M sting (s44).
  - Never picked up: the cedula; how M knew where he would jump is only answered generally in Ep 6 s53.
- **Strongest**: s0 "ang hawak ko lang, isang bilao ng pancit!" **Weakest**: s27–s33, seven sentences of offstage history.

## Episode 3 — Ang Sabi ni Lola (ep03-ang-sabi-ni-lola)
- **Real moment**: Kawit, Cavite, 12 June 1898: flag shown from a window of Aguinaldo's house, march played, Acta read by Bautista, 98 signers.
- **What happens now**: Lola calls him home to Kawit, asks "Apo, uminit na ba ang medalyon?" (s7), says Lolo also vanished, gives him an empty old box that fits the medallion, and sends him to the Aguinaldo Shrine. In 1898 Usman points at a woman carrying a toddler who wears the same medallion. Back home Lola says the toddler was "ang lolo ng Lolo ko" (s36).
- **How it ends**: s38–s39 "Pero may isa pa raw sikreto si Lolo. Nasa Calamba ang sagot." Question s41: "Ano'ng lumang bagay sa bahay n'yo ang may kuwentong hindi pa naikukuwento?" Follow s42: "At mag-follow na kayo, guys, para sa dagdag lakas namin ni Usman, at pang-ulam ni Lola!"
- **Form**: 43 sentences; Indio about 40, Lola 2 (s7, s35), Usman one clause (s16). About 6 sentences of played scene (s6–s8, s34–s35), the rest narration. Fairness s27–s31; fiction spoken at s30 "At ang kathang-isip: ang babae at ang batang may medalyon." Trivia (boondocks) after s23.
- **Ledger**: tattoos 3 (s33); medallion: Lolo's ("Parehong araw, parehong tatsulok", s25), with Indio; a second copy on the 1898 toddler; gear: empty box; allies Usman; powers same; hologram/wishes not stated; money not stated; age not stated; Usman gone in present; M: not named (the woman with the child is unidentified); family: Lola alive in Kawit, knows everything; Lolo "bigla-biglang nawawala" (s10), keeps "isa pang sikreto"; toddler = Lolo's lolo; family guards the medallion "Mula 1898" (s37); villains none; new characters: Lola, the woman, the toddler (invented); real people: Aguinaldo, Marcela Agoncillo, Bautista, no words.
- **Problems**:
  - s33 the third tattoo appears only after the return, so the Indio standing in Kawit has two. Ep 8 s0 says "nakita ko ang sarili kong tatlo pa lang ang tattoo".
  - s37 "Mula 1898, pamilya na namin ang nagbabantay ng medalyon" versus Ep 9 s17 "Nahanap niya [Rizal] ang una, ang medalyon n'yo" (Rizal died in 1896).
  - s23–s24: the child already wears the medallion while carried; Ep 8 s14 shows M arriving, lifting him "sandali" and hanging it on him.
  - Indio looks at this woman here but does not recognise the red-umbrella woman in Ep 5 s46 or Ep 6 s18 as her, and Ep 8 never remarks on it.
  - s11–s12 the box is handed to him ("inabot niya sa akin"); Ep 7 s7 calls it "kahong iniwan ko kay Lola". The leaving is never said.
  - s29 the balcony correction is a new fact first heard inside the fairness block.
  - Repeats: marker read aloud (s13), heat/cool (s14, s32), "Balik ako... mag-isa, may pangatlo nang tattoo" (s33).
- **Strongest**: s7 "Apo, uminit na ba ang medalyon?" **Weakest**: s19–s22, four list sentences (flag, band, Acta, 98 signers).

## Episode 4 — Ang Aso ni Pepe (ep04-ang-aso-ni-pepe)
- **Real moment**: Calamba, June 1869: the boy José Rizal leaves for Biñan with his brother Paciano (dog "Usman" from biographies only).
- **What happens now**: On his day off Indio goes to the Rizal Shrine on Lolo's one-word clue, "Calamba". Usman runs to a stone house where a small boy stands with a big black dog; "Sabi ni Usman, ako 'yan" (s18). The boy hugs the dog and leaves in a karomata. Usman says he knows Indio because "kaamoy mo kasi siya" (s37).
- **How it ends**: s40 Lola's text: "apo, nabasa ko na ang lumang sulat ni Lolo, kadugo natin si Pepe." Question s42: "Ano'ng pangalan ng alaga mong laging naghihintay sa'yo?" Follow s43: "At mag-follow na kayo, guys, para sa dagdag lakas namin ni Usman, at pambili ng buto niya!"
- **Form**: 44 sentences; Indio about 41, Usman 2 clauses (s18, s37), Lola 1 (s40). Mostly narration; the parting (s23–s27) is a played scene without words. Fairness s28–s33; fiction at s32. Trivia (tuko) after s23.
- **Ledger**: tattoos 4 (s39); medallion with Indio; gear: box (not mentioned); allies: Usman, now Rizal's childhood dog; powers same; hologram/wishes not stated; money: has a job with a "day off" (s9); age not stated; Usman: "Sa talon lang siya lumilitaw" (s5); M not in episode; family: Lola texts; Lolo left "Isang salita lang" (s7) and also "lumang sulat" (s40); bloodline to Rizal stated; villains none; new named: Paciano, Alipato (real); real people: the boy Rizal, given a hug and a farewell not in the record (labelled s32).
- **Problems**:
  - s7 "Isang salita lang ang iniwan niya" versus s40 "lumang sulat ni Lolo" in the same episode, and versus the long letter of Ep 7 s11–s16.
  - s29 "Ang tiyak, ayon sa mga talambuhay: may asong Usman si Rizal": facts.md says the name could not be traced to Rizal's memoir; "tiyak" overstates. It also repeats s20 and Ep 1 s38.
  - s13 "Lumitaw si Usman, pero walang biro ngayon" is reused as Ep 6 s16 "Lumitaw si Usman, pero hindi bumati".
  - s19–s22: four sentences of biography (dog, pony, mother, moth) with nothing happening.
  - s30–s31 the "Sa Aking Mga Kabata" doubt is a new topic that appears only in the fairness block.
  - Repeats: marker (s10), heat/cool (s11, s34), "mag-isa ulit" (s38), "pang-apat na akong tattoo" (s39), cliffhanger by Lola's message as in Ep 3.
- **Strongest**: s27 "Naiwan ang aso sa tarangkahan, nakatingin sa daan." **Weakest**: s30–s31, a poem dispute unrelated to the scene.

## Episode 5 — Dalawampung Milyon (ep05-dalawampung-milyon)
- **Real moment**: Treaty of Paris, 10 December 1898: Spain cedes the Philippines, US pays $20 million; Felipe Agoncillo shut out, protests 12 December.
- **What happens now**: Sheltering from rain in the Santuario del Santo Cristo, San Juan, Indio refuses to read the Agoncillo marker aloud, but reads a ₱20 bill with handwriting: a copy of the marker signed "M". In Paris he overhears "Bente milyones" and counts the Filipinos at the table: "Wala" (s22). Usman pulls him to "the right window": Agoncillo writing alone. Back in San Juan he has five tattoos.
- **How it ends**: s46–s47 "may babaeng may pulang payong. Itinaas niya ang limang daliri, tapos nawala." Question s49: "Binili ba tayo, o ipinamigay nang hindi tinatanong?" Follow s50: "At mag-follow na kayo, guys, para sa dagdag lakas ko sa susunod na talon, at pang-gas, kahit bente lang!"

- **Ledger**: tattoos 5 (s43); medallion with Indio; gear: ₱20 bill from M; allies Usman; powers same (handwriting copy of a marker also works); hologram/wishes not stated; money: "pang-gas" implies a motorbike; age not stated; Usman absent in present ("mag-isa", s42); M: writes the bill, shows five fingers, first red umbrella; family: "text ni Lola" (s3); villains none; new: Felipe Agoncillo (real, no words); real people given unrecorded words: the commissioners' "Bente milyones" (s15–s16).
- **Problems**:
  - Guard-rail: s15–s16 "may narinig akong numero. Bente milyones." facts.md lists this as invented; it is not labelled in the spoken fairness block.
  - s35 "Umungol si Usman sa salitang binili": nobody has said "binili" before s35.

  - s33–s34 "sumiklab ang giyera... Dalawang araw lang, lusot na ito sa Senado": "ito" grammatically points at the war, not the treaty.
  - Talking-head: s26–s34, nine sentences, running to February 1899 while he stands in December 1898.
  - Repeats: rain shelter at a shrine (s4 = Ep 1 s12–s13); woman across the street (s46 = Ep 1 s39); heat/cool; "Balik ako sa San Juan, mag-isa" (s42); tattoo count (s43).
  - s23 "Anim na buwan pa lang..." is said again in Ep 8 s9.
  - s6, s44 "ayaw kong basahin": why he now refuses to jump is not said.
- **Strongest**: s21–s22 "Binilang ko ang mga Pilipino sa mesa. Wala." **Weakest**: s38–s40, a rebuttal to arguments the script never made.

## Episode 7 — Ang Sulat ni Lolo (ep07-ang-sulat-ni-lolo)
- **Real moment**: Paco Cemetery, 30 December 1896 to August 1898: Rizal's secret unmarked burial, Narcisa's search, the "RPJ" slab, exhumation.

Lolo names him "José Indalecio Bracken" (s11), says he made five jumps, refused the red-umbrella woman and buried something at the wall. Usman digs up a gold-rimmed lens; through it the days speed past. The letter's last line sends him to Kawit.
- **How it ends**: s37–s38 "At hindi ang medalyon ang hinahanap nila, apo. Ang Huling Tala." then s41–s42 "Mahigit labimpitong buwan pa ang Hunyo 1898. Chill lang, maghihintay kami ni Usman." Question s44: "Kung may lente ka, saan ka unang sisilip?" Follow s45: "At mag-follow na kayo, guys, para sa dagdag lakas namin ni Usman sa Kawit!"
- **Form**: 46 sentences; Indio 37, Lolo (letter) 9 (s11–s16, s36–s38). No live dialogue; Usman silent. Fairness s30–s35; fiction at s34, and s35 "Sa totoo, walang kilalang inapo si Rizal." Trivia (colasisi) after s17.
- **Ledger**: tattoos 5 (s40); medallions: none with Indio; gear: ang lente ("Katotohanan lang ang ipinapakita", s25), dead phone; allies Usman; powers: lens speeds time and shows only truth; hologram not stated; wishes not stated; money not stated; age not stated, full name given; Usman with him in 1896–97; M: asked Lolo for the medallion, was refused; family: Lola sent the photo; Lolo had "lima ang talon... lima ang marka", talked to the medallion and "may sumagot" (s28), told of "labindalawang datu, natutulog sa tanso" (s29); villains: first "nila" (s37); new: Narcisa Rizal (real, no words).
- **Problems**:
  - s7 "Caption niya: sulat ni Lolo, nasa sapin ng kahong iniwan ko kay Lola": Lola's caption contains "ko"/"kay Lola" in Indio's voice.
  - s6 the photo is "hindi ko pa nabubuksan" although Lola announced the letter in Ep 4 s40 and he lost sleep over it in Ep 5 s3.
  - s13 Lolo was at "umagang ito" on his fifth jump; Indio reaches it on his sixth trip. Ep 8 s19 and Ep 9 s18 then call Galilee "Ikaanim kong talon".
  - s16, s36: Lolo buries a lens for him in 1896 and writes "hintayin mo ang babae sa Kawit"; how Lolo knew Indio would be stranded there is never said.
  - s32–s33 the coffin and "ang papel sa sapatos ni Rizal" are first mentioned in the fairness block.
  - s20–s24: five sentences of burial history in a row.
  - Reveal structure repeated: Lolo's hidden legacy (box Ep 3, word and letter Ep 4, letter and lens here, tin and notebook Ep 10 s59).
- **Strongest**: s17 "Minana ko pala ang tinakbuhan ni Lolo." **Weakest**: s7, the confused caption line.

## Episode 8 — Ang Huling Tala (ep08-ang-huling-tala)

- **Ledger**: marks 6 (s32); medallions: family medallion (diwa of Rajah Sulayman) now on the 1898 toddler; M's own medallion (unnamed), used for travel; star medallion "ang Huling Tala" in M's umbrella; gear: lens, kasulatan/map (M's); allies: Usman, M; powers: Sulayman's voice; lens reads the map; "Tatlong hiling ang bituin, pero tulog hangga't hindi gising ang labindalawa" (s24); hologram not stated; wishes 0 spent; money: bakery wages; age not stated; Usman with him in 1898 (plus a second Usman); M: ally, explains five fingers; family: Lolo refused her; Lola addressed (s34); villains: "May isang nakatingin sa payong, hindi sa watawat" (s25); new: Rajah Sulayman (real, long dead, given one invented line, labelled s29–s30); Johnson, Dewey, Delfina Herbosa (real, no words).

  - s15 loop (his own medallion becomes the family's) versus Ep 9 s17 (Rizal found it).
  - Ep 6 s50 "Hindi sa'yo ang medalyon" versus s21 "kung hindi ko kinuha, hindi ito makakarating sa'yo".
  - s16 "ang boses na sumagot kay Lolo": it never spoke to Indio in Ep 1–6.
  - s4–s6: Ep 7's 17-month wait is spent in three sentences.
  - Talking-head: s7–s12, six fact sentences; s9 repeats Ep 5 s23; how he knows Johnson was "hindi sugo ng Amerika" is not shown.
  - s33 "dalawang libong taon pabalik" from 1898 is about 100 BC; Ep 9 s68 says year 30.
  - Guard-rail: s17 invented first-person words for a real ruler.

## Episode 9 — Limang Tinapay (ep09-limang-tinapay)
- **Real moment**: Sea of Galilee: the feeding of the five thousand as written in the four Gospels (Mt 14, Mc 6, Lc 9, Jn 6); no year in the text.
- **What happens now**: Under a tree in Kawit, M says the first point is "aral", not a medallion, and tells the family story: medallions were datus' anito, scattered by conquest, traced by "Lolo Pepe". They jump on M's medallion; Indio narrates the feeding from the edge of the crowd. After dark Usman smells oil; Indio lifts a clay lamp that shows a watcher in a black coat and dies when he lies about being afraid.
- **How it ends**: s77–s78 M: "Babalik tayo sa panahon mo: sa dati mong paaralan sa Quezon City." Indio: "At hawak ko na ang unang gamit ng Season 2: ang ilawan." Question s80: "Sa apat na salaysay, aling detalye ang ngayon mo lang nalaman?" Follow s81: "At mag-follow na kayo, guys, para sa dagdag lakas namin ni Usman sa susunod na talon!"
- **Form**: 82 sentences; Indio 59, M 18, Usman 5. Played dialogue s4–s24, s47–s61, s76–s78 (about 40); Gospel narration s25–s46 (22); fairness s63–s75 (13 sentences); fiction at s69, s70, s75. Trivia (kangkong) after s18.
- **Ledger**: marks: not stated (6 from Ep 8); medallions: M's; star ("Tatlong hiling sa bulsa ko", s76); family medallion in 1898; gear: lens, map, ang ilawan (shows secret watchers; burns only while the holder tells the truth); allies: M, Usman; powers: "agimat" changes clothes (s19); hologram not stated; wishes 0 spent; money not stated; age not stated; Usman in Kawit 1898 and Galilee, speaks; M: guide, sets rules (s21); family: "Lolo mo", "Lolo Pepe" = José Rizal; villains: figure in "itim na abrigo at malapad na sombrero" (s53); new: none named; real people: Jesus, Felipe, Andres with exact 1905 quotations; Rizal given an invented motive (s14–s17, labelled s75).
- **Problems**:
