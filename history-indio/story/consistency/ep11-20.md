# Consistency audit — Episodes 11 to 20 (as they are)

Read for each episode: `script.txt`, `script_multispeaker.txt`, `sentences.json`, `facts.md`, `endcard.txt`, `trivia_break.json`. Sentence numbers are the 0-based index in `sentences.json`. `script.txt` and the multispeaker file carry the same words as `sentences.json` (only tone tags differ). "Dialogue" below = lines by other speakers plus Indio's own direct speech; everything else is Indio narrating in the past tense.

Shared skeleton of all ten (not repeated under each episode): hook, then "Ako 'to si Indio." / "Samahan mo akong mag-time travel, at tuklasin ang history natin."; a notebook clue; a marker read aloud; "Saksi lang ako."; a creature met and "Nakita na kita." plus M raising the Sandugo; a medallion with one or two diwa lines; the hologram; "Grrr, linawin natin" and the Tiyak / Pinagtatalunan / Kuwentong-bayan / Kathang-isip block; "Kaya ikaw, ka-Indio, i-comment mo." plus a question and the follow line.

## Episode 11 — Ang Sugo ng Butuan (ep11-ang-sugo-ng-butuan)
- **Real moment:** Butuan, 1007 and 1011: Rajah Kiling's envoy asks the Song court for Champa's rank; a 1011 mission presents a gold tablet; eleven balangay dated 689 to 988.
- **What happens now:** Broke in Surigao on a second-hand motor with a sidecar, Indio says "Sana yumaman ako" and the star grants it: his first wish. He buys a pickup and a jet ski, reads aloud at the Balangay Shrine and lands in 1011 with M and Usman. In a lake he finds Kiling's medallion (Ang Sugo, understands all languages), calms the Yúmud without a fight, and it is recorded. A shadow with no owner crosses the water and the hologram appears for the first time in the block.
- **How it ends:** New clue (a gold debt forgiven at a river mouth in Laguna); M: "At may sumusunod na sa pera mo, Indio, ramdam ko." (s66). Question: "Kung may isang hiling ka, gagastusin mo ba sa pera?" (s68). Follow: "At mag-follow na kayo, guys, para sa dagdag lakas namin ni Usman sa susunod na talon!" (s69).
- **Form:** 70 sentences. INDIO 53, M 8, USMAN 7, KILING 2. About 19 dialogue, 51 narration. Fairness block s59 to s63, opened by Usman s58. Fiction line: s63 "Kathang-isip: kami nina M at Usman, ang medalyon, ang hiling, ang anino at ang hologram." Trivia after s19.
- **Ledger:** marks: "Anim na marka" (s51), one lights (s50). Medallions: M the Sandugo (s42); Indio Ang Sugo (Kiling); "labindalawang puwang, at tatlo na ang may ilaw" (third not named here). Gear: "lente, ilawan, kuwaderno, bituin at kasulatan" (s52). Creatures: Yúmud, "nakahahanap ng nawala sa tubig" (s53). Powers: Sugo used (s37); Sandugo seals. Kaalaman 12% plus "isang tandang parang tandang pananong" (s54). Wishes: 1 ("una mong hiling", s12). Money/vehicles: ₱120, then zeros that do not fit the screen; motor with sidecar "hulugan pa", pickup, jet ski, big watch, two phones. Age/year: not stated. Usman: only in the jump light and 1011 (s19 to s58); vanishes s64; s7 states the rule. M: decodes the clue, names the power, twice senses a follower, does not know the sign (s57). Family: only "kuwaderno ni Lolo" (s4). Sila: someone photographs the pickup (s16); the ownerless shadow (s48); Usman smells nothing (s49). New names: Rajah Kiling (real, long dead, diwa); Yúmud (folklore). Real people: Kiling gets two invented lines (s34, s45).
- **Problems:**
  - s8 "ubos na ang gas at ang pera ko" against s9 "isang daan at dalawampung piso na lang"; both sentences also say the same thing.
  - s16 and s66 are the same beat twice (M reports a follower); it returns again in Ep12 s61.

  - facts.md header cites the diwa lines as sentences 33 and 44 and the label as 62; in the file they are s34, s45, s63.
  - Opened, never picked up in the block: the ownerless shadow (s47 to s49); the question-mark sign (s54, once more in Ep12 s49, then gone); Ang Sugo is never named or used again in Ep12 to 20 although Dezhou, Sakai, London and Acapulco follow; the Yúmud's skill is not used at the Mactan reef (Ep20 s26).

- **Strongest:** s38 to s41, the Yúmud says "bantay ako, at magnanakaw lang ang tinatakot ko" and Indio answers "Nakita na kita."
- **Weakest:** s21 to s25, the date list between the arrival and the hunt.
