# Audit, Episodes 41 to 50 (the last war), as they are

Read for each episode: `script.txt`, `script_multispeaker.txt`, `sentences.json`, `facts.md`, `endcard.txt`, `trivia_break.json`. Sentence numbers are the 0-based index in `sentences.json`. `script.txt` and `sentences.json` carry the same words in all ten episodes (only the tone tags differ). "Other block" means the thing is not planted in Episodes 41 to 50.

## Block-wide findings (read these first)

1. **No time travel after Ep 41.** Every one of Eps 41 to 49 says "Samahan mo akong mag-time travel, at tuklasin ang history natin" (s3), but the only jump is Ep 41 s9-16. Ep 44 has a "window" on 1570 (s10-13) with nobody entering it. Eps 42, 43, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49 have no real moment on screen; the "real moment" lives only in the fairness block.
2. **Told, not played.** Indio speaks 55 to 65 of about 70 sentences in every episode. Ep 44: 62 of 65. Ep 50: 29 of 29.
3. **Words given to God:** none directly. Closest calls: Genesis 9:11 read in the first person in Indio's own voice (Ep 42 s66 with "nakasulat:", Ep 49 s59 with no "nakasulat"); "Ipinatatawag ka, Saksi" spoken by M "at ang buong hukbo niya" (Ep 45 s54-55); the notebook that writes by itself "Nakita mo na, apo" right after the wish (Ep 49 s28-29).
4. **Scripture, quoted versus summarised.** Marked "buod": Ep 42 s65, Ep 46 s60, Ep 47 s64, Ep 49 s60. Exact quotes with reference: Ep 42 s23 and s66, Ep 48 s65, s66, s68, Ep 49 s59, Ep 50 s14-15. **Paraphrased with no "buod":** Ep 41 s60-61, Ep 46 s59, Ep 44 s6 (verse phrase used as narration), Ep 47 s67 ("ama ng kasinungalingan").
5. **Words given to Lucifer and angels:** Sinag 6 sentences (Ep 41 s42-43, Ep 42 s57-58, Ep 46 s18-19); Lucifer 8 (Ep 48 s18-22, 24, 26, 29); Albo, a Watcher, 3 (Ep 46 s27-29); M, of Miguel's host, in six episodes. Ep 48's fiction label (s70) never names Lucifer or his words.
6. **The same beats repeat:** Usman growls at each lie (Ep 41 s48, Ep 42 s59-60, Ep 44 s24-25, Ep 46 s20-21, Ep 48 s23-30); "the sorting of tiyak / kathang-isip is the weapon" (Ep 43 s51, Ep 44 s26-28, Ep 46 s23, Ep 48 s33-37); "kneel" (Ep 41 s42, Ep 46 s19); "saksi lang" (Ep 46 s36, Ep 48 s18); the hand at Bagumbayan (Ep 46 s57, Ep 47 s40); Usman turns away and whistles (Ep 44 s53, Ep 47 s51); every line of Lola is about food (Ep 47 s28, s35, Ep 48 s63, Ep 49 s15).
7. **The father has three names:** "Tatay Teddy" (Ep 41 s55), "Itay" (Ep 47 s29), "Papa" (Ep 48 s47, Ep 49 s18, Ep 50 s12).

## Episode 41 — Ang Panunumpa (ep41-ang-panunumpa)

- **Real moment:** Manila, steps of the Legislative Building, morning of 15 November 1935: the Commonwealth is inaugurated before about 300,000 people; Manuel Quezon takes the oath (seen from far, no words).
- **What happens now:** Indio, 42 and in his second year as President, has finished 112 arks. On the day of his "postponed" oath at the Luneta he jumps to 1935, watches the faces, and writes nothing. Back in the present the ark workers unveil a bronze marker with his name and "Walang maiiwan"; he swears on it, the hologram shows Kaalaman at 100%, and the question mark turns into a shepherd's staff. Usman weeps; the sky goes black, a red dragon spews a wall of water, Sinag offers to stop it for one kneel, Indio refuses and sends everyone to the arks.
- **How it ends:** "Umangat ang unang arko sa unang alon." / "Nagsimula ang gunaw." Question: "Kung ikaw ang hinilingang lumuhod para sa lahat, ano ang isasagot mo?" Follow: "At mag-follow na kayo, guys, para sa dagdag lakas namin ni Usman sa susunod na talon!"

## Episode 46 — Ang Mga Pinili (ep46-ang-mga-pinili)

- **Real moment:** none on screen. Fairness block: Genesis 4:17 and 4:22 (Cain's town, Tubal-cain the smith), Genesis 3:24 (the flaming sword), the Laguna Copperplate of AD 900 naming Jayadewa, and the Watchers of 1 Enoch.
- **What happens now:** Three weeks of rain; the arks are tied together in Manila Bay. Tala has come back from Tubal's boat and says she refused.
