## Episode 31 — Ang Pader sa Papel (ep31-ang-pader-sa-papel)
- **Real moment**: none dated; Kawit, Cavite, "kasalukuyan": Kawit is low ground that catches upland water and coastal Cavite floods repeatedly (facts.md rows 1–2).
- **What happens now**: The whole family eats sinigang at Lola's table for the first time (Teddy, Liwayway, white-haired Tala, M, Usman now in the present). Three days of rain; the river overflows; the family and M move the street to the roofs with the Punyal, the Agos and boats; nobody is lost but Lola's house sinks. On the roof Indio reads the unbuilt flood-wall signboard (Alcaín Builders) through the lens and decides "Hindi na sapat ang saksi lang". A week later he files as candidate with a handwritten sign.
- **How it ends**: s66–70: sign "José Indalecio Bracken, saksi", the Trinity blade in his pocket, hologram 90. Question s72: "Kung ikaw ang kapitan bukas, ano ang una mong ipapatayo?" Follow s73: "At mag-follow na kayo, guys, para sa dagdag lakas namin ni Usman sa susunod na talon!"
- **Form**: 74 sentences. INDIO 59, LOLA 4, USMAN 3, TEDDY 2, LIWAYWAY 2, TALA 2, M 2. About 30 played with dialogue (s5–19, s32–42, s53–58), about 35 narrated. Fairness block s59–63 (tiyak s60–61, pinagtatalunan s62, kathang-isip s63). Invented-label line: s63 ("Kathang-isip: ang pamilya ko, ang mga anino, ang pader sa papel at ang Alcaín Builders; alamat ang Biringan, at modernong imbento ang Agartha"). Trivia after s28 (sampaguita), just before the flood.
- **Ledger**: marks: not stated. Medallions: "kahon ng medalyon" in Indio's pocket (s33–34), Agos ni Lakandula used (s40); count not stated. Gear: Punyal (s39), lente (s47), talim ng Trinity (s69). Creatures: none; "mga anino" (s26). Powers: Punyal cuts every leash, Agos slides the boats, lens shows the past. Hologram 90 (s70). Wishes: not stated. Money: "may pera pa ako sa bangko" (s65). Age/year: not stated ("kasalukuyan"). Usman: in the present, speaks (s19, s27, s58), "hindi siya maglalaho pag-uwi ko" (s67). M: at the table, "pamilyang binabantayan niya" (s15), on the rope (s42), asks "saan ka papasok?" (s53). Family: Lola; Papa/Teddy thin, gone "Dalawampung taon" (s7), "bago ako naligaw" (s56); Mama/Liwayway; Ate Tala white-haired, back from Biringan (s11–14). Sila: shadows that do not get wet and have no smell "gaya ni Sinag" (s26–28). New names: Alcaín Builders (invented firm). Real people: none.
- **Problems**:
  - s2–3 "Samahan mo akong mag-time travel" but the episode has no jump at all.
  - s41 "Naroon na ang mga bangka, itinali ng mga kapitbahay noong binalaan ko sila, bago ako umakyat sa Banahaw" is a retroactive fix told, not shown, and it undercuts s49–51 "walang nagbago... Nanonood, hindi humahawak" (he did act).
  - s30 "Tuhod, baywang, dibdib", s45 "lumubog pa rin ang bahay ni Lola" against Ep32 s4 "guhit ng tubig, halos baywang".
  - s65 "Walang pera para sa tarpaulin, kahit may pera pa ako sa bangko" contradicts itself inside one sentence.
  - s62 calls barangay-from-balangay "Pinagtatalunan"; Ep32 s38 states it as plain fact.
  - s64 "Pagkalipas ng isang linggo" he is already a candidate; Ep32 s3 says "Ilang buwan matapos ang baha".
  - Paid off here, planted outside the block: s25 "ipinakita sa akin ng orasan sa Agartha", s41 Banahaw, s14 Biringan, s16 "ikaw ang nagdala sa apo ko pauwi noon pa".
  - Opened and never picked up in the block: s69 "ang talim ng Trinity, tahimik, naghihintay"; s56 Teddy's "bago ako naligaw"; Teddy and Liwayway never appear again after Ep32 s49.
  - Redundant: s49–52 says "saksi lang ako" four ways; s1/s21/s29 "tatlong gabi / tatlong araw / ikatlong gabi".
- **Strongest**: s36–37, Mama counting children on each roof, "Pito sa kabilang bubong, anak, pito, kumpleto."
- **Weakest**: s64–70, the candidacy is a list of props (sign, blade, hologram) with no scene.

## Episode 32 — Ang Kapitan (ep32-ang-kapitan)
- **Real moment**: an undated Tagalog shore "noong ika-labing-anim na siglo"; Plasencia's 1589 account of Tagalog customs; R.A. 7160 minimum age 18 for punong barangay.
- **What happens now**: Months after the flood Indio, 25, walks the alleys with Ma'am Celia, Bea, JM and Usman and beats three-term Kap Ramil Samonte. With Ang Kasulatan he finds a true signature, a fake receipt and an erased paragraph; under the "wall" there are only old sacks and a shadow. He goes back to the signing day and memorises the nail-chewing man, then watches a datu divide a harvest. He shows the erased paragraph, digs a real canal, Kap Ramil confesses; Rico Bulan attacks; Sinag offers "Isang pirma lang".
- **How it ends**: s59–62 M at the window, "isang hakbang na lang", Lola: "Kumain muna kayo, mga apo!"; after the fairness block, s68 hologram 90 and "sino ang lalaking may pako?" Question s70: "Kung ikaw ang kapitan bukas, ano ang una mong bubuksan na folder?" Follow s71: same line as Ep31.
- **Form**: 72 sentences. INDIO 57, USMAN 4, LALAKI (Kap Ramil) 3, SINAG 3, GURO 1, BEA 1, JM 1, RICO 1, LOLA 1. About 20 played, 45 narrated. Fairness block s63–67. Invented-label lines: s66, s67 ("Kathang-isip sina Indio at Usman; totoo ang kasaysayan"). Trivia after s25 (banana ketchup), in the middle of the paper reveal.
- **Ledger**: marks: not stated. Medallions: Ang Kasulatan, "ang medalyon ni Jayadewa" (s21), Indio. Gear: not stated. Creatures: Yúmud (s26), tikbalang (s50). Powers: Kasulatan reads what is true, forged or erased; jump to a recent day (s31) and to the 1500s by reading a marker (s35). Hologram 90 (s68). Wishes: not stated. Money/vehicles: JM's "lumang motor na may sidecar" (s15); Rico "nag-imbento ng kuwento tungkol sa pera ko" (s52). Age: "Bente-singko anyos na ako" (s8). Usman: present, red scarf, "campaign manager" (s5–7). M: at the window (s59). Family: "buo ang pamilya pero tahimik, at hindi lumalabas si Ate Tala sa araw" (s49); Lola (s61). Sila: shadow on the sacks (s28–30), nail-chewing man who counts to three (s33), Sinag "ang Nahulog", woman in white (s54). New names: Kap Ramil Samonte, Ma'am Celia, Bea, JM, Rico Bulan (all invented, alive). - **Problems**:
  - s38 "Ang salitang barangay ay galing sa balangay" as fact, against Ep31 s62 "Pinagtatalunan".
  - s26 "Yúmud, diwa ng tubig sa kuwentong-bayan ng Manobo" against Ep37 s18 "ang diwa ng tubig mula sa Butuan" and Ep37 s61 / Ep38 s61 "Kathang-isip: ... ang Yúmud".
  - s18, s46–47: Kap Ramil's lines are tagged LALAKI and nothing in the narration says who is speaking; "Ganyan talaga 'yan, iho" floats.
  - s48 "Umamin si Kap Ramil ... at naging kagawad": a man who signed a false completion certificate is given a seat, no consequence (same pattern as Vangie, Ep33 s34).
  - s38–41: four sentences of Plasencia/1589 lecture while he is supposedly standing on the shore; s41 "Ginamit pa ng mga Espanyol ang ulat niya sa paglilitis" is nothing he could see there.
  - s66 does not label Sinag, Rico, Bea, Celia, JM or the nail-chewing man.
  - Opened, not picked up in the block: s49 Tala never going out by day; s50 the tikbalang misleading "estrangherong naghahanap sa amin" (who?).
  - Repeats: s64 "amoy putik pa rin" (Ep31 s64); P1, P2, P3, P5, P6 all start here.
- **Strongest**: s31–34, behind the cupboard, the engineer's shaking hand and the man who chews nails and counts to three.
- **Weakest**: s35–42, the shore scene: a lecture with one image ("hinahatian ng datu ang ani").

## Episode 33 — Ang Bayan (ep33-ang-bayan)

Tobías "Tubal" Alcaín arrives in person with Kalawang; Ang Kalakal shows the bid is three times the true price and that Tubal wants "ang pintong binubuksan ng labindalawa". Engineer Vangie Dulay comes forward; cooperatives build the wall; it holds in September. Sinag offers a casino; Rico attacks the fifty vehicles.

Money/vehicles: "Limampung sasakyan sa garahe!" (s54). Age: 29 (s6), "may ilang puting buhok na ako" (s3). Usman: present, "Dalawang buto na ang bayad" (s12), smells rust (s22). ## Episode 34 — Ang Senado (ep34-ang-senado)

On the third night of reading the budget Bea spots an odd font; Ang Kasulatan shows a 100-million dike line inserted after the committee vote for Dalisdis Diking Corporation. He watches the delegates enter Barasoain, then reads the line aloud wearing Ang Tinig and it is struck out. Senator Gorio Madlangbayan warns him; Sinag offers the finance committee; Usman sits on the paper; Totoy, 17, gives him a small balangay.
- **How it ends**: s56–59, a dark Makati office: Tubal "Talunin natin siya sa kuwento", Rico "Ako ang bahala sa kuwento." Then the fairness block and hologram 92 (s66). Question s68: "Kung may linyang isinisingit sa dilim, sino ang dapat unang makaalam?" Follow s69: same line.

Creatures: kappa (s47–49), sarimanok ridden (s50–51). Powers: Tinig carries truth to everyone, silence for a lie (s30). Hologram 92. Wishes: not stated. Money/vehicles: "ang mayamang may limampung sasakyan" (s11). Age: 35 (s7), "mas marami na ang puting buhok ko" (s6). Usman: present (s5, s44–45). M: absent. ## Episode 35 — Ang Bitag (ep35-ang-bitag)

He watches the wall of Bilibid and learns that a word, "bandido", can be glued to a life. Sinag leaves a bottle; he talks to Ma'am Celia until dawn and pours it out. M rests her head on his shoulder.
- **How it ends**: after the fairness block, s62–64: a subpoena for a Senate hearing next month, his party tells him "mag-leave ka muna", hologram stuck at 92. Question s66: "Kung may pangalang idinikit sa'yo na hindi totoo, paano mo ito tatanggalin?" Follow s67: same line.

Age: "Trenta'y singko" (s5), six months in the Senate (s4). Usman: present, "hindi tumatanda" (s6). M: arrives wet, head on his shoulder, "Hindi ako aalis ngayon, hindi tulad dati" (s53–55).
