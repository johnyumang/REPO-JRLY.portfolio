# Facts — Episode 24 (version 2): Ang Reyna ng Lungsod na Lumubog (Plato's Atlantis, a story told about 360 BC)

**What is true here is a text, not a place.** Atlantis appears first, and only, in Plato's dialogues *Timaeus*
and *Critias*. The episode enters the page (BIBLE section 14, ruling 2) and the storyteller says so: "Hindi
ito lugar: ito ang Atlantis, isang kuwentong isinulat ni Platon" (s23).

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- Atlantis is never shown or said to be a real sunken city; the teacher and the storyteller both say it is a
  story, and the trivia repeats it. No pale or "lost" race, no ancient technology, no map location.
- No living person, firm, brand, app or church. "Sabi nila", "sabi sa internet", "sabi ng mga iskolar" name
  nobody. Nick Joaquin is in this file only, not in the script.
- No scripture and no rite. The fallen one is shown only by a shadow with burnt wings; no verse is attached,
  and she is not the Lalaking Nakaputi. Vice by suggestion only: he touches the cup and never drinks.
- "He confessed, so it worked" is not the comeback (section 14 ruling 7). "Natutukso ako." lights a lamp
  that was not dead from a lie; what frees M is the look and the one cut the friend taught.
- The salok is NOT spent in this episode (it is wanted in Episode 25). No mark is gained.
- Thinned to four beats (section 14 ruling 9): the siyokoy's return "against the net of the Pugita" (section
  5 table) is NOT used; the Agos, the Punyal and the "Binti" greave of the old script are dropped.
- Usman is only inside the page. Nothing in the story says a part is invented. Trivia at the end; no follow card.
