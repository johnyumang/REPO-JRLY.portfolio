## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- Rizal: no word, no voice, no invented act beyond sitting and writing, no face (from behind).
- British soldiers of 1762 are shown carrying books down a stair: people at work, no caricature.
- Nothing is taken from a museum, a library or a real collection.
- No living person, firm, brand, party or church. No president is named in the tax line. No app is named.
- Usman is only inside the jump (s28 to s77) and has four turns. M speaks today (s16, s24 to s25, s86) and in 1888 (s72 to s73, s76 to s77); her medallion carries them (s26; B1). Nell has six turns.
- The storyteller has 228 of 540 words (42 percent) and never more than three sentences in a row.
- No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia at the end. No follow card.
- Rule 11: Ang Dagat (s8), M (s15), Rico (s20 to s21: the vlogger who reached the chest in Laguna first, seen only on a screen), Usman (s28), the lens (s30), the creature (s36), Nell (s41), Rizal (s50, s53), the ilawan (s59), the knots (s72) and Lundag's name (s73) are each introduced before they matter. Tubal is a name only (s17 to s18).

## 4. Not done, on purpose (for the owner)

- **Rico does not film him losing in 1888** (BIBLE section 5 says he does). Cut for length and for one thread:
  it would need the forged medallion explained. The loss is "live" at the gate instead (s19 to s23).
- **The kappa is not called at the Thames**, and the hologram and KAALAMAN are not shown.
- **The cliffhanger is at the gate under the umbrella, not in a hotel.**
- **The present-day opening** runs from s6 to s26: the today-thing itself (s6 to s14) is about 25 seconds, but
  with the gate scene before the jump it is nearer 55.
