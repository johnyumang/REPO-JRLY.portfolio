called loot: it is "isang libro tungkol sa mga isla nila". The "Lolo Pepe" family story of version 1 is not in
this script (BIBLE section 14, ruling 3).

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- Rizal: no invented word, no invented act beyond sitting and writing, no face (from behind). The one line is
  his own printed sentence, framed by the storyteller as what he will write.
- British soldiers of 1762 are shown carrying books down a stair: people at work, no caricature.
- Nothing is taken from a museum, a library or a real collection.
- No living person, firm, brand, party or church. No president is named in the tax line. No app is named.
- Usman is only inside the jump (s26 to s65). M says two lines today and one in the hotel.
- No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia at the end. No follow card.
- Rule 11: M (s9), Rico (s18), Usman (s26), the lens (s28), the creature (s32), Nell (s38), Rizal (s43, s46)
  and Lundag's name (s63) are each introduced by the storyteller before they speak or matter. Tubal is a name
  only, glossed by M (s16).

## 4. Not done, on purpose (for the owner)

- **Rico does not film him losing in 1888** (BIBLE section 5 says he does). Cut for length and for one thread:
  it would need the forged medallion explained. The loss is "live" at the gate instead (s17 to s20).
- **The kappa is not called at the Thames**, and the hologram and KAALAMAN are not shown.
- **The present-day opening** runs from s7 to s23: the today-thing itself (s7 to s16) is about 35 seconds, but
  with the gate scene before the jump it is nearer 55.
