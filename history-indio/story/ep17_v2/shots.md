One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (87 sentences). All stills NEW in the Bakunawa

**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 16 (v2).** The 1762 of this episode is one picture inside a lens: a convent
library stair with soldiers carrying books and rolled maps; it is NOT Episode 2's wall, kitchen, drain or river
bank, and no wall bursts. The hook is a phone screen in the rain, not a blow, a blade or a fall. The fall is a
slide down wet stone to the foot of great steps: not mud (Ep 2, Ep 12), not water (Ep 11), not a sink (Ep 5),
not being held (Ep 8). The rival is a face on a phone and a lit window: not a pickup and an arm (Ep 12), and
never in one room with Indio. The reading room must not look like Paris (Ep 5) or the school corridor (Ep 10):
one huge round room under a dome, seen from its door. The creature is taken by a line of light on its SHADOW:
not ridden (Ep 12), not wrestled under water (Ep 11, Ep 16). Rizal is seen only from behind, far off, writing:
not the Rizal of Bagumbayan (Ep 6) and not a face. This episode's world: rain and brick by a river at night;
fog, gas lamps and wet roofs; one warm round room of books; and the same gate in the rain at the end.

place are plain cuts or the flash of the jump. Rows 23, 31 and 37 are the high points; row 15 is the establishing `crane_down`.
