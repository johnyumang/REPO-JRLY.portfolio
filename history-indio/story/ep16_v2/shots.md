hair tied; no caricature. The woman in white is never closer than the far bank and her face is never clear.

old Tagalog bow with no arrows (its arrows are light, first seen in Episode 17). The KAALAMAN number takes its
value from Episode 15 (version 2) plus one step, set at the build. The medallions are on his neck (ruling B4); Ang Dagat glows sea-blue when it carries them. Indio ends the episode wet, with three knots.
