**Hero lines.** No significant real figure speaks. Magat Salamat is seen through the lens, from behind, and says
nothing; no word of his survives. The diwa in his medallion has ONE invented line under the owner's exception,
"Ang lihim, may tamang pagsasabihan. Piliin mo." (s86 to s87): it echoes the record (a plan told to the wrong
man) and adds no act and no guilt. It is not offered as a quotation, and the trivia says "hindi 'yon naitala".
Luzon Sukezaemon and Toyotomi Hideyoshi are not shown. No tender beat: no wife or partner is in the scene.

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- Japanese people are people: one kind, hard-working boy; no accent joke, no costume joke. The kappa keeps its
  folklore traits (courtesy, the dish, the cucumber, wrestling, a pledge kept) and is beaten by nobody: Indio
  gives its water back.
- The fall is not Episode 11's: no gold, no sunken boat, no deep water. He is thrown, by the belt, into a
  chest-deep stone canal in daylight.
- The comeback is the friend's lesson and an honest act (the bow, the water given back, his only food). It is
  not "he confessed and the dead thing relit": the confession to M comes after, as the wage he owes, as the
  bible row says.
- No real person of 1588 or 1594 has words; no guilt is put on Magat Salamat beyond the record. No living
  person, firm or brand. No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia at the end. No follow card.
- Usman is only inside the jump (five turns). The rival is not named or seen. Nobody knows who pays the fox.
- Storyteller: 214 of 540 words (40 percent); never more than three sentences in a row.
