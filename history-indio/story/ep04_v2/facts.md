# Facts — Episode 4 (version 2): Ang Aso ni Pepe (Calamba, Hunyo 1869)

Built on `videos/ep04-ang-aso-ni-pepe/facts.md` (checked 2026-11-12; search summaries; Rizal's own memoir was
NOT read in full, so the script never says "isinulat niya"). New in version 2 (web search, 15 Nov 2026,
summaries only): the origin of buko pie; an English rendering of the mother's warning in the moth story.

## 1. What is real in this script, and where it is said

| Line (sentences.json) | Claim | Status and sources |
|---|---|---|
| s10 to s12 (INDIO) | "Buko pie: sabi nila, sa Los Baños ito nagsimula ... gustong gumawa ng apple pie, pero walang mansanas. Kaya buko." | COMMONLY TOLD: the Pahud sisters of Los Baños, Laguna; one had learned apple pie abroad; apples were scarce, so young coconut was used; their bakery opened in 1965 (later Orient Buko Pie Bakeshop). Wikipedia, "Buko pie"; Matador Network, "Buko pie, Philippines". The sources DISAGREE on which sister and how she learned it, and a Laguna source says several bakeries are credited, so the line says "sabi nila", names nobody and no shop, and gives no year |
| s17 to s18 | June 1869, a Sunday afternoon | Confirmed in secondary sources (joserizal.com; Zaide-based summaries). The exact day is not known and is not said |
| s29 to s32 (ANDOY) | The dog belongs to the youngest boy of the house, "Pepe", José Rizal | "Pepe" as his nickname: standard. That the boy Rizal had a big black dog named Usman: SECONDARY SOURCES ONLY (Zaide-based biographies; Wikipedia); the name could not be traced to his own memoir, and one source says the dog statue at the Calamba shrine is labelled otherwise. The trivia says "ayon sa mga talambuhay". Rizal was the seventh of eleven children and the younger of two sons, so "bunsong lalaki" (youngest boy) is correct; "bunso" alone would not be |
| s33 to s34 (INDIO) | "'Yung nasa piso?" | Rizal is on the Philippine one-peso coin (BSP coin series). Common knowledge; a joke, not a claim of the episode |
| s66 to s67 (TEODORA) | "Huwag mong tularan ang batang gamu-gamo, at huwag kang susuway. Masusunog ka ring gaya niya." | ATTESTED in Rizal's memoir (Memorias de un estudiante de Manila) as his mother's words when she read him the fable of the moth from "El Amigo de los Niños". English rendering used: "Don't imitate the young moth and don't be disobedient; you'll get burned like it" (Rizal Weekly, "The Story of the Moth, Part 1: The Text"; study copies of the memoir render it "See that you do not behave like the young moth"). The Tagalog here is OUR translation of that English; the Spanish original was not opened. INVENTED PLACEMENT: in the memoir she says it at home on a night of reading lessons, not at his departure; here it is her reminder at the door |
| s70 to s72 (ANDOY) | He is going to Biñan to study; his older brother takes him | Confirmed in secondary sources: Paciano took him to Biñan in June 1869. The vehicle (a carromata) is found only in Zaide-based notes: kept as scenery, never stated as fact in the trivia |
| s73 to s75 | "Mga walong taon lang. Umiiyak." | He was born on 19 June 1861, so he turned eight that month. That he was sad at leaving home is from his own memoir as quoted by secondary sources; the trembling chin is our picture of it |
| Trivia | The dog "ayon sa mga talambuhay"; June 1869, a Sunday, with Paciano, to Biñan; the moth story from his own recollection; no known descendants | As above; "Rizal has no known descendants": first facts.md, header |

Dropped from the story: the pony Alipato; "Sa Aking Mga Kabata" and its dispute (true and checked; not needed here).

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- One real person speaks one attested line: Teodora Alonso (died 1911). The boy Rizal and Paciano are silent.
  All are generic drawn figures, no likeness. The child is shown only in ordinary, dignified moments.
- No tender beat of a wife or partner applies (the hero is a child); the mother's hands on his face stand in
  its place.
- No living person; the "named once, kindly" rule is not used. No bakery, brand or sign is shown.
- The real outcome stands: the boy leaves for Biñan. No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia at the end.
- Usman is only inside the jump.
