look; writing in pictures is added in code. Nobody just standing; never the same framing twice in a row.

blade, tied wrists, a PACK of dogs lying at his feet, torn papers. Ep 2: a rampart, a phone-screen pile-up, a
stone kitchen, a wall bursting, rats, a drain, a river bank. Ep 3: a jeepney interior, a sinigang table, a
crowd of hats, a window with a flag, a big drum, open hands pushing him out. Here there is ONE dog at ONE
gate, and the dog does not lie down: it sits upright beside him. This episode's world is green and quiet:
mango trees, a stone house, iron bars, a road.
