The medallion knotted in its mane replaces the spine. Trivia: "kuwentong-bayan" |
| Trivia | Found 1987; the date equal to the year 900; the oldest dated document found in the Philippines; Old Malay in Kawi script; Namwaran's children; the chief of Tundun for Jayadewa of Pailah; where Pailah was is disputed | Rows 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12 |

**Hero lines.** Jayadewa is named in the plate and nothing he said survives: the diwa in his medallion has one
invented line under the owner's exception, "Ang nakasulat ay nananatili." (s59). The chief of Tundun speaks a
rendering of the document he issued (s34). Neither is offered as a quotation. No tender beat: nothing is
recorded of a wife. Lady Angkatan and Bukah are the kneeling woman and her brother: seen, silent, unnamed.

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- Two voices of the year 900: the chief (a rendering of his own document) and the diwa (the exception). No
  likeness. The kneeling family is shown with dignity.
- Nothing is taken from a museum or a find site: the medallion comes from the creature's mane, in the year 900.
- No living person, firm or brand. "Sabi sa internet" names no site. No sentence says anything is invented.
- Trivia at the end. No follow card. Usman is only inside the jump.
