## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- One voice of 1570: Sulayman, a rendering tied to the record; no guilt and no false belief is put on him. The
  Spaniards and their Visayan allies are people in the distance, not monsters, and say nothing. The real
  outcome stands: the fort burns. What Indio wins is small: one family reaches a boat.
- No living person, firm, brand or app. "Sabi ng titser ko" names nobody. No sentence says anything is invented.
- Drink and the other woman by suggestion only; nothing is glamorised; the old version's "maliit na supot" is
  gone.
- "He says the true thing and the dead thing relights" is used here as BIBLE 14.7 allows (Episodes 14, 19, 37,
  48). "It was never the bronze" is NOT said: Indio credits the lamp ("sa ilawan"), and Usman only teases.
- Trivia at the end. No follow card. Usman is never in the present day.
