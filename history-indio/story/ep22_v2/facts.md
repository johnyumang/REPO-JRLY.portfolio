## 1. What is from the text, and where it is said

The nine verses, as copied (Ang Dating Biblia, 1905):

> 1 At ang buong lupa ay iisa ang wika at iisa ang salita.
> 2 At nangyari, na, sa kanilang paglalakbay sa silanganan, ay nakasumpong sila ng isang kapatagan sa lupain ng Sinar; at sila'y nanahan doon.
> 3 At nagsangusapang, Halikayo! tayo'y gumawa ng mga laryo, at ating lutuing mabuti. At inari nilang bato ang laryo at ang betun ay inaring argamasa.
> 4 At nagsipagsabi, Halikayo! Magtayo tayo ng isang bayan natin at ng isang moog, na ang taluktok niyaon ay aabot hanggang sa langit, at magtaglay tayo ng isang ngalan; baka tayo'y mangalat sa ibabaw ng buong lupa.
> 5 At bumaba ang Panginoon upang tingnan ang bayan at ang moog, na itinayo ng mga anak ng mga tao.
> 6 At sinabi ng Panginoon, Narito, sila'y iisang bayan at silang lahat ay may isang wika; at ito ang kanilang pinasimulang gawin: at, ngayon nga'y walang makasasawata sa anomang kanilang balaking gawin.
> 7 Halikayo! tayo'y bumaba at diyan din ay ating guluhin ang kanilang wika, na anopa't sila'y huwag magkatalastasan sa kanilang salita.
> 8 Ganito pinanabog sila ng Panginoon mula riyan, sa ibabaw ng buong lupa; at kanilang iniwan ang pagtatayo ng bayan.
> 9 Kaya ang pangalang itinawag ay Babel; sapagka't doon ginulo ng Panginoon ang wika ng buong lupa: at mula roon ay pinanabog sila ng Panginoon sa ibabaw ng buong lupa.

## 4. Guard rails checked for this script

- **God is given no voice, no words and no picture.** Nothing appears in the sky (s47 says so); no light, hand,
  figure, thunder or voice. Verses 5 to 7 are not spoken.
- **Nothing is added to what Genesis 11:1-9 says happened.** One language; a tower being built on a plain in
  Sinar; the language confused; the people scattered; the building left. Everything else is in section 3.
- **Two verses read word for word** by the storyteller, each with book, chapter and verse, from one named
  translation that was opened and copied. Nothing else is given as a quotation; the trivia's summary says
  "sa buod".
- **No joke inside the reading.** From s47 to s72 nobody jokes. The jokes are in the present (s9 to s12), in
  the bread and the game of the knock before the reading (s25 to s37), and with the dog after it (s73 to s84).
  The home line "Buti na lang, hindi ko naintindihan" was cut after the cold read. None is about
  the text, the tower's builders or the Panginoon.
- **They watch from the edge and change nothing written.** What Indio does (two knocks for one invented child)
  touches nothing the text tells. M's rule is said aloud (s17 to s19).
- No reading of the passage is offered: nobody says why it happened, what it means or who was at fault. Nuri
  is a child at work, not a sinner. The people of the plain are people.
- No living person, church, firm or brand. No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia at the end, quiet.
  No follow card. Usman is only inside the lagusan. "He confessed, so it worked" is not used.
- Because of the subject, the owner should read this script himself before it is voiced (as with Episode 9).
