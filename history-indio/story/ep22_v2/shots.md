# Shots — Episode 22 (version 2)

One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (97 sentences). All stills NEW in the Bakunawa
look; writing in pictures is added in code (the two references appear as plain text at the bottom of the
frame while the storyteller reads). Nobody just standing; never the same framing twice in a row.

**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 21 (v2).** The hook is a mouth he cannot read across a leaning stack of
bricks: not a blade (Ep 1), not a bursting wall (Ep 2), not a hand that stops him (Ep 8, Ep 21), not water
(Ep 11, Ep 20), not a tackle (Ep 18). The reading is NOT Galilee (Ep 9): no green hillside, no seated crowd, no
figure in white; this is a flat, hot, dust-coloured plain with the people as small as grains. The home scene
is the outside steps in daylight, not the Lola's kitchen at night (Ep 21) and not the tree at Kawit (Ep 9).
The falling bricks are a carrier's stack at a kiln, never a city wall (Ep 2). The tall cloaked workers are
not the hooded watcher of Ep 9 (he was alone, above, at night) and not the coat in the fog of Ep 21: they
are many, in daylight, working, backs to us. **For this episode: the sky is always empty and is never the
subject of a shot; no beam, cloud-face, hand, figure, lightning or halo; the tower is never closer than far
background, is never struck and never falls; no shot is taken from on or inside the tower.** This episode's
world: pale morning on wooden steps, a white-hot plain the colour of fired clay, grey-red dust, then the same
steps in afternoon light.

Camera (rule 10): column follows pipeline/CAMERA.md; rows marked HIGH POINT are the only big moves, with the
one establishing crane of row 11. No cross-dissolve between two stills that show people (CAMERA 6a): the two
changes of place are flashes.
