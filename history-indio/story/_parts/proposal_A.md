# INDIO: ANG SAKSI — Series Bible v2, Proposal A
## Lens: the short-drama engine

Working rule of this proposal: **every episode is one humiliation, one fall, one comeback, one show-off, one
door left open.** The first three seconds say who looked down on him today. The last three seconds take away
something he has just won. Nothing in between is explained by Indio; it is played.

Season cut used here (owner's brief): S1 = Ep 1-8, S2 = Ep 9-20, S3 = Ep 21-31, S4 = Ep 32-41, S5 = Ep 42-50.

## 1. The one question

**"Kailan makikita ng lahat na ang lalaking tinawag nilang 'tanso lang' ang nag-iisang totoo — at ano ang
kapalit kapag nakita na nila?"** (When will everyone see that the man they called "only bronze" is the one
true thing — and what does it cost him when they do?)

| Season | Its question |
|---|---|
| S1 (1-8) | Bakit siya, ang walang-wala, ang pinili — at sino ang isa pang lalaking may marka? |
| S2 (9-20) | Kapag nasa kanya na ang lahat, may matitira pa bang Indio? |
| S3 (21-31) | Kung patay ang lahat ng medalyon, ano ang sa kanya talaga? |
| S4 (32-41) | Kaya bang manalo ng saksi sa lugar na kuwento ang sandata? |
| S5 (42-50) | Masasabi ba niya ang "Nakita na kita" sa mismong pinagmulan ng kasinungalingan? |

## 2. Indio's own gifts: "Ang Tatlong Likas"

For twenty episodes he and the viewer believe the power is in the bronze. The villains say "ilusyon lang". The
truth, paid in Ep 22 and Ep 30: the bronze only ever warmed because of him.

| Gift | What it does | Rule | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ang Dinig** | He understands animals (Usman first, then any). | He must be still and silent. Animals know only what animals know: smell, fear, hunger, who passed. No dates, no names. | For a few breaths after, human speech is noise to him. (Comedy, and danger: he misses a warning.) |
| **Ang Tanaw** | In any scene he sees **ang hindi tugma**: the one thing that does not fit. | One look, one truth. It goes blind when he looks for himself (greed, drink, pride). | The next thing it shows is one truth about himself, and he cannot look away. |
| **Ang Udyok** | He moves a person to confess or to act. | Three steps, always: he confesses one true thing of his own aloud; he names what he saw ("Nakita na kita"); the person chooses. It never forces. On someone who wants to stay a liar it only leaves them with nothing to say. | Each use spends one of his own secrets. It is dead while a lie is in his mouth. |

**The steps**

| Step | Dinig | Tanaw | Udyok |
|---|---|---|---|
| As a child, unknowing (shown Ep 10) | talks to the stray at the school gate | sees the Kalantiaw page "hindi tugma" | a bully returns Bea's money after Indio says "nakita kita" |

## 3. The rival: Lakan Velmonte, "ang laging nauuna"

**Who.** The son of Aling Charing, Lola Ising's neighbour in Kawit. Same age as Indio, same Grade 5 class. First
honor, three diplomas, perfect teeth, perfect papers. Tubal Alcaín finds him early and makes him the face of
"heritage": a television historian who "recovers" relics. Everybody prefers him: the neighbours, the Senate,
the cameras, for a while even Lola's friends. He is not a monster. He believes his own press. That is why he
is a rival and not a villain.

**His "power".** Tubal is a smith (the name recalls Tubal-cain). He casts **mga Huwad**: bronze copies of the
datu medallions. A Huwad has no diwa. It runs on **being believed**: the views Rico Bulan gathers are its
fuel. So the villain's taunt is a confession: the real illusion is the rival's. (This also answers who of
"sila" can travel: the Huwad na Pintuan.)

**First seen, unnamed.**
- Ep 1: the back of a clean polo in the job line. "Kumpleto ang papel." He gets the job.
- Ep 2: "Top Rider: L.V., five stars" on the app; Indio gets one star.
- Ep 4: "Buti pa ang anak ni Aling Charing."
- Ep 8: at Kawit 1898, behind the crowd, **another man with a mark on his arm** (the S1 cliffhanger).
- **Ep 10: the face and the first name.** Boy Lakan takes the medal for reciting the false Code of Kalantiaw
  while boy Indio stands in the corridor for saying it is false.
- Ep 12: "ang Ginoo" on a drone feed. **Ep 17: full name, title and master**, live on Rico's stream in London.

**How he "beats" Indio (never the same way twice).**
Ep 1 the job. Ep 10 the medal. Ep 12 he reaches the copperplate first and nets the tikbalang. Ep 17 he "finds"
the 1762 crate on camera. Ep 20 he dives and lifts the twelfth medallion first. Ep 21 Samar welcomes his
convoy. Ep 31 he takes the credit for the rescue of his own mother, whom Indio carried. Ep 34 he tops the
Senate vote (number 1; Indio is number 12). Ep 35 he is given Indio's committee. Ep 39 he is the candidate.
Ep 43 his fleet answers the horn first.

**The reversal, in four beats.**
1. Ep 20: in his hand the Kalasag stays cold. "Tanso lang." It wakes on Indio's arm. Indio pulls him out of
   the siyokoy's grip. Lakan thanks nobody.
2. Ep 39: in the last debate Indio does not attack him. He confesses for himself. Lakan wins the room and
   loses the vote by a hair.
3. Ep 43-46: the Huwad crack when the believers stop believing. His "Dakilang Pader" falls on him. Indio spends
   **the last drop of the healing item on the rival**, in front of Aling Charing.
4. Ep 48: when Lucifer says nobody stands with a witness, the first voice is Lakan's: "Saksi ako. Sa kanya."
   The man everybody believed spends his credit on the man nobody believed.

## 6. The healing item: **Ang Hilom**

A plain white clam shell, thumb-sized, holding **three drops of dew of the first morning**.

- **Won from:** the kraken of the sinking city, **Ep 24**. The boss of that episode offers a golden cup; the cup
  is the trap; the cure is the dull shell caught in the kraken's net.
- **Rule:** one drop heals any wound of body, mind or poison.
- **Limits:** three drops in all. It never works on the one who holds it. It does not raise the dead. It does not
  break a bargain or a law (so it cannot simply empty Biringan).
- **The times it matters:**
  1. **Ep 27**: he tries it on his mother in Biringan. Nothing. The drop is not spent, and he learns the limit.
  2. **Ep 29**: drop one, for Papa's memory in Limot, while Indio's own marks bleed.
  3. **Ep 37**: drop two, for Usman, poisoned at Lola's gate the night before the hearing.
  4. **Ep 46**: drop three, for Lakan under his own wall.
  5. **Ep 48**: the shell is empty when Indio and M are hurt. The viewer has known this for two episodes.
  6. Ep 50: dew on the grass of both gardens. Nobody remarks on it.

## 8. The RPG frame

One tattoo opens one hologram. It is shown **once per episode, at the comeback, for two seconds**, and nobody
reads it aloud.

- **Party:** Indio (Saksi), Usman (Gabay), M (Bantay). Barkada with jobs: Bea (Tagasulat), JM (Mekaniko), Ma'am
  Celia (Guro), Totoy (Kapitan, from Ep 40). Three Kakampi slots.
- **Quest:** said once, as a joke, by Usman: "Misyon, par: huwag kang malunod." Never a list.
- **Level = Marka.** Seven in all. **A mark is not a count of jumps. It burns in only when he witnesses
  something and it costs him.** Ep 1-5: one each (job, pay, pride, friend, his last ₱20). Sixth in Ep 8, for
  the morning at Bagumbayan he did not shut his eyes on, paid when he chooses to stay. No mark in Ep 9-19: he
  was rich and nothing cost him. Seventh at Mactan when he gives up the lead to save Lakan. Seven marks, seven
  depths.
- **Skills = the three Likas**, three pips each. A pip fills after a loss, never after a win. The viewer learns
  to want him to lose.
- **Gear** = the twelve medallions and the gamit, shown as lit or cold. "Cold" is the fall made visible.
- **KAALAMAN %** ends in "?". It rises when he finds out something there, with a friend of that time. At 100
  the "?" straightens into a staff and the flood comes: the last level is a bill.
- **The viewer is in the party.** From Ep 19 a small counter, "ka-Indio", sits in the corner. In Ep 43 it turns
  out to be us. It is the true mirror of Rico's follower count.

## 10. Fixes for the ten worst contradictions (CONSISTENCY section 3)

1. **The marks (items 1-4).** A mark is a level, earned only by witness that costs. Five in Ep 1-5, the sixth
   in Ep 8, none while he is rich, the seventh at Mactan, seven to the end. Ep 8 says "lima". Lola's Ep 21 line
   drops the number.
2. **Where the family medallion comes from (5).** One answer: the loop. It is hung on the child in 1898, comes
   down to Lolo Isidro, and Lolo leaves it in the tin of Ep 10. M's line becomes: Lolo Pepe traced **the other
   eleven** on paper.
3. **The star (6).** It never leaves M's umbrella handle. He wishes with his hand on the handle (Ep 11 by
   accident). Ep 41 no longer names the place; Ep 46 reveals only that the handle is a hilt.
4. **The map, the plate, the medallion (8).** Three words: **ang mapa** (later ang susi), **ang tanso** (the
   copperplate), **Ang Kasulatan** (the medallion only). Items 9-11: the medallions go cold in Ep 14 and
   re-warm one by one on screen in Ep 15 with the pandesal; he reads Kawi only after Ang Kasulatan; Lihim and
   Tinig are never used in one breath.
5. **The money (12-13).** One number, "YAMAN", falls on the hologram from Ep 11. The fifty are bought in
   Ep 11-14, locked in Ep 15, never "given away" in Ep 21, impounded in Ep 35, returned in Ep 37, given in
   Ep 38 with the last of the wish. From Ep 15 he pays his way by work.
6. **Usman (14-16).** One rule: he exists only where the past is open (a jump, or a place outside time:
   Biringan, Babel, the depths). In the present, until Ep 30, his words reach Indio only through the nearest
   stray: "Sabi raw ni Usman..." (a running gag that also trains the Dinig). From the blade of Ep 30 he stays.
7. **"No medallion needed" (17) and "I changed nothing" (18).** The door was always the reading aloud; the
   bronze was the handle. After Ep 30 he needs no medallion, still needs a marker, and reaches the future only
   by the Orasan. He can never alter what he witnessed; he acts in the present with what he saw (Ep 38: the
   sabotage becomes evidence). The third wish does not rewrite our past: it opens a second garden.
8. **The villain names (30-31).** One sign, three triangles, from the pawnshop of Ep 1. "Tubal" is first said
   in the story (Ep 17), Tobías Alcaín in Ep 20, Dalisdis proven his in Ep 37. The black-coat watcher, the man
   with the nail, Kalawang, Rufino Paredes and Totoy's father are one man, joined by one prop: the rusty nail.
9. **The last enemy (36) and Sinag.** Lucifer is seen in Ep 30 and named by M in Ep 48, inside the story. He is
   not Sinag; she ends in Ep 46. Each prince falls in Ep 44-45. Tubal's choice is shown. Item 35: the flood
   comes at 100% because the enemy moves when the witness is complete; Indio did not make it.
10. **The family (21-26).** Lolo Isidro, Lola Ising and Ma'am Celia are named at first sight. Indio says
    "Papa"; adults say "Teddy"; "Teodoro" is only on paper. Biringan's three conditions are each met on screen
    in Ep 30 (the blade cuts the false "kusa silang kumain"; Albo comes to the feather; the Daan Pauwi carries
    only one who has a table waiting). The mother's line becomes "Sabi ko sa'yo, uuwi tayo."
