## Episode 25 — Ang Orasan sa Ilalim ng Mundo (ep25-ang-orasan-sa-ilalim-ng-mundo)

  - Count errors: "Ang ikatlong guhit" (s3) is the fourth drawing (tower, mountain, rings, door). "Pangatlong beses kong sinubukan ang Biringan" (s56) is the fourth try (Ep 21 s31, Ep 23 s68, Ep 24 s59).
