# Three independent judgments of BIBLE.md v2

Judge: independent reader, 14 November 2026. Read: `_parts/OWNER_BRIEF.md`, `BIBLE.md` (all), `CONSISTENCY.md`,
`season3/research.md`. Nothing else was read; nothing else was edited. "Row N" = the row of Episode N in
section 10. "S" = section of the bible. Every finding ends with a one-line fix ("Fix:").

Not checked (no web access used): whether invented names match living people or real firms; the mango in
Acapulco; Ortiz 1731; the Yúmud's source. These are marked "to check".

Overall, in four lines:
- The design (gifts, rival, web, Tabò, levels) answers the brief well on paper. The 50 rows do not yet obey it.
- The largest gap for the owner: Season 1 has no show-off at all, and one comeback (confess a truth, the dead
  thing relights) is used about nine times under different KIND labels.
- The largest gap for a viewer: Episodes 12, 20, 24, 30, 37, 45 and 46 carry five to twelve beats each.
- The largest gap for the guard rails: the villain society reads as Freemasons or a hereditary cabal; the Agartha
  clock makes Agartha true; the story confirms a Rizal bloodline; several scripture uses are loose.
