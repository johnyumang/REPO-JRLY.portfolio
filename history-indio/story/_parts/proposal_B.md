# PROPOSAL B — THE MYSTERY WEB
Series bible v2 for "INDIO: ANG SAKSI". Lens: one question, a plant-and-payoff calendar, hidden people, and the 21 dropped threads closed with no new loose ones.

Working rule of this proposal: **nothing is revealed that was not already on screen.** Every reveal below names the episode where the viewer could have seen it.

---

## 1. The one question

**"Ano ang nakita ng aso — at bakit si Indio lang ang nakakarinig sa kanya?"**
(What did the dog see, and why is a jobless nobody the only one who can hear him?)

It is asked in Ep 1 (a dog that knows his name), sharpened in Ep 4 (he was Rizal's dog), Ep 30 (he was there before the depths had names), and answered in Ep 48: Usman was in the field with Abel. He is the only witness of the first lie, he has walked through every age looking for someone who can hear an animal, and every "u-MU-ngol" in fifty episodes was that witness recognising a lie. "Sila" exist to keep that first lie standing. A witness who can hear the dog can end it.

Season questions:
- **S1 (Ep 1-8):** Sino ang babaeng may pulang payong, at bakit niya ako inililigaw?
- **S2 (Ep 9-20):** Sino ang laging nauuna sa amin, at paano niya nalalaman kung saan kami pupunta?
- **S3 (Ep 21-31):** Sino ang nagtago sa pamilya ko, at ano ang nakita ni Tatay sa ilalim ng Banahaw?
- **S4 (Ep 32-41):** Sino sa sarili kong mesa ang pumirma para sa akin?
- **S5 (Ep 42-50):** Kanino ang gunaw, at para kanino ang huling hiling?

## 2. Indio's own gifts (not the medallions)

The lie the series tells him for 25 episodes: "Sa medalyon 'yan, hindi sa'yo." The gear is a training wheel for a gift he already has.

| Gift | What it does | Rule | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ang Dinig** | He understands animals and creatures (Usman first). | Works only while he is silent. Animals never lie, but they know only what they smelled, heard, saw. | For those breaths he cannot hear people, and he feels what the animal feels (hunger, fear, a wound). |
| **Ang Tanaw** | He sees the one thing in a place that does not belong, the thing everyone's eyes slide off. | Works only when he looks where people look away: the low, the dirty, the back row. | He cannot unsee it. Each true sight stays, and when it costs him enough it becomes a **marka**. |
| **Ang Tanong** | One plain question, and the person cannot answer it with a lie: he confesses or he acts. | One question per person, ever. He must first say aloud a true thing against himself of the same weight. | Everyone present hears his confession. Mastered form: "Nakita na kita." |

How the gear maps: the lente is Tanaw with a crutch; the ilawan and Ang Tinig are the first half of Ang Tanong (truth first); Ang Sugo is Dinig for human tongues.

**The steps**

|---|---|---|---|

**Marks = levels.** A marka is not earned by a jump. It is earned when he keeps his eyes open on a truth that costs him. This fixes the count: Ep 1-5 give 1-5; Ep 6 gives none because he blinks at the volley (M: "Huwag kang pipikit"), which is why he is stranded; Ep 8 gives the 6th (he sees himself); Ep 9-19 give none because he is looking at his money; Ep 20 gives the 7th. Seven marks, seven depths, seven heads of the dragon: in Ep 48 each mark answers one prince.

## 3. The rival: Rico Bulan, "Ang Tunay na Saksi"

- **Who:** Federico "Rico" Bulan, history vlogger, first honor, diploma, ring light, four million followers. Everybody's line: "Si Rico nga may diploma. Ikaw, ano'ng natapos mo?"
- **First seen, unnamed:** Ep 1, a voice and a jawline on the pawnshop TV ("Kasaysayan, kasama ako"). Ep 10, the unnamed first-honor boy who recites the false code perfectly and gets the medal while the child Indio is made to stand in the corridor. Ep 12, a hand with a ring light in a pickup window. **Named in Ep 17**; Ep 19 Ma'am Celia, on the phone: "Si Federico? Kaklase mo 'yon. Siya ang may medalya." The Ep 10 scene changes meaning.
- **Why he is not near Indio:** Rico travels on **Ang Huwad**, a thirteenth "medallion" forged by Tubal from ring-bronze. It carries its holder but shows him only what he already wants to see. He has never once seen the past. The illusion is his.
- **How he "beats" Indio:** Ep 10 the medal; Ep 12 there first, leaves with the chest; Ep 17 films Indio at the crate, "magnanakaw ng relikya", millions of views; Ep 20 arrives with vans and a permit; Ep 33 hired as "consultant" of the wall; Ep 35 the forged deed; Ep 39 the campaign; Ep 41 "siya ang gumawa ng baha".
- **The reversal:** Ep 40 Indio leaves Rico's name on the ark list. Ep 44 Ang Huwad cracks on live video and Rico sees the true 1570 through Sulayman's window, the first real sight of his life; he turns the camera on himself and says nothing. Ep 49 he confesses to a child, unasked. The false witness ends as the second witness.

## 6. The healing item: **Ang Sibol**

- **What:** spring water sealed in a small shell, from under the drowned city. Seven drops; a counter of seven dots on the hologram.
- **Won from:** the Kraken, boss of Ep 24. It is what the net was guarding.
- **Rule:** one drop closes any wound of the body, on any being. **Never for himself** (his marks bleed and he must let them).
- **Limit:** it does not heal memory, a lie, a broken trust, or the dead. It is silent below the fourth depth.
- **When it matters:** Ep 25 (the yeti's foot: an enemy healed becomes an ally); Ep 29 (it **fails** on Tatay's forgetting; what works is his name); Ep 38 (JM, crushed under the garage door he opened: Indio spends a drop on the man who sold him); Ep 45 (Kalawang); Ep 48 (Usman, struck by the dragon's tail); Ep 49 (the last drop poured on the mud of Kawit: the first green thing). Ep 26: M distrusts it, "Huwag mong tawaging gamot ang isa pang tasa."

## 8. The RPG frame

Never a menu, never a list read aloud. Per episode, at most two lines and one picture:
- **Party (three slots):** the storyteller names who walks in: "Tatlo sila: si Indio, si M, at isang asong ayaw maglakad." When a slot is empty the picture shows the empty sidecar seat.
- **Quest:** Lolo's kuwaderno writes one line at the end of an episode; that line is the next episode's errand. No "next episode" talk.
- **Skills:** a gift or medallion is named once, at the moment it is used, as a verb: "Tumanaw si Indio."
- **Levels:** the marks. The hologram flashes for one breath; Usman reads it as a joke: "Level seven ka na, par. Sa utang, level ninety." KAALAMAN % is shown, never explained, until Ep 41.
- **Boss / drop:** every won creature leaves one thing (a hair, a shell, a brick). The thing is used later (section 5).
- Trivia after the last line, as its own card; the fiction label only in caption and pinned comment; no follow card; "u-MU-ngol".

Hard rules that end the contradictions: the star never leaves M's umbrella handle until Ep 46; "ang Mapa" is the map, "ang tansong kasulatan" the plate, "Ang Kasulatan" the medallion; Usman has a body only where time is open (jumps, portals, Biringan, the depths) and is a voice from the tattoo in the present until Ep 30; after Ep 30 Indio needs no medallion to cross but always needs a door (a marker read aloud), and only the Orasan goes forward.

## 11. Fixes for the ten worst contradictions (CONSISTENCY section 3)

1. **The marks (1-4).** A mark is a truth watched without blinking that costs him. Counts become 1-5, none, 6, none through S2, 7 at Mactan; Lola's "lima" is said in Ep 3-5 time only.
2. **Origin of the family medallion (5).** One origin on screen: the loop of Ep 6 and 8. "Nahanap ni Lolo Pepe" stays as family talk nobody saw. It has no first maker, which is why Tubal can only forge Ang Huwad.
3. **The star (6).** It stays in M's umbrella handle from Ep 8 to Ep 46. Indio speaks the wishes; he never carries it. Ep 41 does not mention where it is.
4. **"Kasulatan" (8) and the twelve points (7).** Three fixed words (section 8). The map has twelve datu points plus unnumbered "aral" marks that light only when reached.
5. **Cold medallions and early powers (9, 10, 11).** In Ep 15 the Daan Pauwi is found in Dezhou before any crossing, and he reaches Dezhou by ordinary plane ticket bought with the last cash; in Ep 12 he cannot read the plate until Ang Kasulatan is in his hand; Lihim and Tinig are never used in the same breath.
6. **The money and the fifty (12, 13).** The hidden clause of Ep 12 takes back everything in Ep 15 except the fifty, which sit in JM's name. So Ep 18 has no bank, Ep 21 gives away nothing he does not own, Ep 35 can be framed, Ep 38 can give.
7. **Usman (14-16).** Body where time is open, voice from the tattoo in the present, body everywhere from Ep 30. One sentence in Ep 8 states it.
8. **"No medallion needed" and "witness only" (17, 18).** After Ep 30 he needs no bronze, always a door (a marker), and the Orasan to go forward. The past never changes; "hindi na sapat ang saksi lang" means he must act in the present. The third wish opens another world; this one keeps its history.
9. **The villain's names (30, 31, 36).** Every name is spoken by a character before it is used (Tubal 16, 17; Tobías Alcaín 20; Kalawang 33; Rufino Paredes 36, 40; Lucifer 48). Dalisdis is tied to Alcaín on one register page. The seven princes, the dragon and Tubal each get an on-screen end (48).
10. **The family (21-26) and the flood charge (35).** One name each from first sight: Tatay Teddy, Lolo Isidro, Lola Ising, Ma'am Celia. Biringan's three conditions are met one by one in Ep 30. The mother first sees him in Ep 27, so Ep 30's line becomes "Umuwi na tayo, anak." The enemy does not grow with his knowledge: the enemy hurries because the witness is nearly ready.

Guard rails kept: no real person is given a new act or line (the Paris figure is read from paper; Rizal, Sakay, Quezon, Salonga are seen from far); the multiplied thing in Acapulco is a mango, not bread; the paper wall is always in invented San Lorenzo; no year or reference to a living scandal; Agartha is told with its inventor and date, and its people are "mga taong kuweba"; God is given no words; scripture is exact with reference or marked buod; M pays her own price and heaven's approval is not shown.
