# INDIO: ANG SAKSI — Series Bible v2, Proposal C (the role-playing game)

Design rule of this proposal: the medallions are **equipment**; the three gifts are **Indio himself**. Equipment can be taken, forged, bought or go cold. The gifts cannot, and the whole series is the slow proof of that to a man who believes he is nothing.

## 1. The one question

**Can a tambay whose only talent is that he does not look away beat the people who built the world on a lie, without ever striking one of them down?**

- Season 1 (Ep 1-8, the Philippines): *Bakit ako?* Why does the door open for the one man nobody would hire?
- Season 2 (Ep 9-20, Asia and the oceans): *Kanino ko ba kinukuha ang labindalawa?* Who is he really collecting the twelve for?
- Season 3 (Ep 21-31, the hidden places): *Kung patay ang lahat ng gamit, may matitira ba sa akin?*
- Season 4 (Ep 32-41, the office and the flood): *Kaya bang manalo ng totoo sa kuwento?* Can a witness win against a story?
- Season 5 (Ep 42-50, Digmaan sa Langit): *Ano ang gagawin ng saksi sa kaaway na nakaluhod na?*

## 2. Indio's own gifts (not the medallions)

Three gifts, one chain: he **hears**, he **sees**, he **says**. Each is named in the story by somebody else, never by Indio; Usman usually names them to tease him.

### Ang Ulinig (the faint hearing): he understands animals
- **Does:** he hears what an animal means. Animals never lie, but they only know what they smell, hear and fear.
- **Rule:** *Tumahimik ka muna.* It works only while Indio himself is quiet inside: not drunk, not boasting, not lying. One animal at a time at first.
- **Cost:** *Walang libre sa hayop.* The animal is paid first, from his own share (his baon, his water, his jacket). He goes hungry a lot. This is the series' running joke.
- **Steps to mastery:**
  - Child: Ep 10 (seen in the lente) he argues with the strays at the school gate; classmates chant "kausap ang aso".
  - Unknowing: Ep 1. He thinks the medallion makes the dog talk.
  - Doubt: Ep 6. M takes the medallion and Usman still speaks. Indio decides it is the dog's power, not his. Ep 15: no medallion is warm, Usman is gone, and a garage cat tells him "may bata sa labas, gutom". He blames the hunger.
  - Told it is his and can be trained: Ep 28, by dead Lolo Isidro: "Hindi sa tanso 'yan, apo. Sa'yo. At napag-aaralan."
  - Training rungs: one animal (S1-S2) -> a flock or herd (Ep 31, the rats and dogs of Kawit show where the water will come) -> the beings (Ep 36, he hears the fox under her faces).
  - Mastery: Ep 42. The Bakunawa he could not hear in Ep 13, because he was loud and drunk, he hears in total silence. Ep 43: the tambuli works because every creature already knows his voice.

### Ang Malas (ang pagmamalas, the beholding): he sees what others pass by
- The pun carries the underdog story. From Ep 1 everyone says "Malas ka talaga, Indio" (unlucky). In Ep 29 his father says the old meaning: "Hindi ka malas, anak. Nagmamalas ka." Same word; the insult becomes the title.
- **Does:** one true detail stands out for him: the hand that shakes, the brick that is new, the person missing from the table (Ep 5: "Wala.").
- **Rule:** *Huwag kang pipikit.* (M's line at Bagumbayan, Ep 6, becomes the rule.) It shows only what is in front of him now; it never tells him what it means. Meaning he must find out from people.
- **Cost:** *Tatlong hingang dilim.* At full strength the world slows for three breaths while he looks, and he may touch nothing. Then he is blind for three breaths. The fall of an episode usually happens in that dark. (This replaces the unexplained "stops time" of Ep 25, 38, 44.)
- **Steps:**
  - Child: Ep 10, the boy who saw that the "Kodigo" poster was printed last year.
  - Unknowing: Ep 1-5. From Ep 7 he credits the **lente**; from Ep 9 the **ilawan**. They are training wheels.
  - Doubt: Ep 14, the kasulatan goes blank and the lens fogs; he sees the tall man's shaking hand anyway and tells himself "tsamba".
  - Told: Ep 29, Teddy: "Hindi mo kailangan ng ilaw, kailangan mo ng mata."
  - First deliberate use with no gear: Ep 30, the depth of Linlang.
  - Training: Season 4. He teaches it to others (Bea finds the wrong font in Ep 34), which is how a gift levels: *ang naituturo, lumalakas*.
  - Mastery: Ep 48. He looks at the man in white for three breaths and stays standing in the dark after.

### Ang Udyok (the nudge): he moves a person to confess or to act
- **Does:** a person in front of him loses his excuse. He confesses, or he finally does the thing he was afraid to do. Its spoken key is the series' own line: **"Nakita na kita."**
- **Rule:** *Ako muna.* Indio must first say aloud one true thing about himself that costs him. It never forces: the person still chooses. It fails completely on a lie, and on a promise he cannot keep.
- **Cost:** a confession is spent. The same truth never works twice, so every use needs a deeper one. He runs out of cheap truths in Season 2.
- **Steps:**
  - Child: Ep 10, he says "Ako po ang nagbura ng pisara" and the boy who copied the report starts to cry.
  - Unknowing: Ep 1 (the Katipunero boy admits he has no cedula either). He credits luck; Ep 14 the lamp; Ep 19 Ang Tinig.
  - Doubt: Ep 32. Kap Ramil confesses and Usman points out: "Par. Hindi mo suot ang Tinig."
  - Told: Ep 28-29.
  - Training rungs, visible as the size of the room: one man (Ep 32) -> a forger who lies for a living (Ep 36) -> a hearing room (Ep 37) -> a country (Ep 39, where it fails once because he overpromises).
  - Mastery: Ep 48, the father of lies kneels with nothing left to say; Ep 49, Rico Bulan confesses to a child with no one asking.

**The joined gift, Ang Saksi:** hear, see, say. He does all three in one breath only twice: Ep 37 ("Saksi ako.") and Ep 48.

## 3. The rival

**Rico Bulan** (Enrico Bulan), the history vlogger of the channel *KasaysaRICO*: handsome, degree on the wall, studio lighting, a million followers. The world's idea of what a "saksi" should look like.

- **First seen, unnamed:** Ep 1, a smiling face on the tarpaulin at the shrine ("Heritage Walk kasama si ...", the name hidden by a stapled flyer). The tour-guide job Indio was refused went to him. Ep 2: a hand with a ring light gets to the Baluarte "first". Ep 10: the Grade 5 boy who recites the Kodigo ni Kalantiaw and takes the Best in History medal while Batang Indio is sent out for saying it is false. We do not connect the boy and the tarpaulin yet.
- **Named:** Ep 17, London, live on camera: "Rico Bulan po, ang inyong tunay na saksi."
- **How he "beats" Indio:** he always posts first and gets the credit (Ep 12, 17, 33). He carries **Ang Huwad**, a forged door cast in Tubal's foundry from the wax print the pawnbroker took of the Bracken medallion in Ep 1. It opens the past badly: it leaves rust on everything, the traveller can stay only minutes, and it eats the hand that holds it. His "party" is **caught, not won**: beings in steel nets and collars, shown to cheering crowds. His slogan under every Indio video: **"Ilusyon lang."**
- **The reversal:** Ep 19, Indio's unedited confession outruns Rico's edited video for the first time. Ep 37, Rico is asked one plain question under oath and cannot name a single thing he saw himself. Ep 44, his collars break in the bay; his own captives turn on him and Indio shields him. Ep 48, Rico is the one who holds the healing dipper for Indio. Ep 49, he tells a child the truth. The false witness is the one thing in history Indio did change, and it answers Lucifer's "wala kang binago".

## 5. Allies won (Mga Kakampi)

Rico's beasts wear collars; Indio's wear nothing.

## 6. The healing item: Ang Tabò ng Bukal

A coconut-shell dipper, cracked and tied with rattan. In Ep 24 Sinag offers the golden cup; behind the throne Ang Pugita is chained over the city's first spring, and floating there is the dipper the first people drank from before there was gold. Indio's joke: "Tabò? 'Yung pampaligo?" The humblest object in the series heals everything.

- **Won from:** Sinag's Atlantis, guarded by Ang Pugita, Ep 24.
- **Rule:** one salok heals anything: wound, fever, poison, a cold medallion, a memory.
- **Limits:** (1) one salok, then it is dry until the next new moon; (2) **it never heals the one who holds it**, so somebody else must hold it for you; (3) it does not raise the dead; (4) it cannot heal what the sick person will not admit.
- **The times it matters:**
  1. Ep 25: Usman is fading in the snow or the yeti's old wound: one salok. Indio gives it to the yeti, and Usman holds on by himself. First lesson of the limit.
  2. Ep 29: his father in the depth of Limot does not know him. One salok and Teddy says "Indalecio?"
  3. Ep 31: dry. Lola swallows flood water and he has nothing; she lives by neighbours, not by magic.
  4. Ep 38: seven children with fever, or Vangie, pinned under her own gate. He gives it to the children; Vangie lives anyway and that is what turns her.
  5. Ep 48: Indio is the one wounded and cannot use it. Rico Bulan picks it up.
  6. Ep 49: he pours the last salok into the bay with the third wish; the water goes down.

## 8. The RPG frame, on screen, without a menu

- **Party.** Three fixed seats and one guest seat. Indio is the Saksi. **Usman** is the nose: he growls at a lie and names Indio's gifts to mock them. **M** is the seal and the rule-keeper: she never fights for him. The fourth seat is the **friend of that time**, who each teaches one small thing (a knot, a whistle, a way to carry water) that pays later; the table marks the best of these.
- **Quest.** One line near the top, said a different way each time and never by a narrator: an order slip, Lola's text, a line the notebook writes, a dare from Rico's video.
- **Skills.** Indio never announces a skill. Usman does, as a joke: "Ayan na naman 'yung Malas mo." Each use shows rule and cost in the picture: the food handed over, the three dark breaths, the confession said first.
- **Levels: ang marka ay peklat.** A mark is not earned by a jump or a win. It burns in the morning after a defeat that cost something he cannot get back. Seven marks: Ep 1 (the job), Ep 7 (the medallion and 529 days), Ep 8 (he may not go home), Ep 15 (the wealth), Ep 20 (the twelve were for Tubal), Ep 30 (he kneels), Ep 41 (the oath; the "?" becomes the staff). Small falls move only KAALAMAN %, which also drops when he lies (Ep 14: 35 to 18).
- **How a level is felt.** (a) The thing that beat him last time is done in one beat this time, and nobody remarks on it. (b) What strangers call him changes: tambay, indio, magnanakaw, kap, sugo, pastol. (c) Each season's boss asks the same question, **"Sino ka ba?"**, and the answer is the level: Ep 8 "Wala. Tambay lang." Ep 20 "Indio." Ep 30 "Saksi lang ako." Ep 37 "Saksi ako." Ep 50 "Ako 'to si Indio."
- **The hologram** appears at most once an episode, for three seconds, one line changed. The red cord with its knots does the rest.

## 10. Fixes for the ten worst contradictions

1. **The marks (3.1-3.4).** *Ang marka ay peklat*: seven marks, seven named losses (Ep 1, 7, 8, 15, 20, 30, 41). No mark for a jump. Lola's "lima" in Ep 21 is cut.
2. **Where the family medallion came from (3.5).** Only the loop of Ep 8. "Lolo Pepe found it" becomes family talk that Ep 8 corrects.
3. **The star (3.6).** It never leaves M's umbrella handle until Ep 46. A wish is made by speaking with a hand on the handle; in Ep 11 the umbrella is leaning on his sidecar. Ep 41's giveaway line is cut.
4. **Usman (3.14-3.16).** One rule: Usman is where there is no "ngayon" (the past, a lagusan, Biringan, the depths) until the blade in Ep 30; after it, always. The present-day lines of Ep 13 and 20 go. Why Indio hears him is the Ulinig, not the medallion.
5. **"No medallion needed" (3.17).** From Ep 30 the gifts need no gear; crossing time still needs Ang Pintuan. Medallions are equipment to the end.
6. **Witness or changer (3.18).** He never changes the past. In the present he acts, which is what "hindi na sapat ang saksi lang" means. The one thing he changes is a living man, Rico. The third wish opens another world; it undoes nothing here.
7. **The money (3.12-3.13).** The first wish grows while he spends on himself and freezes the moment he shares (Ep 15). Houses and yacht are sold in Ep 15-16; the fifty stay because nobody will buy a "thief's" cars; impounded Ep 35, returned Ep 37, given away Ep 38.
8. **The villain names (3.30-3.31).** One seal, the three triangles, joins pawnshop, tie, cigarette end, Rico's phone, Alcaín Builders and Dalisdis (the seal upside down, Ep 34). "Tubal" is said in the story in Ep 16. The nail-chewer = Kalawang = Rufino Paredes = Totoy's father, each step shown.
9. **Freeing the mother and Tala (3.25-3.26).** Three locks, three keys, on screen: the city's law (the blade cuts the lie "ang kumain ay amin", Ep 30), the limit of the Daan Pauwi (Teddy goes first and calls them), Albo's consent (given in Ep 30, not Ep 23). The mother's "ang laki mo na" is cut.
10. **The last enemy (3.36).** Seen from Ep 13, named by M in the first line of Ep 48, kneels in Ep 48. The dragon is driven off by M's host, off Indio's screen; the seven princes go silent one by one; Tubal is left holding contracts nobody will sign. Sinag is his servant, not himself.
