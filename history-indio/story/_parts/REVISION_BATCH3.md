# Revision order for Episodes 13 to 18 (after the curator's review and a stranger's cold read)

A stranger read the spoken lines of all six, once, in order. Every episode could be followed, but every one
"plays as a narrator reading a summary over pictures": the storyteller has 54 to 65 percent of the words, the
climax is narrated, and the friend and the dog barely speak. The owner's Episode 1 has the storyteller at 48
percent. Revise your episode, in place, to these orders. Keep 520 to 540 spoken words. Do not commit or push.

## A. Make it scenes with people talking (all six)
1. TAGAPAGSALAYSAY has **at most half** of the spoken words (derive.py prints the count per speaker).
2. The storyteller only sets the place, introduces who arrives (rule 11), and tells what is DONE, in short
   sentences. What people WANT, fear, refuse, teach and decide is SAID by them, in dialogue. The fall and the
   comeback are played in lines between Indio and whoever is in front of him, not reported.
3. The storyteller never speaks more than three sentences in a row without a character speaking.
4. The friend of that time has at least six spoken lines and teaches the trick in his or her own words. Usman,
   in every jump, has at least four lines (he warns, jokes and says what he smells). M speaks when she is there.
5. To make room: cut recap of earlier episodes to one sentence, cut any history the friend can say in a line,
   cut side threads that are not paid in this episode (leave them as picture notes), and never have the
   storyteller repeat a character.
6. After rewriting, re-run derive.py, renumber `facts.md` and `shots.md` to the new `sentences.json`, and check
   that no shot is hands only or an object with no person.

## B. Rulings so the six agree with each other and with Episodes 1 to 12
1. **How he jumps.** Since Episode 8 the Lolo's medallion is no longer his. When M is with him, it is "ang
   medalyon ni M" that warms at the marker and carries them. When M is gone (Episode 14 after she leaves), the
   one medallion still warm is "ang kay Rajah Kiling, ang una niyang napanalunan"; never "ang mana sa Lolo niya".
2. **The lens** is "ang lente ng Lolo niya, na nagpapakita ng totoong nangyari sa lugar na tinitingnan". Not
   "the hidden", not only "the past".
3. **The ilawan** (from Galilea, Episode 9) lights only for a true word and shows who is watching in secret.
   He carries it. Say what it is in one clause when it first matters.
4. **Where the medallions are.** On his neck through Episode 17. In Episode 18 the storyteller says: since the
   lens was snatched in London he keeps them in a bag.
5. **Ang Dagat** (won in 14, re-warmed in 15) carries its holder across the sea, in the present day. It is how
   they reach Sakai (16), London (17) and Acapulco (18); nobody pays a fare. He may still have no money for food.
6. **The red thread and the knots:** "ang pulang tali sa pulso niya; bawat buhol, isang nilalang na naging
   kakampi niya". Knots: 1 the Yúmud (11), 2 the tikbalang (12), 3 the kappa (16), 4 Lundag (17), 5 the nahual
   (18). Episodes 13, 14 and 15 make no knot. Say the number.
7. **The woman in white.** In 15 she is unmasked as a nine-tailed fox who was paid, by someone Indio does not
   know, to keep the medallion from him. In 16 she has followed them; Indio's secret from M is that in China he
   took her cup and nearly stayed. Episodes 17 and 18 do not use her.
8. **Totoy** goes to Dezhou with Indio and ends Episode 15 at Lola Ising's table. Episode 16 says in one clause
   that he stays with the Lola. **M** comes in at the Lola's door at the end of 15; Episode 16 opens by saying
   Indio let her in and they agreed: no more secrets.
9. **The cars:** 22 in Episode 13, 50 in 14; in 15 they and their debt are left with JM; from 16 on he has none
   and little money.
10. "Nakita na kita" are the three words that win a creature. Whenever an episode speaks of "the words", it says
    them.
11. No voice plays Rizal in Episode 17 (already removed).
