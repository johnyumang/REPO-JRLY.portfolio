# The owner's brief for the story of "INDIO: ANG SAKSI" (binding)

Series: 50 Tagalog/Taglish vertical animated episodes for Meta (Facebook/Instagram Reels), all ages.
Arc: Philippine history (Ep 1-8) -> the medallion hunt in the Philippines, Asia, Europe, America (Ep 9-20)
-> the hidden places and theories: Biringan, Babel, the Watchers, Atlantis, Agartha, the vision, Banahaw,
the seven depths (Ep 21-31) -> politics and the flood (Ep 32-41) -> the last war, "Digmaan sa Langit"
(his working word "Ragnarok") (Ep 42-50). All 50 episodes already exist as videos; pictures are being redrawn
now; narration will be re-voiced only where the rewrite needs it. Each episode keeps its REAL MOMENT (its
facts.md) and its place in the order.

## What he said last night about the stories
1. Too much is told by Indio alone and the story is hard to follow. He wants SCENES we watch in the third
   person: a short storyteller line, what people do in (parentheses), then they talk. No talking heads.
2. Indio does not know the history and does not lecture it: he finds it out there, with friends he makes in that
   time. He whispers to us today's sabi-sabi (what people say today) against what he sees. Jokes from Indio.
3. A reason drives every scene and pays off at its end. Every episode has its own plot and its own KIND of
   twist, and the twist runs through a fall: Indio seems to LOSE, then comes back, and then he may show off big.
4. The underestimated hero of today's Chinese short dramas, through the whole series: he is humble and always
   looked down on; there is always a RIVAL people think is better, who is not near him; a villain says his
   power is "ilusyon lang". He grows stronger after every defeat.
5. His own gifts even without the medallion: he talks with the dog, he SEES what others do not, he can make a
   person confess or move a person to act. He first thinks the gift is not his; as a child he used it
   unknowing; late he learns it can be mastered; at the end he masters it.
6. Allies are won: beings he calls up and captures, each after a hard fight. An item that heals everything is
   won from a boss.
7. A villain, neutral ones, traitors: with mystery. Significant characters are not named when first seen:
   hidden, built, revealed when the story needs it.
8. About 20% real, 80% invented, and it must keep people watching; never a history class. The feel of a
   role-playing game: party, quest, skills, levels.
9. NO "this part is invented" talk inside the story (he calls it filler). NO redundant scene or picture: he must
   never feel "napanood ko na 'to". The trivia goes at the END. No generic follow card.
10. Say "umungol" as u-MU-ngol.

## Guard rails (they protect him; never break them)
- The real part is true and sourced (facts.md, two sources). The real outcome of history stands at the end of
  an episode: Indio never changes what really happened.
- Invented words only for people long dead, and nothing that puts guilt or a false belief on them.
- Living people, real parties and real companies are never given words and never put in a scandal.
- God is given no words. Scripture is exact (with reference) or marked "buod".
- Agartha and similar: none of the racist imagery season3/research.md warns about (no "Aryan"/Nordic
  master-race imagery, no black suns, vril, swastika-like symbols); Agartha is told as a 19th-century European
  invention.
- The label of fiction lives in the caption and pinned comment only, never spoken.

## Canon already fixed by the owner (from SERIES.md; do not contradict, you may deepen)
- Indio = José Indalecio Bracken, jobless young man of Quezon City, raised by Lola Ising (and the Lolo, now dead).
  Family lore: descended from Rizal ("Lolo Pepe"). Mother and older half-sister (Ate Tala, child of a white-haired
  Watcher, "Albo, ang Puting Bantay") were taken to Biringan; the Papa went looking and never came back.
- Usman: talking black aspin, only Indio and animals understand him; exists only in jumps/the past until late;
  he was Rizal's dog; deeper, the dog of Abel the shepherd.
- M: the woman with the red umbrella, a second traveller; of Michael's host, guardian of the family, forbidden
  to love her charge; her medallion is Sikatuna's "Ang Sandugo" (the pact that seals a defeated creature as ally).
- Twelve datu medallions, each with a diwa, a power and a limit: Sulayman/Ang Pintuan (time door), Sikatuna/Ang
  Sandugo (pact), Kiling/Ang Sugo (understands every language), Jayadewa/Ang Kasulatan (reads any writing truly),
  Kolambu/Ang Kalakal (true worth, what a person truly wants), Matanda/Ang Dagat (crossing), Paduka Pahala/Ang
  Daan Pauwi (way home), Magat Salamat/Ang Lihim (unseen), Lakandula/Ang Agos (speed), Tupas/Ang Piging
  (multiplying), Kudarat/Ang Tinig (voice that wakes courage, only when true), Lapulapu/Ang Kalasag (shield).
  Joined: the anting (armour).
- Gamit: lente (shows what truly happened), ilawan (shows who watches in secret; burns only while its holder tells
  the truth), kuwaderno ni Lolo, the star "Ang Huling Tala" (three wishes: 1 wealth, 2 one night when all are safe,
  3 the first paradise), the kasulatan/map, the key (susi) to the portals, weapons (punyal, busog at palaso,
  kampilan), the Trinity (hilt = the umbrella handle, star, blade from the lowest depth): the blade that cuts
  only lies. Mounts: bicycle, motorcycle with sidecar, the fifty vehicles, tikbalang, balangay, sarimanok, Bakunawa.
- Hologram from one tattoo: marks (level), medallion slots, gear, Mga Kakampi, KAALAMAN % ending in "?" (the crook
  of a shepherd's staff; at 100% the gunaw begins).
- "Sila": the heirs of Cain, builders; leader with a name recalling Tubal-cain; "Ang Bulaang Saksi", a history
  vlogger who sells false history, is their voice; seven princes of the seven depths; a fallen one from M's host
  (chief tempter); Lucifer.
- Fall and comeback of Season 2: wealth, luxury, vice, everything lost inwardly, one piece of bread shared.
- Season 4: kapitan -> mayor -> senator -> framed over an invented flood wall (Dalisdis Diking Corporation,
  invented) -> "Saksi ako" at the hearing -> President -> the arks.
- Season 5: the gunaw (the dragon's flood, Rev 12:15), battles in Manila Bay, Mactan/Sulu, he rides the Bakunawa,
  the safe night, Lucifer faced and beaten by witness not by a blow, the third wish, two gardens, "Ako 'to si Indio."
- Indio never kills. He wins by finding what a thing truly is and saying "Nakita na kita."
