# INDIO: ANG SAKSI — consistency audit of the 50 episodes as they are

Audit date: 14 November 2026. Text only: nothing in `videos/` was changed. Every script.txt,
script_multispeaker.txt, sentences.json, facts.md, endcard.txt and trivia_break.json of Episodes 1 to 50 was read
in full by five readers (ten episodes each); the curator then checked the five ledgers against each other.

- Sentence numbers are the 0-based index in each episode's `sentences.json` ("Ep14 s43").
- **The full audit, episode by episode with quoted words**, is in `story/consistency/`:
  `ep01-10.md`, `ep11-20.md`, `ep21-30.md`, `ep31-40.md`, `ep41-50.md` (about 650 words per episode: real
  moment, what happens, how it ends, form, ledger, problems, strongest and weakest moment).
  `consistency/DIGEST.md` is the three-line summary of each episode.
- This file is the cross-series reading: the timeline of Indio's own story, then what breaks it.

## 1. The verdict in ten lines

1. The spine holds: jobless witness -> the twelve -> the family -> the office -> the flood -> the last word. No
   episode has to be thrown away, and every real moment can stay where it is.
2. **It is told, not played.** Indio speaks 42 of 42 sentences in Episode 1 and 55 to 67 of about 70 in every
   later one (table in section 6). The owner's first complaint is true of all 50.
3. **One skeleton is used about forty times**: marker read aloud -> medallion heats -> Usman's line -> a list of
   history -> "Umungol si Usman" -> Tiyak / Pinagtatalunan / Kathang-isip -> return -> a sting. Section 5.
4. **A spoken fiction label is in 47 of 50 episodes** (all but 1, 2 and 5), against the ruling of 13 November.
   Section 6 lists every sentence number.
5. **The trivia break sits in the middle** of Episodes 3 to 50 (after sentence 12 to 36); the owner wants it at
   the end. The same follow line closes 41 episodes.
6. The marks, the star, the first wish, where Usman may be and "no medallion needed" each contradict
   themselves across seasons (section 3).
7. The villain web has names that never meet on screen: Tubal / Tobías Alcaín / Alcaín Builders / Dalisdis /
   Kalawang / Rufino Paredes / "ang ama ni Totoy"; and the last enemy is never named in his own episode.
8. Twenty-one threads are opened and never closed (section 4).
9. Allies are collected and not used: of about fifteen recorded creatures, four ever act before Episode 43.
10. Guard rails: no living person, party or company is given words; God is given no words. There are
    eleven places where the line is close (section 7); each has a fix in `REWRITE_PLAN.md`.

## 3. Contradictions (things the viewer is told two ways)

**The marks**
1. One mark per jump is the rule of Season 1 (Ep1 to Ep5: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5). Ep6 gives none. Ep8 s32 gives the
   sixth before any jump. Episodes 9 to 19 hold eleven jumps and no new mark; the seventh comes at Ep20. Nothing
   in Episodes 21 to 30 adds one, and Ep30 s37 says "ang pitong marka". The viewer never learns what earns a
   mark.
2. Ep8 s0 "tatlo pa lang ang tattoo" for the earlier Indio at Kawit; in Ep3 s33 he had two at that moment.
3. "Ang ikaanim na talon": Ep8 s19 and Ep9 s18 call Galilee the sixth jump; Bagumbayan (Ep6) was already his
   sixth trip, and Ep7 s13 uses "lima" for the Lolo.
4. Ep21 s66 "lima na ang marka" is the Lola speaking of the past, but it is heard after "pito" (Ep20) and reads
   as a mistake.

**The medallion and the star**
5. Where the family medallion comes from, three ways: Ep3 s37 "Mula 1898, pamilya na namin"; Ep8 s15 the loop
   ("Ito pala ang pinanggalingan"); Ep9 s17 "Nahanap niya [Lolo Pepe] ang una, ang medalyon n'yo".
6. The star (three wishes): in M's umbrella handle (Ep8 s22); "sa bulsa ko" (Ep9 s76) with no handover; Indio's
   gear (Ep11 s52); back in M's umbrella (Ep20 s48); then the hilt-and-star reveal (Ep46 s53), which Ep41 s25 had
   already given away ("nasa payong ni M").
7. Twelve points = twelve datus (Ep8 s23 to s24) against "Hindi lahat ng tuldok ay medalyon" and "may bagong
   tuldok" (Ep9 s7, s62).
8. "Kasulatan" names three things: the map (Ep8, Ep11 s52, Ep14 s46), the copperplate (Ep12 s53) and the
   medallion of Jayadewa (Ep12 s42).
9. Ep15 s12 and s29: "Malamig ang lahat ng medalyon", yet Ang Dagat carries him to Dezhou with no re-warming;
   Ep15 s24 names "Ang Daan Pauwi" before it is found.
10. Ep12 s15 to s18: he reads Kawi before he has Ang Kasulatan (s42).
11. Ep20 s31 and s33: Ang Lihim (works only in silence) and then Ang Tinig (speech) in one breath.

**The money**
12. Ep18 s4 "nasa bangko pa ang buong unang hiling" after fifty vehicles, a yacht, planes and houses; Ep18 s5
    vows no buying, against hotels in Ep16 s70 and Ep17 s62; Ep18 s8 "sold out" against s38 "hindi namin
    naibenta".
13. Ep21 s8 to s10 lists what he gave away; Ep33 to Ep38 still have the fifty; Ep37 returns them; Ep38 gives

    Ep42 says he has been in the present "mula nang magising ang lahat ng marka", Ep30 says since the blade.
16. Ep7 and Ep8: the script header says Usman does not speak; the trivia break of both opens with his line.
