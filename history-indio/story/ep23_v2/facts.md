## 4. Guard rails checked for this script

- **God has no voice, no words and no picture.** Genesis 6:3 is not spoken, summarized or pictured. The sky
  over the mountain is empty.
- **Scripture is exact or "sa buod".** One clause, word for word, with book, chapter and verse, read by the
  storyteller (s33). No character speaks a verse. The trivia's summary is marked "sa buod".
- **1 Enoch is never scripture and never fact:** "ayon sa lumang aklat ni Enoc, na wala sa Biblia ng
  karamihan ng simbahan" (s34), once, by M; the trivia repeats where it stands.
- **Nothing is asserted about angels.** The word is not in the script. The story does not say who the "anak
  ng Dios" were, what the Bantay were, or what Albo and M are.
- **No joke touches the verse.** s31 to s32 (before) and s34 (after) are plain; the sabi-sabi of s35 is about
  the internet, not the text.
- No church, living person or people of the region is named or blamed. The mountain is rock and snow in an
  undated past: no flag, post, fence, road or uniform. The people of the mountain are a boy and his sheep.
- The giant is not a monster: he strikes once, at a shield, wounds nobody, and is given back his rest.
  Albo is light and white hair, never a human type.
- "He confessed, so it worked" is not used. "It was never the bronze" is not said. No sentence says anything
  is invented. Trivia at the end. No follow card. Usman is only inside the lagusan.
- Nothing of history is changed: there is none in the scene.
