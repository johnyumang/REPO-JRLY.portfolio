One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (90 sentences). All stills NEW in the Bakunawa

**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 18 (v2).** The hook is a man who cannot make a sound, standing in a boat
with a friend shouting FOR him: not an accusation shouted at him (Ep 3), not a blade (Ep 1), not his own
drunk shout at a creature (Ep 13), not M walking out (Ep 14), not a tackle (Ep 18). The crowd is never on
land and never a mob: it is a ring of narrow boats in fog, people seated, paddles across their knees; nobody
lays a hand on him. The listening from boats is not the back row of the yard in Ep 3 and not the hillside of
Ep 9. The water is seen only from its surface: no underwater shot (Ep 11), no sea or ship (Ep 13, 18), no
canal (Ep 16), no river mud (Ep 12). The sarimanok is not the Bakunawa in the sky (Ep 13) and is not ridden
through a forest (Ep 12) or over roofs (Ep 17): it comes down once, stands on a prow, turns its back, bows,
and rises over open water. The confession is said SITTING, low in the boat, not on his knees and not under a
tree at night (Ep 14). The phone of today is on a rock by a lake at dusk, not at a locked gate in rain (Ep
17). This episode's world: grey cloud on a high lake today; pearl fog and one small fire at dawn in 1639; the
bird the only strong colour in the frame; then the same shore at dusk.

Camera (rule 10): column follows pipeline/CAMERA.md; rows marked HIGH POINT are the only big moves, with the
one establishing crane of row 11.

New stills: 45. Notes for the picture machine: Sultan Kudarat is never closer than row 17 and has no readable
face; the datus are seated, far, dignified. No mosque, no prayer, no weapons raised by the lake people:
paddles only. Bapa Usop: lean, weathered, a plain wound head cloth and a malong; keep him identical. The
sarimanok is drawn after Maranao art (many colours, long curling tail, scroll-like crest), large enough to
carry three; it is grave, never comic, and carries no fish here. Ang Tinig: a bronze disc with an open mouth
of three curved lines like sound; keep it identical for Episodes 32 and 39. The Spaniards are not drawn.
Rico's face on the phone is invented and generic; Ma'am Celia is the teacher of Episode 10, older. Indio is
plain: no watch, no sunglasses. Usman is in no present-day still.
