Kept out of the spoken script on purpose, as in the old file: Kudarat's dates and reign; the 1637 fall of

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- The Maranao and their lake: people first (a man who feeds strangers without a question, a crowd that is
  wary for a stated reason and then fair). No prayer, mosque or rite is shown; Islam is neither explained nor
  joked about; no invented religious words. The meeting is seen from the water; the datus are silent.
- One real person in view (Kudarat), far, no likeness, one rendered line from a recorded speech; nothing
  that puts guilt or a false belief on him. The Spaniards are a smell and a word, never drawn as monsters.
- The sarimanok is named as Maranao lore names it; the script does not say what the Maranao believe about it
  beyond "ibon ng mga kuwento namin".
- No living person, real firm, platform or church; "sabi ng titser ko" names nobody. Usman is only inside
  the jump. No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia at the end. No follow card.
- "He says the true thing and the dead thing wakes" is used here because BIBLE row 19 and ruling 14.7 assign
  it (14, 19, 37, 48). The confession follows the Udyok rule: "Ako muna", said aloud, everyone present hears,
  and he says the medallions are borrowed and will go back (REWRITE_PLAN row 19), not "hindi ako nagnakaw".
  Lihim and Tinig are not used together (BIBLE 9.5). No mark is gained (none in 9 to 19).
- History stands: the lake is not held; nothing he does changes the meeting or its outcome.
