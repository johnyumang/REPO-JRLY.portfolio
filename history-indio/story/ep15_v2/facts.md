**Hero line.** None. No word of Paduka Pahala survives in the sources opened (Wikipedia gives none; the only
quoted text of the moment is the emperor's epitaph). The diwa in his medallion has one INVENTED line under the
owner's exception, "Hindi na ako nakauwi sa mga anak ko. Ikaw, may naghihintay pa." (s72 to s73); it speaks only
of his own life, puts no guilt and no belief on him, and the trivia says it is not a recorded saying. The
tender moment is s40 (above), seen far off through the lens.

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- Real people of 1417 (the king, his wife, his sons, the emperor's men) are far figures in the lens with no
  words and no likeness. The one voice of 1417 is the diwa (the exception), one line.
- The fox is named as Chinese folklore names her, is a hired trickster and not a monster, and no person of
  China is shown as a villain. Nothing is taken from a grave or a museum.
- No living person, firm, brand or church. "Sabi ng titser ko" names nobody. No sentence says anything is
  invented. No scripture. Usman is not in the present day (he is only named, s20, and speaks in the trivia
  clip). No mark, no knot (M is away). Nobody says the rival's name.
- A child travels with an adult stranger: he chooses it aloud (s31 to s32), is never left alone, and ends at a
  grandmother's table with a plate. The wine is never offered to him and nobody drinks it.
- Trivia at the end. No follow card.
