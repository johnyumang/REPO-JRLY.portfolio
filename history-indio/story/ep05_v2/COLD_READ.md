# Cold-reader test — Episode 5, version 2

**The test.** A reader who had seen nothing of the series read the master script once, comment lines removed,
and retold it in five sentences.

**Result: passed.** The retelling, in the reader's words:

> 1. Si Indio, walang trabaho at bente pesos na lang ang pera, ay nakaupo sa simbahan sa San Juan at ayaw
>    sanang magbasa ng marker.
> 2. Nabasa niya nang malakas ang sulat sa isang napulot na bente, kaya uminit ang medalyon at napunta siya sa
>    Paris, Disyembre 10, 1898.
> 3. Tinulungan siya ni Ambo, isang marinong taga-Batangas na tagahugas ng pinggan, na makapasok bilang weyter
>    sa silid kung saan pinipirmahan ng mga Amerikano at Kastila ang kapalaran ng Pilipinas.
> 4. Nang makita niyang walang Pilipino sa mesa, nabitawan niya ang bandeha at itinapon siya sa kusina, kung
>    saan natanaw niya si Felipe Agoncillo na mag-isang sumusulat ng protesta.
> 5. Ibinigay niya ang huling bente kay Ambo, nagdala si Ambo ng lampara, at limang Pilipino ang tumayo sa
>    niyebe bago bumalik si Indio na may ikalimang marka.

Questions that would bring the reader back: who is the woman with the red umbrella, and why is she counting
him; how many marks are there.

Best moment: counting the faces at the table, "Wala.", and the tray falls; then Ambo holding the note:
"Presidente. Natin." Jokes that landed: "Hindi naluluma. Hindi kasi tumatagal."; "hindi makapasok kahit sa
isang opisina sa Quezon City"; "Bonjour, par."

## What confused the reader, and what was changed after the test

| Finding | Change made |
|---|---|
| "Ayoko sanang tumalon ngayong gabi" sounded like jumping off something | "ayoko sanang bumalik sa nakaraan ngayong gabi" |
| Who or what is Usman? | The picture line says "ang itim na asong si Usman"; Indio answers him: "Ikaw ang may balahibo. Ako ang naka-polo lang." |
| "Mali ang bintanang pinasok mo": he came in by a door | "Mali ang silid na pinasok mo kanina. 'Yang bintanang 'yan ang tama." |
| The two dollars a head and the twenty million are never connected | At the paper: "Ayan ang numero. Bayad ng Amerika sa Espanya." The dispute itself stays for the trivia, as the format asks |
| The count is off: Ambo and four washers are already five | Ambo, Indio and three others: five |
| "Nakapasok ako" twice; Ambo says the same thing twice | The second of each is reworded |
| "Naloko na." flat | "M na naman. Naisahan ako." |
| "Treaty of Paris" is never said | The trivia now says "sa Kasunduan sa Paris" |

Left as it is, on purpose: the note buys nothing in 1898 (the gift is what it shows Ambo, and it is still
Indio's last money); M, Marcela and the red umbrella are three different things (the woman is far and
faceless; she is named in Episode 8); the marks. The twenty-peso line is the owner's "today-thing".
