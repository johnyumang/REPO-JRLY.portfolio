One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (108 sentences). All stills NEW in the Bakunawa

**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 4 (v2).** In particular: Indio is NOT pushed, thrown out or knocked down
here (Ep 2: shoved into mud; Ep 3: pushed out of a yard; Ep 4: falls back from a gate). He is led downstairs
by a sleeve and left at a sink. No rain shelter (Ep 1), no phone screen (Ep 2), no crowd (Ep 3), no gate with
bars (Ep 4). This episode's world is cold blue night and warm gold rooms: snow, chandeliers, a scullery, one
lit window. The five in the snow must not look like Ep 1's raised fists or Ep 3's parted crowd: they stand
still, small, seen from above.
