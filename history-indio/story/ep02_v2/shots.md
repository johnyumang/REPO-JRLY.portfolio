# Shots — Episode 2 (version 2)

One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (93 sentences). The picture is what the stage
lines of the master script say. All stills are NEW in the Bakunawa look unless marked; writing in pictures is
added in code. Nobody just standing; never the same framing twice in a row.

**Must not resemble Episode 1 (v2):** a queue at a window; a pawnshop counter and hands on glass; a food stall
with a steaming pot; a roofed shrine in night rain; a yard of torches; a blade at a neck; ropes on wrists; dogs
lying at his feet; a crowd tearing papers; a woman under a lamp post; a hand lifting wax to a bulb. This
episode's world is daylight smoke, stone, a kitchen, a drain and a river bank.
