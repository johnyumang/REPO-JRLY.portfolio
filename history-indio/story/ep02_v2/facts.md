**No hero line and no tender beat in this episode, on purpose.** The owner's rule gives the iconic line to a
significant hero who appears. Nobody of that kind stands at this wall on 6 October 1762: Anda had left two days
before, and no attested or traditional line of his was found. Giving the archbishop or a British officer a
line would be an invented quote. The high point is Indio's own.

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- No real person speaks or is shown close. Rojo and Anda are only spoken of, unnamed, inside the story.
- No living person, party or firm. The pancit shout-out rule ("a real living person may be named once, kindly")
  is not used in this episode.
- The real outcome stands: Manila falls; Indio saves one invented kitchen.
- No sentence in the story says that anything is invented. The trivia is after the last line. No follow card.
- Usman is only inside the jump; no dog in the present-day scenes.
- Violence: no body is shown; the storming is seen from the end of a street and from outside the wall.
