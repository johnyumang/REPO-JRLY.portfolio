**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 19 (v2).** The hook is NOT Episode 11: there Indio himself was dragged under
at night with gold in his fists, seen from below in dark water. Here it is bright morning, seen from ABOVE the
surface: the rival, chest-deep on a pale reef, a dull bronze disc held high in one hand, a ring light floating
beside him, and Indio still dry, running in from the edge of the frame. The underwater shots (rows 29 to 33)
are shallow, sunlit and green, with the surface visible overhead; nobody clutches treasure: the picture is an
OPEN hand and a disc falling away. The battle is not the wall of Episode 2, the volley of Episode 6 or the
fort of Episode 14: it is far, across water, through mangrove roots, spears as thin lines against a sunrise,
no blood. The siyokoy never bite or claw; they hold, and the eldest has white fins. The men bouncing off the
air is not the kapre's blow (14) or the jaguar's paw (18): nobody falls hard, clubs snap, no one is hurt. The
armour closing on him is the first time in the series; the voice at dusk is not the phone call of Episode 19
(no phone, no screen): two people on sand and a far light on the sea. Lechon smoke is far off: no stall, no
sign, no vendor's face. This episode's world: white morning on a reef, grey-blue mangroves before dawn, an
orange sunrise over shallows, green sunlit water, a violet dusk.
