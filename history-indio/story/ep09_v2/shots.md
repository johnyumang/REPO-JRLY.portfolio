Revised for rule 9 (the storyteller tells every action in a full sentence): shots that were silent now carry a storyteller line; the pictures themselves do not change.

One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (109 sentences). All stills NEW in the Bakunawa
look; writing in pictures is added in code (the three references appear as plain text at the bottom of the
frame while the storyteller reads). Nobody just standing; never the same framing twice in a row.

**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 8 (v2).** Not the torch-lit yard (Ep 1), not the silent crowd by the bay
(Ep 6), not the backs of Kawit (Ep 8): this crowd is seated, in groups, on green grass, seen from high and far.
Not the gold-rimmed lens circle (Ep 7, 8): the lens is used once, on the map. The night here is a single small
flame, not a phone (Ep 7). **The Gospel scene is never closer than a long shot; the figure in white is always
far and from behind; no face; no halo effects; no shot of loaves multiplying.**
