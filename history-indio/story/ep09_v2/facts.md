## 4. Guard rails checked for this script

- **No voice plays Jesus.** The owner's rule 3 (a hero speaks an iconic line on camera) is met by the
  storyteller reading three verses with their references; "God is given no words" and SERIES.md's rule for this
  episode come first. He is seen far off and from behind, never in close-up.
- Scripture is exact or not spoken at all. Nothing is marked "buod" because nothing is paraphrased aloud.
- No reading of the miracle is offered. The sceptical "they only shared their food" line of the first draft
  was cut after the cold read (it could offend believers and read as preaching to others; SERIES.md already
  forbids it in narration). M's "aral" is about Indio (a witness watches and does not take), not a lesson
  drawn from scripture.
- No jokes from the first sight of the crowd (s21) until the storyteller says "Gabi" (s45). The one joke
  about bread on the map was cut as too close.
- No living person, church or denomination is named. Quiet trivia. No follow card.
