# Cold-reader test — Episode 9, version 2

**The test.** A reader who had seen nothing of the series read the master script once, comment lines removed,
retold it in five sentences, and was asked to read it both as a believing Christian and as a non-Christian.

**Result: passed.** The retelling, in the reader's words:

> 1. Gutom si Indio sa Kawit noong 1898, at dinala siya ni M at ng asong si Usman sa Dagat ng Galilea para
>    manood lang, bawal humawak o makipag-usap.
> 2. Mula sa malayo, nakita nila ang Guro na nagpakain ng napakaraming tao gamit ang limang tinapay at dalawang
>    isda, at may labindalawang bakol pang natira.
> 3. Pagsapit ng gabi, nakahukay sila ng lumang ilawan na sumisindi lang kapag totoo ang sinasabi mo, at may
>    aninong nakatalukbong na nagmamasid sa kanila.
> 4. Nagsinungaling si Indio na hindi siya takot, namatay ang apoy, at tumakbo siya at naiwan ang batang si Yoni.
> 5. Bumalik siya, umamin na takot siya, sumindi ulit ang ilaw, nawala ang anino, at binigyan siya ni Yoni ng
>    huling igos.

Questions that would bring the reader back: who has the white hair and why did the dog not growl; what is at
the second dot, "sa Pilipinas mismo".

Best moment: "Takot ako, Yoni. Takot na takot. Pero hindi kita iiwan." and the flame growing; the fig halved
with the dog. On respect: "mostly respectful: face unseen, verses quoted straight, 'Mula rito, walang biro' is
honoured."

## What the reader flagged, and what was changed after the test

| Finding | Change made |
|---|---|
| "Sabi ng iba, nagbahaginan lang daw sila ng baon" whispered in the middle of the miracle: a believer may bristle; M's answer may read to others as the show taking a side | CUT, both lines. The episode now offers no reading of the miracle at all |
| "Tinapay! Sa wakas, may pagkain sa mapang 'to": a joke about the loaves, close to the sacred | CUT. "Walang datu. Guhit lang. Ano'ng hahanapin natin dito?" |
| "Gutom pa rin ako. Ano'ng aral doon?" right after could read as shrugging at the miracle | "At sa unang pagkakataon, hindi ko hinanap kung ano ang makukuha ko." His answer to M at the end now connects: "Hindi lahat ng tuldok, kinukuha. 'Yung iba, sinasaksihan lang." |
| The rule "walang kakausapin" is broken at once with Yoni | The rule is now "Sa mga tao roon: walang hahawakan, walang kakausapin"; Yoni is at the edge, and Indio says "Wala akong kinausap doon" |
| "Hindi galing sa kanila": whose? | "Hindi 'yan galing sa mga taong nasa ibaba kanina." |
| "May ilaw na hindi mo malulusutan" vague | "May ilaw na ayaw sa sinungaling." |
| "Tumakbo na ako sa Bagumbayan" means nothing to a new viewer | "Pumikit na ako minsan, noong sinabihan akong huwag. May nawala sa akin. Hindi na ulit." |
| Usman is a dog only late | Picture line and Yoni: "Ang ganda ng aso mo." |

Left as it is, on purpose: the three verses read one after another while the hero only sits (that is the
episode: he witnesses); the map's words "tuldok" and "medalyon" (Episode 8 set them up); who M is.

Not done: a second cold read of the corrected text. Because of the subject, this episode should also be read
once by the owner himself before it is voiced.
