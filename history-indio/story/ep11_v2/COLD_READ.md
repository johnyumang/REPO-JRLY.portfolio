# Cold-reader test — Episode 11, version 2

**The test.** A reader who had seen nothing of the series read the master script once, comment lines removed,
and retold it in five sentences.

**Result: passed.** The retelling, in the reader's words:

> 1. Naubusan ng gas si Indio papuntang Butuan, at nang hawakan niya ang payong ni M at sabihing "Sana yumaman
>    ako," natupad ito bilang una sa tatlong hiling.
> 2. Bumili siya ng pickup at gintong alahas, tapos napunta sila sa Butuan noong taong 1011, kung saan
>    ipinakita ng batang si Agyu ang mga balangay at ikinuwento ang sugo ni Rajah Kiling sa emperador.
> 3. Nakakita si Indio ng ginto sa lumubog na bangka at sumisid kahit binalaan siya na may bantay.
> 4. Hinila siya pababa ng itim na nilalang hanggang mabitawan niya ang ginto, dumikit sa palad niya ang
>    medalyong tanso, at naintindihan niyang bantay ito, hindi halimaw, kaya ibinalik niya ang ginto at
>    iniahon siya nito.
> 5. Pagbalik sa ngayon, kakampi na niya ang Yúmud, pero may kumuha ng litrato ng sasakyan niya at may aninong
>    walang may-ari sa ilog.

Questions that would bring the reader back: who is following his money (the man in white?); what will the
other two wishes cost.

Best moment: "Ang dami kong pera. Walang nagbebenta ng hangin dito," and the watch held out as a bribe.
Jokes that landed: "Para kang bagong kaldero"; "Mayaman na. Sandali lang."; "Ako pa rin ang paborito, ha."

Mula ngayon, maiintindihan mo ang lahat ng wika. Hindi mo lang masasalita." "At itong buhol?" "Ang bantay ng ilog. Kakampi mo na siya." |
| Who is DIWA? | Named by M in that same line |
| A lake, then a river | "May bantay ang ilog na 'yan." |
| What is Champa? | "'yung malaking kaharian sa kanluran" |
| What lens? | "ang lente ni Lolo" (picture line) |
| The ending fizzles beside the underwater climax; the new power is explained and not used | A new short scene on the dock: he understands a foreign trader telling his partner to press the scale, cannot say a word of it, and points at the thumb; Agyu catches it. Usman: "Naiintindihan mo, hindi mo masabi. Ganyan ako sa'yo dati." |
| The montage line is crammed | Split into two picture lines |

Left as it is, on purpose: the man in white (a plant; he is named in Episode 48); the marks; "Nakita na kita"
(the series' line; here it is said for the first time to a creature, after he has put the gold back); the
ukay-ukay line (the "today-thing").

Length: 359 words, a little under the model; the under-water scenes are pictures.
