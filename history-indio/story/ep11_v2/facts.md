**The hero line is the diwa's, and it is invented.** No word of Rajah Kiling survives. Under the owner's
approved exception the diwa of a real, long-dead datu may speak invented lines: "Matagal akong naghintay ng
sugong marunong munang makinig." (s45). It says nothing about anything outside his own life (the first
version's second line, about Galilee, is cut: CONSISTENCY section 7, item 2). No tender beat: nothing is
recorded of a wife.

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- No real person speaks except the diwa (the exception). No likeness of Kiling: a voice only.
- The Yúmud is presented as a guard, never as a devil; its people are named with respect in the trivia.
- Indio takes nothing from an archaeological site of today: the medallion is found in 1011, in a boat that
  has not yet become a find. The present-day shrine is only where he reads aloud.
- No living person, bank, car maker or shop. No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia at the end.
- Usman is only inside the jump.
