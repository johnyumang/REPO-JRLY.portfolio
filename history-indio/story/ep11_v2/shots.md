# Shots — Episode 11 (version 2)

Revised for rule 9 (the storyteller tells every action in a full sentence): shots that were silent now carry a storyteller line; the pictures themselves do not change.

One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (99 sentences). All stills NEW in the Bakunawa
look; writing in pictures is added in code. Nobody just standing; never the same framing twice in a row.

**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 10 (v2).** First underwater episode: green-brown river light, bubbles, a
sunken hull. The port is a wide tropical river with great plank boats, not Ep 2's stone or Ep 5's snow. The
rich Indio (gold watch, chain, sunglasses pushed up) is a new look and must be a little ridiculous. No hand
gripping a wrist (Ep 8): here a hand holds an ANKLE. No lamp-in-the-dark close-up like Ep 9 until the last
shot, and then the lamp is held over water. The first hologram is a small light for three seconds, nothing
readable.
