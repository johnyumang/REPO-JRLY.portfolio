# Shots — Episode 10 (version 2)

Revised for rule 9 (the storyteller tells every action in a full sentence): shots that were silent now carry a storyteller line; the pictures themselves do not change.

One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (101 sentences). All stills NEW in the Bakunawa
look; writing in pictures is added in code. Nobody just standing; never the same framing twice in a row.

**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 9 (v2).** This is the first episode set among children in a modern school:
bright morning colour, a blackboard, a corridor, a gate with a tree. The lens circle is used here as a WINDOW
into a classroom (Ep 7 used it on a wall and a grave; Ep 8 on a crowd): frame every lens shot through a real
window pane as well, so it reads differently. No hand gripping a wrist (Ep 8: M holds his arm, loosely). No
food stall like Ep 1's paresan: the banana cue is a tray on a stool. The digging at the gate must not copy
Ep 7's dig: one person, a spoon, late afternoon.
