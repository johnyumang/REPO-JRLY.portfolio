**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 12 (v2).** Nobody goes into the water and nobody is pulled under (Ep 11);
no lamp goes out (Ep 9); nobody is thrown (Ep 12); no blade, no rope, no accusing crowd (Ep 1, Ep 3): the
shore only watches and goes quiet. The people far off at the Mass must not look like the seated crowd of Ep 9
or the five close kneelers of Ep 12: they are tiny, seen between tree trunks, with the watchers' backs in the
foreground. The night creature is not the netted, glaring mount of Ep 12 and not the dark hand of Ep 11: it
is very long, bright-scaled, calm, and it looks at him once and leaves. The rich Indio's look continues from
Ep 12 (sunglasses, gold watch) and ends this episode bare-wristed, sleeves rolled, with half a fish. This
episode's world: a white yacht deck at hard noon; then a small green island seen from under trees; then a
moonlit shore full of clay pots; then a net, a fish and a cooking fire.

**The Mass (rows 13 and 14).** Never nearer than the watchers themselves. No close view of the priest, the
table, the cross or the rajahs; no readable face on the sand. No phone, bottle or sunglasses in either still.
Both shots hold still. No sound effect but waves.

Camera (rule 10): column follows pipeline/CAMERA.md; rows marked HIGH POINT are the only big moves, plus the
one establishing `crane_down` (row 10). Every still shows a face or a body. Changes of time are a flash (the
jump) or a plain cut; no cross-dissolve, because every still has a person in it (CAMERA.md 6a).
