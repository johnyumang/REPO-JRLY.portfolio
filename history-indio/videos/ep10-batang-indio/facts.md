## Fiction (labelled in the fairness beat, captions and pinned comment)
Indio, Usman, M, the medallions, the lens, the kasulatan and its points; the gamit of this episode, the Lolo's notebook (kuwaderno) hidden in the school library, which writes a clue when a marker is read aloud (SERIES.md S2 row 2), and the school's own marker; Indio's family (the Lolo, his Saturday walks, his copy of Scott's book, his death, the money trouble); the public school, its gate, Grade 5, the recitation, "Ma'am" and the classmates; the textbook in the scene (the episode does not say which real textbook; H7 is why it is plausible that a 2012 class still met the Code). The teacher is fair: she checks the library and corrects the lesson. No real person speaks.

## How the boy knew (plausibility, 2012)
Everything the boy says was in print long before 2012: Scott's book (1968, revised 1984) and the NHI resolution (2004, approved 2005). He knows it because the Lolo owned Scott's book and folded the page. He says only "hindi po totoo 'yon"; the explanation is said by the grown-up narrator.
