# Facts — Episode 28 (Season 3 Episode 8): Ang Bundok ng mga Puwesto (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 28)

**What is true:** Mount Banahaw (Quezon and Laguna) is a stratovolcano complex about 2,170 m high and a holy mountain to many religious groups, who pray at sacred sites called *puwesto*. Apolinario de la Cruz, "Hermano Pule", of Lucban, founded the Cofradía de San José in December 1832, a brotherhood of *indio* Catholics excluded from colonial church life; it met on the 19th of each month and had several thousand members by 1841. In October 1840 243 members were arrested; his petitions to the archbishop and the Real Audiencia were refused (1841). On 23 October 1841 the cofradía, gathered on the slopes of Banahaw, beat back the forces of the alcalde; on 1 November a larger government force broke the camp and many were killed; Pule was captured and executed on 4 November 1841, aged 26. A shrine with a bust and an historical marker stands in Lucban.
**Witness rule:** the jump goes to 1841. Hermano Pule and the members are generic far figures with no words; the attack is shown with restraint (no bodies in detail, no execution close-up; the execution is told, not shown, with Usman turning away).
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, Usman, M (Mikaela), Giliw, Lola Ising, Lolo Isidro as a guide of the dead, Teddy Bracken, the flashlight, the writing in the cave, the cave itself, the notebook, the anting, the tambuli's power, the flood-wall signboard (an invented project; no real project, official or contractor is meant), and the seven depths.
**Usman:** only inside the 1841 jump and in the passage under the mountain (not the present; he crosses into the present in Ep 30).
**The find:** Teddy's flashlight; the tambuli (a horn that calls every ally, once). **Mga Kakampi:** none. **Knowledge:** 80% → 82%.
**Label for captions (Season 3 rule):** history (Hermano Pule) is "totoo ang kasaysayan"; the depths and the guide of the dead are the series' fiction.
