# Cold-reader test — Episode 10, version 2

**The test.** A reader who had seen nothing of the series read the master script once, comment lines removed,
retold it in five sentences, and was asked whether the teacher or any child is treated unfairly.

**Result: passed.** The retelling, in the reader's words:

> 1. Bumalik si Indio, kasama sina M at Usman, sa dati niyang paaralan noong 2012 para silipin sa lente ang
>    sarili niya noong Grade 5.
> 2. Sinabi ng batang Indio na hindi totoo ang Kodigo ni Kalantiaw, kaya pinagtawanan siya, pinalabas sa
>    pasilyo, at ang kaklaseng sumagot ayon sa libro ang binigyan ng medalya.
> 3. Gusto ng malaking Indio na lapitan at aluin ang sarili niya, pero pinigilan siya ni M.
> 4. Umamin ang batang Indio ..., kaya umamin din ang batang may medalya ..., at kinabukasan sinabi ni Ma'am
>    na tama si Indio.
> 5. Sa kasalukuyan, hinukay ni Indio ang latang ibinaon ng Lolo niya: may papel na "Tama ka" at kuwadernong
>    may babala.

Questions that would bring the reader back: who is "ang batang laging nauuna"; where are the ships going.

Best moment: Bea splitting the banana cue, "Hindi ako tumawa, ha"; and "Hindi na binawi ang medalya" / "Pero
narinig ko ang 'tama ka'." On fairness: the teacher "is treated well: gentle, checks the book, corrects
herself publicly."

## What the reader flagged, and what was changed after the test

| Finding | Change made |
|---|---|
| The boy owning up to wiping the blackboard comes from nowhere, and why it makes the other boy confess is a leap | Replaced: the boy tells the teacher, "Ma'am, baka po mali ako. Hindi ko pa po tapos basahin ang libro ni Lolo. Pero 'yun po talaga ang nabasa ko." The other boy, listening: "Kinabisa ko lang po 'yung sa akin. Hindi ko rin po alam kung totoo." |
| A teacher may wince at a child sent out for a polite correction | She no longer sends him out: he runs out when the class laughs; she calls after him, quiets the class, comes out and kneels to him. The medal is hung by a visitor before she can stop it |
| The boy's "walang patunay na mas matanda pa ang kodigo sa taong 1914" is stiff for a ten-year-old | "walang lumang papel na bumabanggit sa kodigo. Bago lang po ito lumitaw. Hindi po tugma." |
| How did the Lolo get the test paper? "Sampung taon" does not fit 2012 to now | The teacher hands the paper to the old man who fetches the boy (picture); "Itinago ni Lolo. Lahat ng taong 'yon." |
| "Kapag kaya na niyang tumayo nang mag-isa sa pasilyo": he already did | "Kapag handa na siyang maniwala sa sarili niya." |
| "Ang pangalawang tuldok": of what? The lens is unexplained | "sa mapa"; M: "Ipapakita niyan ang nangyari rito." |

Left as it is, on purpose: the chant twice (the hook is a flash-forward); who M and Usman are; the ships (the
next episode's door); the banana cue line (the "today-thing"; it is also what Bea shares).
