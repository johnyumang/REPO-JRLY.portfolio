# Facts — Episode 19 (Season 2 Episode 11): Ang Tinig (checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:**
- Lake Lanao lies about 700 m above sea level.
- In 1637 the Spaniards under Governor-General Corcuera took Sultan Kudarat's fort at Lamitan, and Kudarat escaped.
- In 1639 Spanish forces entered Lanao. Kudarat went to the Lanao datus and urged them to resist.
- A speech of his was recorded in a Spanish source. Within months the Spaniards withdrew, and they did not hold inland Lanao again for about two and a half centuries.
- The sarimanok is the Maranao legendary bird, from the totem bird Itotoro.

**Witness rule:**
- No real person speaks.
- The 1639 gathering is seen from afar through the lens, and Indio says he cannot hear it (sentences 23–24).
- The speech is only reported as a Spanish record and paraphrased from it (sentences 25–26). The script says it is a translation of a Spanish record, not Kudarat's own exact words (sentence 58).
- Sultan Kudarat appears only as the diwa, with two invented lines (sentences 38 and 47) under the approved datu exception, labelled in sentence 60.

**Fiction, on purpose:**
- Indio, M and Usman; Rico Bulan and his videos; the fisherman.
- The marker by the lake (its text is not quoted; no specific real marker is claimed).
- The island nest; the medallion; the diwa and his lines; "Ang Tinig" and its limit.
- The sarimanok as a living bird, the pact and the flight; the hologram; "Alcaín Builders".

**Find:** the medallion of Sultan Kudarat, "Ang Tinig" (fiction). **Power used on screen:** Indio's true sentences come out clearly; a lie would come out as silence. **Sakay and Mga Kakampi:** the sarimanok. **Hologram:** six marks, knowledge 54%.

**Rules kept:** nothing is taken from a museum or a grave (a nest on an island, in the past); Usman appears only inside the jump; no flood-control thread (Rico's attack is about relics, not contracts).

**The fall, by suggestion only (SERIES.md):** sentences 10 and 41 name drink and nights he cannot remember, as a cost, with no detail.

## Kept out of the spoken script on purpose
- Kudarat's dates (1581–1671) and reign (about 1616/1619–1671).
- The 1645 treaty with Governor Fajardo.
- His dealings with the Dutch, including the sale of rice and of slaves taken in raids on the Visayas (WP "Muhammad Kudarat"). This is true and is part of the honest record; it is not said in this episode because the episode's subject is the 1639 speech. It is noted here so a later episode or caption does not present him as spotless.
- The leaders of the 1639 expedition (Atienza, Fr. Agustín de San Pedro).
- The present-day Marawi (left out on purpose).

- Who recorded the speech, and whether it was 1638 or 1639: both are stated as disputed.
- The full Spanish original was not read.
- The marker by the lake is a story device; no specific real marker or text is claimed.
