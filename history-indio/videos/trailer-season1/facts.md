## Music and the national anthem
Not Lupang Hinirang. Republic Act 8491 (Flag and Heraldic Code of the Philippines, 1998): Sec. 37 requires the anthem, played or sung, to follow Julian Felipe's musical arrangement and composition; Sec. 38 says it "shall not be played and sung for mere recreation, amusement or entertainment purposes" except on listed occasions; Sec. 50 sets penalties (public censure, plus a fine and/or imprisonment of up to one year; summaries give the fine as PHP 5,000–20,000). A trailer is entertainment, and a remix would break Sec. 37 too. Checked through search summaries of the law text (jur.ph, digest.ph, the Prezi and SlideShare reproductions); the official text (Official Gazette, lawphil.net) could not be opened from this session, and bills to amend RA 8491 have been filed, so re-check the fine before quoting it publicly.
