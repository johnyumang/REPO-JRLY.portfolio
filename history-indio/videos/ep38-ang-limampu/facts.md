# Facts — Episode 38 (Season 4): Ang Limampu (written from Kabanata 38, story version 2; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** Laguna de Bay is the largest lake in the Philippines and is used for regional flood management. Bayanihan is the Filipino idea of community cooperation, whose traditional symbol is neighbours carrying a whole bahay-kubo to a new place (no longer common). Carlos "Botong" Francisco (1912–1969), National Artist from Angono, painted "Bayanihan" among his murals.
**Ages and offices (SERIES.md, Season 4):** Indio is 38 and a senator; the real minimum for senator (35) is respected. No calendar year is given for the future.
**Fiction, on purpose:** the flood of this chapter, San Lorenzo, the floodgates, Engineer Vangie, Rico Bulan, Tubal and Kalawang, the medallion powers and allies, the receipted dikes. No real living person, party, company or office-holder is shown or accused. The 2025 flood-control investigations are not named here.
**No vice is shown here.** Sinag's temptation is to keep one vehicle; Indio refuses.
