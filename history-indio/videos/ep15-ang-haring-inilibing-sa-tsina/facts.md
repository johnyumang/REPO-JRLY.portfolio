# Facts — Episode 15 (Season 2 Episode 7): Ang Haring Inilibing sa Tsina — version 1 (written 2026-11-12, to be checked against story version 2 before frames)

**What is true:** Paduka Pahala of Sulu sailed in 1417 with over 340 people and two other Sulu kings; the Yongle Emperor received him in Beijing for 27 days; he died at Dezhou on the way home along the Grand Canal; the emperor built his tomb there; his eldest son went home, his widow, two sons and attendants stayed, and descendants surnamed An and Wen still live in Dezhou. The húli jīng is a fox spirit of Chinese folklore.
**Witness rule:** no jump to the past in this episode. The 1417 events are seen only through the lens, at a distance; Paduka Pahala and the Yongle Emperor are generic far figures with no words. The diwa's two invented lines are labelled in the fairness beat.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, Totoy, Lola Ising, M, the fifty vehicles and the fall, the medallions, the crossing, the woman in white and her words, the lamp, the diwa's voice, Ang Daan Pauwi, the Lola's confession.
**Usman:** absent (no jump); the sidecar is empty with his red kerchief; Indio growls in his place in the fairness beat.
**The find:** the medallion of Paduka Pahala, "Ang Daan Pauwi". **Power used on screen:** it takes Indio home to the Lola. **Mga Kakampi:** none (the fox follows him unrecorded). **Knowledge:** 18% at the bottom, rising to 26% by the end (ebook spine).
**Sources:** the season2/research.md Dezhou block, which rests on Wikipedia "Paduka Pahala" (read in full) and two news pieces seen only as search summaries. Re-open those before quoting a figure on screen.
