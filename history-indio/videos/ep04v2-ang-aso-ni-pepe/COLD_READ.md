# Cold-reader test — Episode 4, version 2

**The test.** A reader who had seen nothing of the series read the master script once, comment lines removed,
and retold it in five sentences.

**Result: passed.** The retelling, in the reader's words:

> 1. Pumunta si Indio sa Calamba dahil iyon lang ang salitang iniwan ng Lolo niya, at naubos ang huling pera
>    niya sa buko pie.
> 2. Nang basahin niya ang marker, uminit ang medalyon at napunta siya sa Calamba noong Hunyo 1869, kung saan
>    tumakbo palayo ang aso niyang si Usman papasok sa isang bahay na bato.
> 3. Hinarang siya ng isang mabangis na batang asong itim, pero sa payo ni Andoy ay umupo siya, naghintay, at
>    ibinigay ang pie, kaya umamo ito.
> 4. Nalaman niyang ang batang aso ay si Usman mismo noong bata pa, aso ng batang si Pepe, na umiiyak habang
>    nagpapaalam sa ina para mag-aral sa Biñan.
> 5. Pagbalik sa ngayon, nag-text si Lola Ising na ayon sa lumang sobre ng Lolo, kadugo raw nila si Pepe.

Questions that would bring the reader back: is Indio really related to Rizal, and why does he smell like that
house; who asked before him.

Best moment: the two black dogs face to face with the same white patch, "Ako 'yan, par. Noong hindi pa kita
kilala."; then the boy's trembling chin. Jokes that landed: "Malas pati sa aso"; "Mahal ang bayad. Isang buong
pie"; "Eh di hindi mo aso 'yon."

## What confused the reader, and what was changed after the test

| Finding | Change made |
|---|---|
| Nobody says "Rizal"; a child may not know who Pepe is | Andoy: "Si Pepe. José Rizal ang buong pangalan." Indio: "Rizal? 'Yung nasa piso?" "Anong piso?" |
| "Tatlong talon": three what? | "Tatlong beses na kaming tumalon sa nakaraan." |
| The dog talks and jokes? Not established | Indio, as Usman runs: "Hindi ka man lang babati? Ikaw pa namang asong madaldal." |
| Who can see whom? Usman went in to the boy, unseen | Simplified: Usman never goes in. He stops at the gate, muzzle through the bars, and will not turn round when called. The boy hugs only his own young dog, and Indio steps back |
| The moth line works only if you know the story | One whispered line after it: "Gamu-gamo. 'Yung lumapit sa apoy kahit pinagbawalan.", with a small inset picture |
| "Hindi raw ako ang unang Bracken na nagtanong": who is Bracken? where does it come from? | It is now inside the Lola's text ("At hindi raw ikaw ang unang nagtanong."), and Indio asks: "Eh sino ang nauna sa akin?" |
| Flat jokes: "Mamaya ka na"; "At gutom pa rin ako" | "Hapunan kita mamaya."; "At kinain na naman ng aso ang hapunan ko." |

Left as it is, on purpose: the medallion and the marks (series mechanics, one line each); the hook repeating
inside scene 3 (the owner's rule); the buko pie line (the "today-thing"; it also becomes the price he pays).

Not done: a second cold read of the corrected text.
