# Facts — Episode 14 (Season 2 Episode 6): Ang Kuta sa Bunganga ng Ilog (written from Kabanata 14, story version 1; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** the site of Fort Santiago held the palisaded fort of Rajah Matanda, armed with bronze guns; Martín de Goiti came in 1570 and the fortified city burned; Legazpi founded Spanish Manila on 24 June 1571 after the rulers of Maynila and Tondo came to terms; the fort and the first walls were built in stone from 1590 to 1593. The kapre as Philippine folklore.
**The fall (by suggestion only):** bottles at noon, strangers asleep, a morning he cannot remember, a small bag he hides; M sees him with another woman and leaves without a word. Nothing explicit, nothing glamorised.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M, Usman, JM, the fleet of 50, the medallion and its voice, Ang Dagat and its limit, the kapre scene, the men in black and the unnamed man with the triangle mark, the vlog, the hologram. Usman appears only inside the jump.
**The find:** the medallion of Rajah Matanda, "Ang Dagat". **Recorded:** the kapre. **No flood control anywhere.**
