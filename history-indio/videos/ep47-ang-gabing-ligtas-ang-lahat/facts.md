# Facts — Episode 47 (Season 5 Episode 6): Ang Gabing Ligtas ang Lahat (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 47)

**What is true:** eleven balangay boats have been found in Butuan (only about three excavated and recovered; the rest remain in situ), dated in current Wikipedia to 689–988 CE (7th to 10th century), though older reports gave disparate dates; Genesis 11:1–9 tells of Babel, where the language of the people was confounded and they were scattered; whether the word "barangay" comes from "balangay" is commonly taught but disputed by some historians; "the father of lies" is how John 8:44 speaks of the devil.
**Witness rule:** no real historical person appears. Scripture is not quoted: the script only says what a passage contains; God is given no words.
**Restraint (binding):** the night of Indio and M is shown only as a walk on the pier, an open red umbrella, a closed door of a small hut, and the morning; nothing explicit. Both are adults (Indio is 42, said aloud in sentence 41: SERIES "Season 4: ages, offices and the law" and "Invented names").
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M (Mikaela), Usman, Lola Ising, Inay (Liwayway), Teddy, Tala, Kapitan Totoy, the arks, the three wishes and the star (the second wish: one night in which everyone is safe), the hologram, the child to come. No real living person is meant.
**Label for captions (Season 3–5 rule):** "Ang tagpo ay ayon sa salaysay ng serye; kathang-isip ang mga tauhan."
**Find:** none; the second wish is spent. **Mga Kakampi:** a small light with no name yet. **Knowledge:** 100%; one wish left.
