# Facts — Episode 31 (Season 3 Episode 11, season finale): Ang Pader sa Papel (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 31)

**What is true:** Kawit, Cavite, is a low-lying coastal town that catches water flowing down from the upland parts of the province, and towns along the Cavite coast (Kawit, Noveleta, Rosario, Bacoor and others) have flooded again and again, with evacuations. The word "barangay" is commonly traced to *balangay*, the plank boat; dictionaries and historians differ on the exact path of the word.
