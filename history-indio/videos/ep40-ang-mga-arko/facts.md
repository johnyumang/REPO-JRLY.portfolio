# Facts — Episode 40 (Season 4): Ang mga Arko (written from Kabanata 40, story version 2; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** the balangay of Butuan are plank boats joined edge to edge with dowels and fibre lashings; the first were excavated from 1976 near the Masao River, and the boats date from about the 7th to the 10th century (689–988). Scripture quoted exactly from Ang Dating Biblia (1905): Genesis 9:11 (the promise after the flood) and Revelation 12:15 (the serpent spews water like a river). In the story, the coming water is the dragon's, not God's: Genesis 9:11 is kept.
**Ages and offices:** Indio is 41 in his first year as President (minimum 40 respected). No calendar year is given for the future.
**Fiction, on purpose:** the arks (invented public works built in the open with the books shown), the quiet oath, the forward crossing, the allies at work, Tay Sebyo, Totoy and his father, Tubal, Rico Bulan, Sinag. No real person, party or company. "Ang gunaw ng kuwentong ito" is fiction and not taught as a lesson of faith.

- **The four foreign guests** of the chapter (Hu Lanxi, Okada Haru, Agnes Thornbury, Don Chuy Tabares) are not named in this episode.
