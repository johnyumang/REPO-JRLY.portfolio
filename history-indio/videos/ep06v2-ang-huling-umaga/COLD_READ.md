# Cold-reader test — Episode 6, version 2

**The test.** A reader who had seen nothing of the series read the master script once, comment lines removed,
and retold it in five sentences. This reader was also asked whether anything felt disrespectful to the death.

**Result: passed.** The retelling, in the reader's words:

> 1. Nagplantsa si Indio at pumunta sa Luneta nang alas-sais para sa una niyang date kay M, dala ang taho.
> 2. Pinabasa siya ni M ng marker nang malakas, at napunta siya sa Bagumbayan, Disyembre 30, 1896, kasama ang
>    asong si Usman at ang batang nagtitinda ng tubig na si Kiko.
> 3. Doon lumitaw si M na may pulang payong, hinawakan ang kamay niya, at sinabing huwag siyang pipikit habang
>    binabaril si Rizal.
> 4. Pumikit pa rin si Indio sa putok, at pagdilat niya ay wala na si M at ang medalyon niya, sulat na lang
>    ang naiwan.
> 5. Naiwan siya sa 1896 na walang daan pauwi, pero naririnig pa rin niya si Usman at hindi ito naglaho.

Questions that would bring the reader back: how does he get home with no medallion; who is M and where is
"pinanggalingan"; why can he still hear the dog.

Best moment: Kiko's "Tingnan mo kung sino ang may hawak ng riple. Mga kababayan natin," with the second line
aiming at the first; and "Libre na ngayong umaga. Walang gustong uminom." The reader found nothing
disrespectful: the death is off screen and quiet, and the dog's howl is right.

## What confused the reader, and what was changed after the test

| Finding | Change made |
|---|---|
| The hook ("Pagdilat ko, wala na sa akin ang lahat") gives away the ending | "Sabi niya, huwag akong pipikit. Isang segundo lang akong pumikit. Isang segundo lang." |
| "Pepe" is not said to be Rizal | "Si Pepe. Si Rizal. 'Yung batang umiiyak sa Calamba." |
| Who is the woman with the book? | Kiko: "Si Josephine raw, ang mahal niya." |
| "Consummatum est" is not translated | Kiko, after the silence: "Narinig mo, kuya? 'Tapos na,' sabi niya. Sa wikang Latin." |
| The film's "he turned to face the sky" is set up and never paid | Indio, after: "Hindi ko nakita kung bumaling siya. Pumikit ako." The trivia says it is told, not proven |
| Indio's panic about the medallion comes seconds after the shots and makes the death a backdrop | A beat of silence first: the hat on the grass, Kiko crossing himself, Kiko's line, Indio's line. Only then does he notice his empty hand |
| Was the theft because he blinked? | Usman: "Pumikit ka, par. Kaya walang marka. Kaya rin niya nakuha." |
| "Bawat order. Bawat tip." is opaque to a new viewer | "Bawat order ng pancit. Bawat tip. Bawat bente. Siya pala ang nagdala sa akin dito, hakbang-hakbang." |
| "Sa tanso 'yon" took a second | "sa medalyon 'yon" |

Left as it is, on purpose: the marks (one line: "Kaya walang marka"); "Walang jeep", the one dry joke after the
death, placed after the water and the long shadow shot; the taho line (the owner's "today-thing", before the
jump, away from the execution).

A ten-year-old, per the reader: follows the execution and the theft; heavy but nothing graphic. A grandmother:
follows the Rizal part and is moved; the phone and M need clear pictures (shots 7, 15 to 17, 33 to 35).
