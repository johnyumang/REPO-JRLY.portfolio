# Facts — Episode 24 (Season 3 Episode 4): Ang Reyna ng Lungsod na Lumubog (checked 2026-11-12; script version 1 from story version 2)

**What is true here is a text, not a place.** Atlantis appears only in Plato's dialogues *Timaeus* and *Critias* (about 360 BC). Classicists agree it is fiction, an allegory about the pride of nations. The episode visits Plato's story through a portal and says so; it never presents Atlantis as a real sunken city.

**Corrections to the story chapter (version 1):**
- The chapter's "mga apat na raan at animnapung taon bago si Kristo" is wrong: Plato wrote about **360 BC**.
- The kraken is from Scandinavian lore, not from Plato. The script says so.

**Fiction (labelled):** the portal, the queen, Sinag, the cup and the bed, the net on the kraken, the pact, Ma'am Celia's video call, and everything Indio, M and Usman do there.
