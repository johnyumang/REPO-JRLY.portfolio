(both checked 2026-11-12; Wikipedia articles read in full, other pages as search summaries). New in version 2

## 1. What is real in this script, and where it is said

| Line (sentences.json) | Claim | Status and sources |
|---|---|---|

| s68 (BAUTISTA) | "¡Independencia!" | Ambrosio Rianzares Bautista wrote and read the Acta de la proclamación de independencia del pueblo Filipino, in Spanish: CONFIRMED (Wikipedia; thecorpusjuris.com). | Trivia | 12 June 1898; window, balcony 1919; Bautista wrote and read the Acta in Spanish; 98 signers; the band and the march without words; who waved the flag is disputed | All from the checked tables (Ep 3 rows; Ep 8 rows A3, A4, A19) |

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- One real person speaks one word: Bautista (see s68 above). Aguinaldo and Hilaria are seen and silent. Faces are
  generic drawn figures, not likenesses. No living person; the "named once, kindly" rule is not used here.
- The story does not confirm any bloodline (BIBLE section 14, ruling 3): the Lola guesses.
- The real outcome stands. No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia after the last line. No follow card.
- Usman is only inside the jump.
