# Cold-reader test — Episode 3, version 2

**The test.** A reader who had seen nothing of the series read the master script once, comment lines removed,
and retold it in five sentences.

**Result: passed.** The retelling, in the reader's words:

> 1. Natanggal si Indio sa trabaho kaya umuwi siya kay Lola Ising sa Kawit, at pinapunta siya nito sa bahay ni
>    Aguinaldo dala ang medalyon.
> 2. Napunta siya sa Hunyo 12, 1898, at doon nakita niya ang isang paslit na may kaparehong medalyon.
> 3. Akala niya ninakaw ang kanya kaya hinila niya ito sa leeg ng bata, sinigawan siyang magnanakaw, at
>    itinulak palabas ng bakuran.
> 4. Nakita niya sa alikabok ang sarili niyang medalyon, kaya bumalik siya pasan ang tambol ni Tinong at
>    humingi ng tawad sa babae.
> 5. Pinadaan siya hanggang harap, napanood ang watawat at ang "¡Independencia!", tapos pag-uwi sinabi ni Lola
>    na baka ninuno niya ang bata at "Calamba" ang iniwang salita ni Lolo Isidro.

Questions that would bring the reader back: who is the woman who is "hindi taga-ngayon"; what is in Calamba.

Best moment: the apology with the drum on his shoulder, then "Hmp. Padaanin ang tambol!" and the crowd
parting; also "Sa libro ko, isang linya lang ito. Dito, yumayanig ang lupa." Jokes that landed: half a seat in
the jeep; "Kanina pinalabas ka. Ngayon, pinapadaan ka pa"; "Anong balkonahe, kuya?"

## What confused the reader, and what was changed after the test

| Finding | Change made |
|---|---|
| Who or what is Usman? | The picture line now says "ang itim na asong si Usman", and Indio answers him: "Ikaw ang aso. Ikaw ang huwag mawala." |
| "Basahin mo nang malakas": read what? He is suddenly in 1898 | Lola: "...basahin mo nang malakas ang marker", and a picture line of Indio reading the marker at the shrine (which also shows the balcony of today) |
| Two women confused: the one with the child and the one who shouts | The shouter is now TINDERA, a vendor with a bilao; a picture line says the woman in the salakot stays silent |
| "Ako na lang ang hindi pa nare-recycle" unclear | "Ako na lang ang lumang hindi pa napapakinabangan." |
| "Palo" heard as a blow | "isang patpat ng tambol ko" |
| "Ako ang muntik magnakaw" said twice | Once as his thought; the spoken apology now says "Walang kasalanan ang bata." |
| "Sino'ng nagdagdag?" of the balcony is never answered | On his return: "At ayan na ang balkonahe. Idinagdag lang pala pagkatapos." The year is in the trivia |

Left as it is, on purpose: the red umbrella behind the door (a plant for Episode 15, picture only); the wax on
the medallion (it is from Episode 1's pawnshop and is how he knows which one is his); the marks. The reader
found the jeepney line the most lecture-like: it is the owner's "today-thing", two sentences and a joke, inside
a present-day part held under 40 seconds.
