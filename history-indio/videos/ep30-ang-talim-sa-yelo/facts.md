# Facts — Episode 30 (Season 3 Episode 10): Ang Talim sa Yelo (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 30)

**What is true (only what the script says):**
- Genesis 3:24, quoted exactly from Ang Dating Biblia (1905): "...inilagay sa silanganan ng halamanan ng Eden ang mga Querubin at ang isang nagniningas na tabak na umiikot, upang ingatan ang daang patungo sa kahoy ng buhay." The sword is placed WITH the cherubim; the text does not say a cherub holds it; scholars read it variously.
- Dante's *Inferno* is a poem, not scripture. Its hell has nine circles; the lowest, the ninth, is the frozen Cocytus, for traitors.
- John 8:44 calls the devil a liar "at ama nito" (Ang Dating Biblia: "sapagka't siya'y isang sinungaling, at ama nito"). The script paraphrases, saying only that he is called a liar and its father.

**Fiction, on purpose:** the seven depths (the series' own count, "hindi ito ang bilang ni Dante"), the princes Poot, Linlang and Palalo, the blade as a piece of the flaming sword, Indio's awakening and the end of his need for a medallion, Usman's crossing into the present, Lolo Isidro as a guide of the dead, Teddy, Liwayway, Tala, Lola Ising, Biringan's gate, Rico Bulan's shadow, the hologram. The diwa of Paduka Pahala speaks one invented line under the first approved exception ("Ang tahanan ay kung saan may naghihintay, iho."); his real record: a ruler of Sulu who went to the Ming court in 1417 and died at Dezhou on the way home (see Episode 15 facts.md).

**Restraint:** the marks "bleed" as light through ink, never as wounds; no gore. No real person appears. No scripture is mocked; God is given no words.

**Label for captions (Season 3 rule):** "Ang tagpo ay ayon sa ating kuwento; ang talata ay mula sa Genesis 3:24 (Ang Dating Biblia, 1905); ang mga bilog ay mula sa tula ni Dante." Every caption and the pinned comment also carry: "Imbento ang linya ng diwa ni Paduka Pahala; ang totoo, dumalaw siya sa korte ng Ming noong 1417 at namatay sa Dezhou pauwi." The YouTube description quotes Genesis 3:24 exactly ("...sa silanganan ng halamanan ng Eden ... ang daang patungo..."), checked against three independent copies of Ang Dating Biblia.

**The find:** the blade of the Trinity. **Mga Kakampi:** none. **Hologram:** 7 marks (open), Knowledge 88%, new line "Hindi na kailangan ng medalyon".
