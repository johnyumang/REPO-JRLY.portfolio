# Facts — Episode 26 (Season 3 Episode 6): Ang Baging ng mga Kaluluwa (written 2026-11-12)

**What is true here is what careful studies and first-hand accounts REPORT about a ceremony; the episode adds the series' fiction.** The plant-vision ceremony of the Upper Amazon (the brew is called ayahuasca in Peru) is used by Indigenous peoples, among them the Shipibo-Konibo of the Ucayali river in Peru, and by the mestizo healing tradition called vegetalismo. The script names no plant and says nothing about how the brew is made, obtained or dosed (user's and conductor's rule). What the script says about the experience comes from the studies below.
**Witness rule:** no real person appears or speaks. Maestra Adela is an invented character (a Shipibo-Konibo healer), shown as a full person with a family and humour; her songs are NOT real icaros: only original humming (no recording of a real song may be used).
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M, Usman (only inside the vision; he is not in the present in this episode), Maestra Adela and her family, the visitor (Sinag), the diwa of Rajah Kiling, the lamp, the road to the city, the woman at the door, the key and its new groove.
**Character rule (SERIES.md, "The vision-journey episode"):** Indio has just come back from drink and drugs, so he fears a relapse and M objects; afterwards he sorts what he saw into tiyak, pinagtatalunan and kathang-isip; a vision is not proof; Sinag tempts him to keep chasing visions and he refuses.
**Knowledge:** 76% (ebook spine).
**Before publishing:** a Shipibo-Konibo reader (or a scholar of Shipibo culture) should review the episode, its frames and captions. Platforms (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok) will very likely age-restrict it; the captions carry a plain "huwag gayahin" line and the law.
**How this was checked:** every source below was seen ONLY as a web-search summary on 2026-11-12 (maps.org and the journal pages were not opened from this machine). Re-open them before any figure is quoted on screen as a number.
