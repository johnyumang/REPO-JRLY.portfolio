# Facts — Episode 42 (Season 5 Episode 1): Ang Buwan na Parang Dugo (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 42)

**What is true:** In Visayan folklore the Bakunawa is a sea serpent (a "moon-eating dragon") that swallows the moon and so causes eclipses. A Spanish-era account (Fr. Tomás Ortiz, *Práctica del Ministerio*, 1731, as given in Blair & Robertson vol. 43) says that during a lunar eclipse people in various districts went out with bells and other noisemakers and struck them to protect the moon, "which they say is being eaten or swallowed by the dragon, tiger, or crocodile". Bernardo Carpio is a Tagalog legend: a giant (or man of great strength) caught between two great rocks in the mountains of Montalban (now Rodriguez, Rizal), whose struggling causes earthquakes. Revelation 6:12 puts a great earthquake, a darkened sun and a moon "like blood" together at the opening of the sixth seal; Revelation 12:15 has the serpent pour water like a flood from its mouth; Genesis 9:11 is God's promise that all flesh will not again be destroyed by a flood.
**Label for captions (Season 5 rule):** "Kuwento ng serye ang gunaw at ang digmaan, hinango sa Pahayag 6:12 at 12:15; sa Genesis 9:11, hindi Diyos ang nagpadala ng baha. Kuwentong-bayan ang Bakunawa at si Bernardo Carpio."
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio as President (age 42, spoken in sentence 4; no calendar year spoken), Usman, M (Mikaela), Sinag, Tay Sebyo, Nong Doming (an old Limasawa fisherman of the present who only resembles the fisherman Indio met there in Episode 13; he is not that man, who lived in 1521 per ep13 script_multispeaker.txt lines 23, 36-38, 46; sentence 55), the arks, the flood walls, the flood itself and the war, the Bakunawa as the moon's guard, the Bakunawa as a mount, the sarimanok as a mount, the lens, the hologram, the Sandugo seal, the shepherd's crook and the "army of the dragon". No real living person, party or company appears.
**Usman:** present (in the present since Episode 30, as the story says; the episode says so in sentence 11).
**The find:** the Bakunawa, recorded under "Sakay" (and as a Kakampi). **Power used:** the lens (shows only what is true), the Sandugo (seal of a pact with a former enemy). **Knowledge:** 100% (the crook is open).

## Changes from the story chapter, and why
- M only names the reference (as in the story); Indio then gives the exact 1905 fragment "at ang buong buwan ay naging gaya ng dugo" (the book's note "ilalagay ang eksaktong teksto"). Genesis 9:11 is read as written, never voiced as God.
- The hologram list is shortened to six creatures already recorded in earlier episodes (húli jīng, Spring-heeled Jack and the nahual are named "at marami pa") to keep the line speakable.
- "ang ibong kulay-bahaghari" became "ang ibong makulay": Episode 19 draws it in red, gold, green and blue, and "rainbow" is not in the Maranao sources.
- Indio's age is spoken (sentence 4: "Apatnapu't dalawa na ako", the age Episode 41 gives for the oath), as SERIES.md asks for each office; no calendar year or real date of the future is spoken.
- Revelation 12:15 is marked as a summary ("buod ng Pahayag 12:15") in sentence 65, since it is not word for word.
