# Facts — Episode 23 (Season 3 Episode 3): Ang mga Bantay sa Hermon (written from Kabanata 23, story version 2; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** Genesis 6:1–4 (scripture, quoted exactly in part from Ang Dating Biblia 1905, never mocked; God is given no words) names "ang mga anak ng Dios", "ang mga anak na babae ng mga tao" and "ang mga higante" before the flood. The 200 Watchers, their oath and Mount Hermon come from 1 Enoch (chapters 6–7), an old book counted as scripture only by the Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox Tewahedo churches and Beta Israel, not by most churches; its oldest parts date to about 300–200 BC. Mount Hermon is a real mountain on the Lebanon–Syria border.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Albo (the White Watcher, a being of light with white hair; never "Nordic"), the Giant of Hermon, the cloak, the feather, the stone circles, Liwayway's story, M's origin. Albo says he is not one of Enoch's 200, so the story does not add to 1 Enoch.
**Mount Hermon today** is a contested border area: the frames show the mountain and snow only, with no flags, troops or posts.
