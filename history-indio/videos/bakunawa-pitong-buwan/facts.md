# Facts — Ang Halimaw na Kumain ng Anim na Buwan (checked 2026-11-11)
| Claim | Status | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Visayan legend: Bathala made seven moons, one for each night of the week; the Bakunawa swallowed six | legend (told as "ayon sa alamat") | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakunawa , https://owlcation.com/humanities/facts-about-bakunawa-dragon-seven-moons |
| Bakunawa is a giant sea serpent with a mouth "the size of a lake" | legend | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakunawa |
| People banged pots, gongs and drums to make it release the moon; some versions have them shout "Bitaw!" | legend / reported custom; "Bitaw" from one source | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakunawa , https://www.aswangproject.com/bakunawa/ |
| Eclipses were explained as the Bakunawa swallowing the moon | recorded belief | https://www.aswangproject.com/bakunawa/ , https://theweeklysillimanian.wordpress.com/2018/08/17/the-blood-red-lunar-eclipse-mythology/ |
| The moon-swallowing serpent "bacunaua" is recorded in Fr. Alonso de Mentrida's Visayan dictionary in the 1600s | confirmed in secondary sources; year given as 1637 by most, 1632/1628 by others, so the script says "1600s" | https://www.aswangproject.com/bakunawa/ , https://bibliotikal.com/posts/bakunawa-myth-origins.html |
| The seven-moons story first appears in writing in 1913, in Fernando Buyser's Mga Sugilanong Karaan | reported by Wikipedia and one other source | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakunawa , https://bibliotikal.com/posts/bakunawa-myth-origins.html |
| It may be an early-20th-century retelling of older oral folklore | commentary, not settled | https://bibliotikal.com/posts/bakunawa-myth-origins.html |

Not verified: primary sources (Mentrida's dictionary, Buyser's book) were not read; search summaries only. The moon being "released" (vs. spat out, or Bathala intervening) differs by version.

Added: Bakunawa has gills, wings and a red tongue (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakunawa); the Bagobo of Mindanao tell of Minokawa, a giant bird that swallows the moon (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minokawa) — from the page titles/summaries and prior knowledge, not re-read in full.
