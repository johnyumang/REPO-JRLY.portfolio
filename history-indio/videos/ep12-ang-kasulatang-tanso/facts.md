# Facts — Episode 12 (Season 2 Episode 4): Ang Kasulatang Tanso (written from Kabanata 12, version 1 of the story file; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** the Laguna Copperplate Inscription (found 1987 near the mouth of the Lumbang River, Wawa, Lumban, Laguna; dated Saka 822 = AD 900; Old Malay in Kawi script; clears the children of Namwaran of a debt of 1 kati 8 suwarna of gold; names Jayadewa, leader of Pailah, and the chief of Tundun; the oldest known dated document found in the Philippines). The tikbalang and how folklore says it is tamed; Mariang Makiling as Laguna folklore.
**Witness rule:** in the year 900 Indio, M and Usman watch from apart and speak to no real person. Jayadewa appears only as the diwa in his medallion (two short invented lines, labelled).
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M, Usman, the vehicles and the house, the medallion, the diwa's voice, Ang Kasulatan and its limit, the Punyal, the steel net and the three-triangle mark, the woman in white (never named as Makiling by the narration), the party and the man at the gate.
**The finds:** the medallion of Jayadewa, "Ang Kasulatan"; the Punyal (Sandata). **Power used on screen:** Ang Kasulatan reads a hidden clause in Indio's own contract. **Sakay and Mga Kakampi:** the tikbalang, his first living mount.

## Could not verify / kept out on purpose
- **A historical marker at the find site in Wawa, Lumban:** not verified; the script does not say "marker".
- **Medang:** disputed, spoken as disputed. **Pailah's location** (Pila, Laguna, by the Pila historians; other readings): not spoken.
- **The tikbalang:** only the WP "by one account" taming is used; "three golden hairs" and "serves for life" (popular retellings) are not. No Laguna-specific tikbalang source was found.
- **The scene of the year 900** (a family kneeling, a scribe incising) is staging; the plate records the acquittance, not the ceremony.
