## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- One real person speaks one line: Agoncillo (died 1941), the substance of his own written protest. The
  commissioners are far, generic figures with no words. No likeness.
- No living person; the "named once, kindly" rule is not used. No real hotel or shop.
- The real outcome stands. The story never says "binili" as a fact: Indio brings it as today's sabi-sabi, Usman
  growls at the word, and the trivia says it is argued.
- No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia after the last line. No follow card. Usman only in the jump.
