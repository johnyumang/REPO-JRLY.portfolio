# Facts — Episode 39 (Season 4): Ang Sugo (written from Kabanata 39, story version 2; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** Nicasio "Asiong" Salonga (11 October 1924 – 7 October 1951), "Hari ng Tondo", was a notorious gang leader of Tondo, Manila, shot dead in 1951; his life was filmed in 1961 and again later. He is only seen from across the street: no words, no invented deeds. The 1987 Constitution requires a President to be at least 40 on election day and a resident of the Philippines for at least ten years immediately before it.
**Ages and offices (SERIES.md, Season 4):** the chapter has Indio running too young; the script moves time on ("makalipas ang dalawang taon") so he is 40. No calendar year is given for the future.
**Fiction, on purpose:** the candidacy, the "Saksi" campaign, Senator Elvira Villacorta, Alcaín Builders, Vice President Urbano Ilagan, Rico Bulan, Pipay, the medallion powers. No real living person, party, candidate or company is shown or accused. The actor of the 1961 film (a real politician) is deliberately not named.
