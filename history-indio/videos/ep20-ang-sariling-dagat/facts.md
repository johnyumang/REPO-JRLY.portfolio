# Facts — Episode 20 (Season 2 Episode 12, season finale): Ang Sariling Dagat (checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:**
- On the morning of 27 April 1521, Magellan's landing party was defeated at Mactan and Magellan was killed.
- The ships could not come close because of the reef and rocks. Pigafetta, the one detailed eyewitness, counts 49 men who went ashore (11 stayed with the boats) against about 1,500 of Lapulapu's warriors.
- Lapulapu had refused to recognise Humabon's authority, the Spanish king and tribute. Humabon's warriors did not join the fight.
- The fight lasted over an hour. Magellan was wounded in the arm by a spear and in the leg by a large sword (probably a kampilan), and was killed by many warriors.
- That Lapulapu killed him with his own hand is legend.
- The Mactan Shrine (Liberty Shrine) in Lapu-Lapu City honours him.
- The siyokoy is Philippine folklore; its name comes from Hokkien *shui gui*.

**Witness rule:**
- No real person speaks.
- 1521 is seen from behind the mangroves.
- The narration says "ayon kay Pigafetta" for the numbers (sentences 16–17, 53).
- Lapulapu appears only as the diwa, with one invented line (sentence 37) under the approved datu exception, labelled in sentence 56.

**Fiction, on purpose:**
- Indio, M and Usman; Tobías Alcaín ("Tubal") and his men; Rico Bulan; the vans.
- The medallion in the reef; the siyokoy as guardians and the digging machines.
- The powers (Agos, Lihim, Kalakal, Tinig, Kalasag); the Kampilan found in the sand.
- The anting, the star, the key; the seventh mark; the woman's voice and the light from Samar.

**Find:** the medallion of Lapulapu, "Ang Kalasag"; the Kampilan; the anting; the key; the seventh mark (all fiction). **Mga Kakampi:** the siyokoy. **Hologram:** seven marks, knowledge 60%.

**Rules kept:**
- Nothing is taken from a museum or a grave (sentence 34: "sa labas ng anumang libingan").
- The sword is a story object found in the sand, not a historical artefact.
- Usman appears only inside the jump: in the light at the marker and in 1521. He vanishes on the return (sentence 27), so he is absent in the present-day fight and on the beach (sentence 58).
- The weapons wound no one (sentences 40–41).
- No flood-control thread: the digging machines disturb a reef, with no contract or official named.

## Kept out of the spoken script on purpose
- Magellan setting fire to houses, the poisoned arrow, and the recovery of his body refused for trade goods (true; cut for length).
- The poisoning of survivors at Humabon's feast (true; for a later episode).
- Datu Zula.
- The Aginid's "Lapulapu Dimantag" and its claims (the Aginid's authenticity is debated; not used).
- The local legend that Lapulapu turned to stone and guards the sea (WP; could serve a caption, labelled legend).
- Rajah Tupas's role in 1521 as Humabon's representative (WP "Treaty of Cebu (1565)"; a possible bridge to Episode 18 in captions).
