# Facts — Episode 36 (Season 4 Episode 5): Ang Paglayo (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 36)

**What is true:** After the Pact of Biak-na-Bato (signed 14–15 December 1897) Emilio Aguinaldo and about two dozen revolutionary leaders went into exile in Hong Kong (from 27 December 1897). In May 1898 the first Philippine flag was sewn by hand in Hong Kong, at 535 Morrison Hill Road, by Marcela Mariño de Agoncillo with her daughter Lorenza and Delfina Herbosa de Natividad, a niece of José Rizal; it was first displayed at Kawit, Cavite, on 12 June 1898. A children's playground (Morrison Hill Road Playground, Wan Chai) stands on the site today, with a commemorative marker put up by the Hong Kong authorities. Whether the flag now kept in Baguio is the original is disputed. In Philippine election law, "residence" means domicile, and the Supreme Court has held (Romualdez-Marcos v. COMELEC, G.R. No. 119976, 18 September 1995) that domicile, once fixed, is not lost by living elsewhere for a time while meaning to return.
