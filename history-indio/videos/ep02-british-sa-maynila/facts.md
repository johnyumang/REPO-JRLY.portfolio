# Facts — Episode 2: British sa Maynila (1762–1764) (checked 2026-11-12)

**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, his delivery job, the pancit order from "M", the medallion, the time jump, the talking dog and the second tattoo are invented. Captions say so. Indio only witnesses; nothing he does changes events.

**How this was checked:** pages could not be opened directly from this session (network), so every row rests on web-search summaries of the listed pages, cross-checked between at least two of them. Re-open the links before quoting anything word for word.

| Claim in the script | Status | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Baluarte de San Diego is a real bastion in Intramuros, breached by British cannon in 1762 | confirmed (Intramuros Administration says the British breached it in the 1762 siege; restored afterwards). The plaque's wording was not read, so the script never quotes it | https://intramuros.gov.ph/bsd/ , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baluarte_de_San_Diego |
| The British fleet reached Manila Bay in late September 1762; the city fell on 6 October ("halos dalawang linggo") | confirmed; arrival is given as 23 or 24 September depending on the source | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1762) , https://www.britishbattles.com/frederick-the-great-wars/seven-years-war/capture-of-manilla/ , https://www.kronoskaf.com/syw/index.php?title=1762_-_British_expedition_against_Manila |
| "Halos pitong libo" British force | confirmed as commonly reported: 6,839 regulars, sailors and marines | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1762) , https://www.esquiremag.ph/long-reads/notes-and-essays/the-1762-british-occupation-of-manila-part-1-a00029-20170907-lfrm2 |
| Sepoys from India were part of the force | confirmed (Madras sepoys); exact number not verified, so none is given | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Filipino , https://www.asj.upd.edu.ph/mediabox/archive/ASJ-19-1981/singhrye.pdf (1981) |
| Manila did not yet know Spain and Britain were at war | confirmed in secondary sources (defenders were caught off guard) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_occupation_of_Manila , https://www.historytoday.com/archive/british-manila-1762-1764 |
| The wall was breached at the San Diego bastion and stormed on 6 October 1762 | confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1762) , https://intramuros.gov.ph/bsd/ |
| Archbishop Manuel Rojo was acting governor-general and surrendered | confirmed. Why an archbishop held the post was not found, so the script does not explain it. A 2005 column dates the surrender 5 October; most give 6 October | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Rojo_del_R%C3%ADo_y_Vieyra , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_occupation_of_Manila |
| The city was looted; the British demanded a ransom of four million pesos | looting confirmed; ransom: most sources say 4 million Spanish dollars (pesos), one Wikipedia-derived page says 2 million. Only part was ever paid (one source: about 516,260 collected by early 1763). Script says "humingi" and flags the amount paid as unclear | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_occupation_of_Manila , https://www.britishbattles.com/frederick-the-great-wars/seven-years-war/capture-of-manilla/ |
| Simón de Anda left Manila before the fall (4 October) and set up a government in Bacolor, Pampanga | confirmed; some sources mention Bulacan first, then Bacolor | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sim%C3%B3n_de_Anda_y_Salazar , https://countrystudies.us/philippines/5.htm |
| Anda resisted with Filipino troops; the British held only Manila and Cavite | confirmed | https://museum.intramuros.gov.ph/precolonial/the-british-occupation-of-manila , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_occupation_of_Manila |
| Peace was signed in Paris in February 1763 by people who did not know Manila had been taken | confirmed (Treaty of Paris, 10 Feb 1763; the capture was unknown to the signatories) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Spanish_War_(1762%E2%80%931763) , https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/1901/january/british-conquests-philippines (1901) |
| The British left in April 1764 | confirmed (first week of April 1764); one source says May | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_occupation_of_Manila , https://www.britishbattles.com/frederick-the-great-wars/seven-years-war/capture-of-manilla/ |
| Some sepoys stayed and settled in Cainta | tradition, widely repeated; no headcount; sources differ on whether they deserted or were allowed to stay. Script says "ayon sa tradisyon" | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Filipino , https://www.asj.upd.edu.ph/mediabox/archive/ASJ-19-1981/singhrye.pdf |

## Could not verify (kept out of the script or hedged)
- Exact wording of the plaque at Baluarte de San Diego; whether an NHCP marker (not only an Intramuros Administration plaque) stands there.
- Number of sepoys; number of defenders; exact casualty figures (sources give about 150 British).
- How long the looting lasted and how much of the ransom was really paid.
- Whether troops from Pampanga attacked the British lines during the siege (not found in the pages searched).
- Why the archbishop, not a soldier, was acting governor.
- Red coats: British regulars wore red; what the sepoys and sailors wore was not checked. Drawings are generic.
