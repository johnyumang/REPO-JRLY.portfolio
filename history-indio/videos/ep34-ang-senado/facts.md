# Facts — Episode 34 (Season 4 Episode 3): Ang Senado (checked 2026-11-12; script version 1 from story version 2)

**What is true here:** the Senate and its qualifications under the 1987 Constitution (Art. VI sec. 3), the President's minimum age (Art. VII sec. 2), the opening of the Malolos Congress at Barasoain Church on 15 September 1898, the Malolos Constitution, and the kappa and sarimanok as folklore and art. Every senator, firm, budget line, notary and office in the plot is invented.

**Fixed from the story chapter (Season 4 rule):** the chapter said "makalipas ang tatlong taon" and its note said Indio was younger than 35. The script instead lets time pass: two terms as mayor, more grey hair, and he is 35 when he wins the Senate seat, which is the real minimum on election day. The rule is spoken in the setup (sentences 9 and 10) and again in the fairness beat (61).
