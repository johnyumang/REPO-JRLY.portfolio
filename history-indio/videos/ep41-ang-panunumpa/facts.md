# Facts — Episode 41 (Season 4 finale): Ang Panunumpa (written from Kabanata 41, story version 2; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** the Commonwealth of the Philippines was inaugurated on the morning of 15 November 1935 on the steps of the Legislative Building in Manila (today the National Museum of Fine Arts), before a crowd of about 300,000; Manuel L. Quezon was inaugurated as its President. Quezon is seen from far away and given no words. Scripture named, from Ang Dating Biblia (1905): Revelation 12:3 ("isang malaking dragong mapula") and 12:15 (water cast out "gaya ng isang ilog"), Genesis 9:11 (the promise after the flood) and Genesis 4:2 (Abel, "tagapagalaga ng mga tupa").
**Ages and offices:** Indio is 42, in his second year as President; no calendar year is given for the future.
**Fiction, on purpose:** the 112 arks, the marker with his name, this oath, the hologram, the shepherd's staff, the dragon of this story and its flood, Sinag, Tubal, and the family boarding. The story's flood is not taught as a lesson of faith; God is not shown and has no words; Genesis 9:11 is kept (the flood is the dragon's).
