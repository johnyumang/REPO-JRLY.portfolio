# Facts — Episode 11 (Season 2 Episode 3): Ang Sugo ng Butuan (checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** the balangay boats of Butuan (eleven known, dated 689–988 CE, the first nine found in 1976), Butuan's missions to the Song court (1001–1011; the 1007 request for Champa's rank; the 1011 gold-tablet memorial), and the gold statuette called the Golden Tara (found 1917 in Esperanza, Agusan del Sur; now in Chicago; identity disputed).
**Witness rule:** Indio, M and Usman watch the port and speak to no real person. The court in China is never shown as a visited place: it is told in narration from the records. Rajah Kiling appears only as the diwa in his medallion, with two invented lines (sentences 32 and 37) under the first approved exception, labelled in sentence 64, with his real record in sentence 63.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M, Usman, the medallions and their fates, the diwa's voice, Ang Sugo and its limit (he understands every language but cannot speak it), the old boat where the medallion was hidden, the fisherman, the shadow with no owner, the hologram (12% knowledge, the sign at the end of the bar), the notebook's clue.
**The find:** the medallion of Rajah Kiling, "Ang Sugo". **Power used on screen:** Indio understands the diwa (sentence 31) and the fisherman's warning, which saves them (sentences 42–45).

## Could not verify / kept out on purpose
- **The outcome of the 1007 request** (refused or granted): sources disagree; spoken as disputed.
- **Kiling's dates:** the Song list gives him 1001–1009; his death is not recorded. The script says only "pagkatapos ng panahon niya" (fiction).
- **The departure scene in 1011:** the record is the mission's arrival at court; the ships leaving Butuan are staging.
- **The balangay seen at the port** are of the type found at Butuan; the excavated boats are older (689–988), which fits a port still using such boats in 1011, but no source describes the port in 1011.
- **"320 AD"** (the Balangay Shrine's old dating) is outdated; not used.
- **Golden Tara:** whether it is a Tara is disputed; it was found in Agusan del Sur, not Butuan city; not said to be made in Butuan.
- **Clue for Ep 12:** the Simunul mosque's 1380 founding is tradition; the present building is from the 1960s with 17th-century pillars (research.md version 3 block).
