# Facts — Episode 11 (Season 2 Episode 3): Ang Sugo ng Butuan — version 2 (Season 2 version 4 rules and master plot version 2), checked 2026-11-12

**What is true:** the balangay boats of Butuan (eleven known, 689–988 CE), Butuan's missions to the Song court (the 1007 request for Champa's rank; the 1011 gold-tablet memorial), Rajah Kiling as named in the Song record, that the Yúmud is a water spirit in Agusan Manobo folklore (Garvan, 1931), and that flood-control projects paid for but not built were investigated in 2025 (named only in general).
**Witness rule:** Indio, M and Usman speak to no real person. The Song court is told in narration and seen only through the lens. Rajah Kiling appears only as the diwa, with two invented lines (sentences 35 and 46) under approved exception 1, labelled in sentence 62, his real record in sentence 59.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M, Usman, the motorcycle, the wish, the money and the vehicles, the stranger, the signboard and its project (no real project, contractor, office or person is shown or named), the medallions and their fates, the diwa's voice, Ang Sugo and its limit, the Yúmud's words and the pact, the hologram.
**Usman and the present:** the series rule (Usman exists only during a jump and in the past) is kept: the sidecar stays empty in the present with his kerchief tied to it, and his growl at the signboard comes from inside the jump light.
**The find:** the medallion of Rajah Kiling, "Ang Sugo". **Power used on screen:** Indio understands the diwa and the Yúmud and wins without fighting. **Mga Kakampi:** the Yúmud ("nakahahanap ng nawala sa tubig"). **Sakay:** the second-hand motorcycle with a sidecar.

## True, kept for captions and pictures but no longer spoken (cut for length)
- Nine of the boats were first found in 1976 near the Masao River (WP "Balangay").
- The Golden Tara: a 21-karat gold statuette, 9th–10th century, found in 1917 near Esperanza, Agusan del Sur; now in the Field Museum, Chicago; identity disputed (WP "Agusan image").

## Could not verify / kept out on purpose
- **The Yúmud's description** rests on two search summaries quoting Garvan (1931); its look is the show's design.
- **No Butuan-specific bakunawa, sigbin or busaw** was found; not used.
- **The 2025 investigations:** named only in general; no names of people, companies, offices or places from them are used anywhere in the episode.
- **The outcome of the 1007 request:** disputed.
- **Kiling's dates:** the Song list gives 1001–1009; "pagkatapos ng panahon niya" is fiction.
- **The 1011 departure scene** is staging.
- **The Surigao ferry route** is plausible travel staging, not a claim.
