# Facts — Episode 11 (Season 2 Episode 3): Ang Sugo ng Butuan — version 4 rules (checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** the balangay boats of Butuan (eleven known, 689–988 CE, the first nine found in 1976 near the Masao River), Butuan's missions to the Song court (1007 request for Champa's rank, 1011 gold-tablet memorial), the Golden Tara (found 1917 in Esperanza, Agusan del Sur; in Chicago; identity disputed), and that the Yúmud is a water spirit in Agusan Manobo folklore (recorded by Garvan, 1931).
**Witness rule:** Indio, M and Usman speak to no real person. The Song court is told in narration and shown only through the lens. Rajah Kiling appears only as the diwa in his medallion, with two invented lines (sentences 32 and 43) under approved exception 1, labelled in sentence 63, his real record in sentence 61.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M, Usman, the first wish and its money, the medallions and their fates, the diwa's voice, Ang Sugo and its limit, the Yúmud's words and the pact, the shadow with no owner, the hologram, the notebook's clue. The Yúmud is labelled "kuwentong-bayan" in sentence 62.
**The find:** the medallion of Rajah Kiling, "Ang Sugo". **Power used on screen:** Indio understands the diwa and the Yúmud (sentences 35–37), learns it is a guardian, and wins without fighting. **Mga Kakampi:** the Yúmud, "nakahahanap ng nawala sa ilalim ng tubig".

## Could not verify / kept out on purpose
- **The Yúmud's description** comes from two search summaries quoting Garvan (1931); the Gutenberg text was blocked from this machine. The script uses only what the quote says (a water spirit of deep pools, harmless). Its look (moss hair, ember eyes) is the show's design, not folklore.
- **No Butuan-specific bakunawa, sigbin or busaw** was found in sources; not used. A real Agusan river custom, the "abayan" ritual against crocodiles (Inquirer, late-17th-century origin [summary only]), was considered and left out.
- **The outcome of the 1007 request:** disputed; spoken as disputed.
- **Kiling's dates:** the Song list gives 1001–1009; his death is not recorded ("pagkatapos ng panahon niya" is fiction).
- **The 1011 departure scene** is staging; the record is the arrival at court.
- **A historical marker at the Balangay Shrine:** not checked; the script says only "ang nakasulat sa tabi ng bangka".
- **Golden Tara:** found in Agusan del Sur, not Butuan city; not said to be made in Butuan.
