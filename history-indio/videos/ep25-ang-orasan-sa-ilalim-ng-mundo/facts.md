# Facts — Episode 25 (Season 3 Episode 5): Ang Orasan sa Ilalim ng Mundo (checked 2026-11-12; script version 1 from story version 2)

**What is true here is a history of an idea, not a place.** "Agartha" was invented in France in 1873 and put underground in 1886. It is not from Himalayan tradition. The yeti is Himalayan folklore; the samples tested were bears and a dog.

**Imagery rule (SERIES.md, research §5):** the modern Agartha meme has been taken up by neo-Nazi and "Aryan" movements. This episode uses no Nordic "master race" figures, black suns, "vril" or swastika-like symbols, and does not name "Vril". Albo is never called "Nordic" and is never drawn as a human racial type.
