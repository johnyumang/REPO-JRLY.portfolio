# Facts — Episode 46 (Season 5 Episode 5): Ang Mga Pinili (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 46)

**What is true:** the texts the episode points to say what the fairness beat says: Genesis 4:17 (Cain builds a city), Genesis 4:22 (Tubal-cain, a worker in bronze and iron), Genesis 3:24 (cherubim and a flaming sword that turns every way, set to keep the way of the tree of life; the text does not say a cherub holds it), Revelation 12:7 (Michael and his angels). The Watchers come from 1 Enoch, which most Bibles do not include (it is canonical in the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church). The Laguna Copperplate Inscription (found 1987 at Wawa, Lumban, Laguna; dated Saka 822 = AD 900) clears a family (the children of Namwaran) of a debt and names Jayadewa, leader of Pailah.
**Witness rule:** no real historical person appears. Scripture is not quoted: the script only says what a passage contains, with the book named on screen and the reference in the captions; God is given no words.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio (in his forties, President: see SERIES "Season 4: ages, offices and the law"), M (Mikaela), Usman, Tala, Albo, Sinag, Tubal (Tobías Alcaín), Kapitan Totoy, the arks, the clay tablet of Babel in the keel, the depths, the medallions and their diwa, the Trinity (hilt = the handle of M's umbrella; star = the guard; blade = the shard from the lowest depth), the invented dikes and contracts. No real living person, firm, office-holder or party is meant.
**Diwa line (approved exception, labelled):** JAYADEWA: "Basahin mo nang malakas, dahil hindi nakikipagtawaran ang nakasulat." No words of Jayadewa are recorded; the real record is what the copperplate says of him.
**Indio never kills and takes no revenge:** he reads the record aloud, does not strike, does not draw the Kampilan, and leaves Tubal a place in the arks.
**Label for captions (Season 3–5 rule):** "Ang tagpo ay ayon sa salaysay ng serye; kathang-isip ang mga tauhan. Ang Genesis at Pahayag ay binanggit ayon sa teksto; ang mga Bantay ay mula sa 1 Enoc."
**Find:** the whole Trinity. **Mga Kakampi:** Albo; Tala; Tubal's men. **Knowledge:** 100% (marks 7, all open).
