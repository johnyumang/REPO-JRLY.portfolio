# Facts — Episode 48 (Season 5 Episode 7): Ang Saksi (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 48)

**What is true:** the Battle of Manila Bay was fought on 1 May 1898: the US Asiatic Squadron under Commodore George Dewey destroyed the Spanish squadron of Admiral Patricio Montojo. In the Treaty of Paris (10 December 1898) Spain ceded the Philippines to the United States with no Filipino at the table; whether the US$20 million was a "sale" is argued (see Episode 5's facts). José Rizal was shot at Bagumbayan on 30 December 1896 (Episode 6's facts). "Lucifer" is the word of the Latin Vulgate at Isaiah 14:12 ("morning star"), a verse addressed to the king of Babylon; later tradition joined it to the story of a fallen angel. The war in heaven, Michael and his angels against the dragon, is in Revelation 12:7–9. The popular picture of the rebel angel owes much to John Milton's *Paradise Lost* (1667). The phrase "father of lies" comes from John 8:44. The Greek *martys* means "witness", the root of "martyr".
**Fiction, on purpose:** the war in the Philippines, the figure called Lucifer standing on the bay and every word he says (the series' own; he is not a historical person), the dragon's flood, the Bakunawa as a mount, the Trinity, the star, the shepherd's staff, the twelve datu and their anting, the Kakampi, the arks, the "ka-Indio" army, Usman's growl as a lie-detector, M and Miguel's host, Giliw, Lola Ising, Inay (Liwayway), and Indio himself (President in this season; an invented office-holder).
**Restraint:** Indio never kills and takes no revenge; the Trinity cuts only lies, never a body; the seven marks show light, not wounds; nothing gory. God is given no words; the light behind Miguel's host does not speak. Scripture: three verses are quoted word for word from Ang Dating Biblia 1905 (Isaias 14:12, part; Pahayag 12:7, part; Juan 8:44, last clause), checked 2026-11-12 against the text files of github.com/nehemiaharchives/bbl (resources/bbltexts/abtag). The captions' "ama ng kasinungalingan" is a summary, not the 1905 wording.
**Speakers:** INDIO, LUCIFER (new designed voice: calm, reasonable, warm, never a monster voice), USMAN, M, LOLA. INAY appears without words.
**Label for captions (Season 5):** "Ang tagpo ay kathang-isip ng serye; ang mga sanggunian ay ayon sa Bibliya (Isaias 14:12 sa saling Latin, Pahayag 12:7–9, Juan 8:44) at kay John Milton; totoo ang kasaysayang binanggit (Look ng Maynila 1898, Paris 1898, Bagumbayan 1896)."
