# Facts — Episode 8 (version 2): Ang Huling Tala (Kawit, 12 Hunyo 1898, mula sa likod)

Built on `videos/ep08-ang-huling-tala/facts.md` (checked 2026-11-12; Wikipedia articles read in full, other
pages as summaries) and `datu_notes.md`. New in version 2 (web search, 15 Nov 2026, summaries only): the word
"sinigang".

## 1. What is real in this script, and where it is said

| Line (sentences.json) | Claim | Status and sources |
|---|---|---|
| s14 to s15 (INDIO) | "Sinigang: galing sa salitang 'sigang', ibig sabihin, pakuluan. Atin 'yan mula pa noong una." | The word: Wikipedia, "Sinigang" (a nominalised form of the Tagalog verb sigang, "to stew"); Wiktionary, "sinigang" (sigang + -in-). That the dish is native and older than Spanish rule is the common account (Wikipedia: "culturally Tagalog in origin"); no primary evidence was seen, and one writer derives the word differently. Said as Indio's pride, not as a dated claim |
| s5 to s7, s22 to s24 | Cavite, June 1898; Kawit, 12 June 1898, afternoon | Confirmed (row A1: between four and five in the afternoon; A2: a Sunday) |
| s30 to s32 (ALING PINING), stage line | Apolinario Mabini, paralysed in the legs, was carried to Kawit in a hammock | Confirmed for the hammock and the relays of bearers (Wikipedia, Apolinario Mabini). That he arrived on the 12th itself rests on Ocampo (row A10: weak on the hour), so nothing is said about when he arrived. He was 33 (born 23 July 1864) |
| s35 to s36 (MABINI) | "Maaga pa ito. At padalos-dalos." | A RENDERING, not a quotation: in his memoir La Revolución Filipina he judged the proclamation premature and imprudent, because the Americans were hiding their intentions while the Filipinos revealed theirs (row A11, via Wikipedia and Ocampo). INVENTED: that he said so aloud in the yard that afternoon. The trivia says "diwa ito ng isinulat niya", not a quote |
| s33 to s34 against s35 to s37 | "Sabi sa libro, buong kalayaan ... lahat masaya" against Mabini's face | The episode's sabi-sabi. The Acta proclaims independence "under the protection of the Mighty and Humane North American Nation" (row A5: certain that the clause exists); that is in the trivia, not in the scene |
| s76 (DIWA), s78 to s79 | Rajah Sulayman; "mahigit tatlong daang taon" | Rajah Sulayman of Maynila resisted the Spaniards in 1570 to 1571 (datu_notes.md; Wikipedia, Rajah Sulayman). 1570 to 1898 is 328 years. The voice in the bronze is the owner's approved exception: one invented line, about nothing outside his own time |
| Trivia | Mabini and the hammock; his written judgment, as a rendering; the protection clause of the Acta; Paris six months later; Sulayman as a real ruler | Rows A5, A10, A11; Episode 5 facts; datu_notes.md |

Deliberately NOT repeated from Episode 3: the window, the flag, the band, Bautista, Aguinaldo, Hilaria, the 98
signers. Dropped from the first version of Episode 8: L. M. Johnson, Dewey, Delfina Herbosa (true and checked;
too many for 165 seconds; Delfina belongs to Episode 36).

## 3. Guard rails checked for this script

- Mabini: one line, a rendering of his own written view, marked as such; generic figure, no likeness; shown
  with dignity (a thin man in a hammock with sharp eyes; "lumpo ang paa" is said by a baker, without pity).
  He had no wife: no tender beat applies.
- The diwa: the approved exception; no likeness of the real Sulayman.
- Indio speaks to no real person and changes nothing; the story does not confirm any bloodline (the child's
  eyes are "ang mga mata ng Lolo niya": the Lolo is invented).
- No living person. No sentence says anything is invented. Trivia at the end. No follow card.
