**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 7 (v2), and above all not Episode 3**, which is the same afternoon. Episode
3 was the FRONT: the window, the flag, the drum, the vendor's pointing finger, open hands pushing him out, the
crowd parting. Episode 8 is the BACK: backs of heads, bread trays, a hammock, the lens. The window and the flag
are never shown here, only heard as a far cheer. The earlier Indio appears ONLY inside the lens circle, small.

(Ep 7). This episode's colour is flour-white, bread-brown and the red of one umbrella at the very end.
