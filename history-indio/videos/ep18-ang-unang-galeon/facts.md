# Facts — Episode 18 (Season 2 Episode 10): Ang Unang Galeon (checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:**
- The *San Pedro*, guided by Andrés de Urdaneta, found the return route from the islands to New Spain (the tornaviaje) and reached Acapulco on 8 October 1565.
- This opened the Manila–Acapulco galleon trade, 1565–1815.
- Rajah Tupas was the last rajah of Sugbu. He signed the Treaty of Cebu in June 1565, was baptized in 1568 and died that year.
- The nahual is Mesoamerican folklore.

**Witness rule:**
- Indio, M and Usman speak to no real person.
- The 1565 arrival is seen only through the lens. Cebu and the treaty are told in narration, not visited.
- Rajah Tupas appears only as the diwa, with two invented lines (sentences 40 and 52) under the approved datu exception. They are labelled in sentence 61, with his real record in sentences 25–28 and 58–59.

**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M and Usman; the bread at the pier; the jump into an unnamed galleon-era night; the cook who carried the medallion; the wall of the old warehouse; the medallion and its design (a table and a sun); the diwa's voice and lines; "Ang Piging" and its limit; the nahual's hunger and the pact; the children under the bridge; the hologram; the toy galleon and the note.

**Real record of the datu (said in the episode):** Tupas never went to Acapulco. His tie to the episode is that the first return voyage sailed from his Sugbu in 1565, the year of his treaty.

**Find:** the medallion of Rajah Tupas, "Ang Piging" (fiction). **Power used on screen:** the shared bread multiplies; a double of what one keeps fails; the doubles are gone by dawn. **Earlier power reused:** Kolambu's "Ang Kalakal" (sees what the other truly wants). **Mga Kakampi:** the nahual. **Hologram:** six marks, knowledge 47%.

**Rules kept:**
- Nothing is taken from a museum or a grave: the medallion is in the wall of an old warehouse, in the past.
- Usman appears only inside the jump: he arrives in the light at the marker and vanishes on the return.
- No flood-control thread.
