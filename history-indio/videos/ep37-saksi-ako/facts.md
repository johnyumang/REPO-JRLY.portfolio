# Facts — Episode 37 (Season 4 Episode 6): Saksi Ako (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 37)

**What is true:** The Laguna Copperplate Inscription is dated Saka 822 (about 21 April 900 CE) and is the earliest known calendar-dated document found in the Philippines; it is an acquittance that releases the children (descendants) of Namwaran from a debt in gold of 1 kati and 8 suwarna, naming Lady Angkatan and Bukah and the lord minister of Pailah, Jayadewa. It was found near the mouth of the Lumbang River, Lumban, Laguna (1989 in most accounts, 1987 in some), was first read by the Dutch scholar Antoon Postma (presented 1991), and is kept at the National Museum of the Philippines. Later translators differ on some words. Where the plate was handed over in 900 is not known. Under the 1987 Constitution (Art. XI sec. 17) and R.A. 6713 (sec. 8), public officials file a sworn Statement of Assets, Liabilities and Net Worth (SALN) on taking office and every year after. PAGASA is the national weather agency (an office; the storm is invented).
**Label for captions (Season 4 rule):** "Totoo ang Kasulatang Tanso ng Laguna (taong 900) at ang tungkulin ng SALN. Kathang-isip ang pagdinig, ang komite, ang kumpanya, ang mga opisyal, ang hukom at ang lahat ng tauhan; walang totoong tao ang inaakusahan."
