# Facts — Episode 8: Ang Huling Tala (Kawit, 12 June 1898, seen "from the other side"; the 529 days before it; what became of it)

**Fiction, on purpose (not researched here; listed so the fairness beat can name it):** Indio (José Indalecio Bracken), Usman, the medallions, M, the toddler and his family, the lens, the red umbrella, "ang Huling Tala" (star medallion and kasulatan/map), the twelve datus, "Sila", the earlier Indio and second Usman in the crowd, Indio's job during the wait. The one labelled datu line is fiction; his real record must come from `datu_notes.md` (not part of this file).

**Real people in this episode:** Aguinaldo, Ambrosio Rianzares Bautista, Mabini, "Col." L. M. Johnson, the band, the crowd; in flashback/timeline only: Marcela and Lorenza Agoncillo, Delfina Herbosa de Natividad, Bonifacio, Dewey, Julián Felipe. Indio only watches them. No invented words. The only real words that may be spoken are quoted or paraphrased from the Acta itself (section A5/A6) and Mabini's documented view (A9), and they must be framed as "nakasulat sa Akta" / "sinulat ni Mabini", never as dialogue Indio hears.
