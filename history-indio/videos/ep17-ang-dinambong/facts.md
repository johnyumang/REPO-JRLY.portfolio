# Facts — Episode 17 (Season 2 Episode 9): Ang Dinambong — version 1 (written 2026-11-12, to be checked against story version 2 before frames)

**What is true:** the British took Manila in October 1762 and demanded a ransom of four million pesos (how much was ever paid is unclear; one estimate is about a quarter); they took the galleons *Filipina* (silver from Acapulco) and *Santísima Trinidad* as prizes; Augustinian manuscripts, the copper plates of Murillo Velarde's 1734 map and grammars of Philippine languages went to Britain, and some are now in the British Library and at King's College London. José Rizal was in London from May 1888 to March 1889 and worked at the British Museum on Antonio de Morga's *Sucesos de las Islas Filipinas* (Mexico, 1609), which he published with his own notes in Paris in 1890. Lakandula of Tondo welcomed Legazpi in 1571 and helped defend Manila against Limahong in 1574. Spring-heeled Jack is a London legend first reported in 1837.
**Witness rule:** Rizal is seen only from far behind the shelves, bent over a book: no words, no thoughts, no contact. The "Lolo Pepe" line is family lore under the second approved exception, labelled in the fairness beat. Lakandula's two lines are the diwa exception, labelled.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M, Usman, the notebook's clue, the crossing, the crate and the medallion inside it, the jump to 1888 on M's medallion, the bow of light, Jack as a creature recorded in the hologram, Rico Bulan, Tubal's men and their mark, the hotel scene.
**Usman:** only inside the jump to 1888 (sentences 21–41); he fades when the jump ends; in the present Indio "growls" in his place.
**The find:** the medallion of Lakandula, "Ang Agos" (speed along something that already flows; it cannot stop at once). **Power used on screen:** escape along the rain water running to the river. **Mga Kakampi:** Spring-heeled Jack. **Knowledge:** 40% (ebook spine v1).
**The medallion is not taken from any collection:** it lies in an uncatalogued crate of a country house's effects in a riverside store (fiction), and the script says "wala sa anumang katalogo".
**Traps kept (season2/research.md):** no church bells; the 1762 loot is NOT said to be in the British Museum (British Library and King's College London only); the British Museum appears only as the place where Rizal studied.
**Sources:** season2/research.md "Version 4" London block and "Lolo Pepe" block, `videos/ep08-ang-huling-tala/datu_notes.md` §2 (Lakandula), and the creature table. Items marked [summary only] were seen as search summaries, not read: re-open before quoting on screen.
