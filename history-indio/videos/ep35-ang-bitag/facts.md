# Facts — Episode 35 (Season 4 Episode 4): Ang Bitag (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 35)

**What is true:** Macario Sakay fought on against American rule after the United States declared the Philippine-American War over (4 July 1902); he and his men came down and surrendered in 1906 on a promise of amnesty carried by Dominador Gómez, and were arrested soon after; he was convicted of *bandolerismo* under the Brigandage Act of 1902 (Act No. 518), a law that treated armed resistance as banditry, and was hanged at Bilibid Prison, Manila, on 13 September 1907. Sources disagree on his year of birth (1870 or about 1878) and on the exact date of the surrender (June or July 1906). The 1987 Constitution (Art. VI, sec. 3) requires a senator to be at least 35 years old on the day of the election. In 2025 the Philippines lived through Senate and executive investigations into flood-control projects, including alleged "ghost" projects; the episode names them only in general.
**Label for captions (Season 4 rule):** "Totoo ang kasaysayan ni Macario Sakay at ang mga imbestigasyon sa flood control noong 2025 (binabanggit lang nang pangkalahatan). Kathang-isip ang bitag, ang kumpanya, ang mga tauhan, ang partido at ang pagiging senador ni Indio."
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, Usman, M (Mikaela), Bea Marasigan, JM Robles, Ma'am Celia Abad, Rico Bulan and his vlog "Ang Totoong Kasaysayan", Sinag and the bottle, the Hiraya Diking Corporation, its paper president Jovy Ladrillo, the forged contract and deed of sale, the impound, the subpoena, Indio's party (unnamed), Indio's seat in the Senate, the medallions (Ang Tinig, Ang Kasulatan), the hologram. **No real living person, party, company, project or senator appears.** The scheme Indio is framed in is new and invented; it is not modelled on any real case or person.
**Names checked (web search, 2026-11-12, summary only):** "Hiraya Diking", "Jovy Ladrillo", "Rico Bulan": no public figure or firm found with these names; no contractor named "Hiraya" found in the DPWH results of the search.
**Age (SERIES.md, Season 4 rules):** Indio was born about 2001 (Grade 5 in 2012, Episode 10); he is 35 in this episode, his first year in the Senate. No calendar year is given for the present.
