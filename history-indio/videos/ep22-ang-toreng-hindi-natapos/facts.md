# Facts — Episode 22 (Season 3 Episode 2): Ang Toreng Hindi Natapos (written from Kabanata 22, story version 2; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** the Tower of Babel is told in Genesis 11:1–9 (scripture, quoted exactly from Ang Dating Biblia 1905 and never mocked; God is given no words). Scholars link it to Etemenanki, the ziggurat of Marduk in Babylon, whose known form was rebuilt under Nebuchadnezzar II in the 6th century BC; archaeology has not found "the" tower of Genesis. The idea that the tower was built against a new flood is from Josephus (Antiquities 1.4.2, about AD 94), not from Genesis.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Tay Sebyo, the balangay as a vessel of light, M's promise, the hairy giant builders and their kinship with Tala, the man with the three-triangle seal, the Ladrilyo ng Babel and its power, the diwa's line, the notebook. Usman appears only in the light of the lagusan and inside the jump.
**Exact quotations:** Genesis 11:1 (whole) and 11:4 (first half). Sentence 22 is Indio's paraphrase of 11:4, given as what he hears.
