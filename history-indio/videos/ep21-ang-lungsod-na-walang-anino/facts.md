# Facts — Episode 21 (Season 3 Episode 1): Ang Lungsod na Walang Anino (written from Kabanata 21, story version 2; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** Biringan is a legend of Samar: an invisible, shining city of engkanto, said in the tale to lie near Gandara and Pagsanghan, with seven portals across Samar (one said to be at an old tree at Northwest Samar State University in Calbayog); the stories spread from the 1960s, and none of it is backed by a map or record. It is told here as the belief of the people of Samar, respected as such, not as proof.
**Note on place:** Kabanata 21 puts the fishing village "between Calbayog and Catarman". That village and its people are fiction; the legend's location (near Gandara and Pagsanghan, per WP) is what the fairness beat states.
**Fiction, on purpose:** every character (Tala, Liwayway, Teddy Bracken, Nanay Pacing, Totoy, JM), the key and the lagusan, the hologram and its counter, the city as Indio sees it, the notebook clue. Usman appears only in the light of the open lagusan, as Kabanata 21 sets out.
**No real person is given words. No scripture in this episode.**
