# Shots — Episode 7 (version 2)

Revised for rule 9 (the storyteller tells every action in a full sentence): shots that were silent now carry a storyteller line; the pictures themselves do not change.

One row per shot, in order. "Sent." = index in `sentences.json` (111 sentences). All stills NEW in the Bakunawa
look; writing in pictures is added in code. Nobody just standing; never the same framing twice in a row.

**Must not resemble Episodes 1 to 6 (v2).** No crowd at all (Ep 1, 3, 6). The phone here is a dying light in a
hand, never a list of app screens (Ep 2). No gate with bars (Ep 4): the cemetery is seen over a wall from a
tree. No window watched from below (Ep 5): what he watches is inside a lens. The field of Ep 6 appears once,
empty, at noon. This episode's world is hard noon light, a curved stone wall, a tree, dug earth, and the
gold-rimmed circle of the lens (everything seen through it is tinted warm and slightly curved).
