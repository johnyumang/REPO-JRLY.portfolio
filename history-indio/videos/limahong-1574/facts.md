# Facts — Ang Piratang Muntik Nang Maging Hari ng Maynila (checked 2026-11-11)
| Claim | Status | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Limahong (Lin Feng in Chinese records) was a Chinese pirate warlord hunted by Ming authorities | confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong , https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/?p=1127939 , https://archium.ateneo.edu/phstudies/vol67/iss3/3 |
| Spanish Manila was only about three years old (founded 1571) when he attacked in 1574 | confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intramuros |
| His fleet appeared off Manila in late November 1574 | confirmed (landing dated 29 or 30 Nov depending on source) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://www.herodote.net/29_novembre_1574-evenement-15741129.php |
| "More than sixty ships" | disputed in detail: 62, 62–70 or 60–72 junks; crew 2,000–6,500 depending on source. Script says "mahigit animnapu" and flags the numbers as uncertain | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://mandirigma.org/?p=366 |
| The fleet also carried families, farmers and carpenters/artisans (colonists) | reported (Wikipedia battle article; a 19th-century account says women and artisans were aboard to form a settlement) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/12635/pg12635.html |
| His men landed near Parañaque and marched on Manila | confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mart%C3%ADn_de_Goiti |
| The first attack was led by his Japanese lieutenant Sioco | confirmed in secondary sources | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://mandirigma.org/?p=366 |
| Goiti's house was attacked and burned; Martín de Goiti (maestre de campo) was killed | confirmed (one page gives his death year as 1575; most give 1574) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mart%C3%ADn_de_Goiti , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://es.hispanopedia.com/wiki/Batalla_de_Manila_(1574) |
| The first assault was driven back | confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://mandirigma.org/?p=366 |
| Governor was Guido de Lavezaris (elderly) | confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong |
| Manila had few soldiers and only wooden (log palisade) defences; stone walls came about 1590 | confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intramuros , https://museum.intramuros.gov.ph/?p=718 , https://www.pressreader.com/philippines/manila-bulletin/20200608/281891595505685 |
| The pirates rested about two days before the second attack | confirmed in secondary sources | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://www.herodote.net/29_novembre_1574-evenement-15741129.php |
| Juan de Salcedo arrived from Ilocos (Vigan) with reinforcements before the second attack | confirmed; size of his force disputed (50 arquebusiers; 200 or 300 Ilocano warriors) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong |
| Second attack on 2 December with the whole fleet; Sioco attacked again | confirmed (fleet sighted night of 2 Dec; fighting may have run into 3 Dec) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://es.hispanopedia.com/wiki/Batalla_de_Manila_(1574) |
| Native warriors fought beside the Spaniards | reported (Ilocano warriors with Salcedo; native allies) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://archium.ateneo.edu/phstudies/vol67/iss3/3 |
| Sioco was killed in the battle | reported (one account: shot by a Spanish marksman; infobox marks him killed) — manner of death not confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://es.hispanopedia.com/wiki/Batalla_de_Manila_(1574) |
| Limahong withdrew and built a fort in Pangasinan near Lingayen (Agno River delta) | confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong , https://www.ourpangasinan.com/?p=786 |
| In 1575 Salcedo followed and besieged him for about four months | confirmed for the siege (arrived 30 Mar, escape 4 Aug 1575); one source says seven months | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangasinan |
| A Ming officer (Wang Wanggao, "Omoncon" in Spanish sources) arrived to check on Limahong | confirmed | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong , https://archium.ateneo.edu/phstudies/vol67/iss3/3 |
| In August 1575 he escaped through a channel he had dug to the sea | confirmed escape; the channel's exact location is disputed (Agno delta / Domalandan, Lingayen), so the script says "raw" | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong , https://vigattintourism.com/tourism/articles/Limahong-Channel-Lingayens-History , https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/?p=1127939 |
| After that he almost vanishes from the record | confirmed that his end is unknown; later traces differ (Guangdong/Taiwan; a 1589 mention) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong , https://www.ourpangasinan.com/?p=786 |
| He wanted to be "king" of Manila | disputed: some sources say he meant to found a kingdom, others only a settlement; one historian (Ang See, as cited on Wikipedia) suggests he first sought refuge. The title and hook say "muntik", and the fairness beat says this is debated | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_(1574) , https://mandirigma.org/?p=366 , https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/12635/pg12635.html |
| Most details come from Spanish accounts | commentary; Ming records exist too but are thinner on the Manila fighting | https://archium.ateneo.edu/phstudies/vol67/iss3/3 , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limahong |

Not verified: research was from search summaries only; the pages themselves and the primary sources (Blair & Robertson vol. 4, the Ming records, Shutz 2019) were not read in full. Many hits were Wikipedia or copies of it, so several "two sources" rows are not truly independent. Not settled: ship and crew numbers; exact dates (29 vs 30 November; 2 vs 3 December); how Sioco died; size of Salcedo's relief force; siege length (four vs seven months); location of the escape channel; whether Limahong aimed at a kingdom or a settlement; his fate after 1575.

Left out of the script on purpose (single-source or legend): Goiti's wife Lucía del Corral taunting the pirates from the window and surviving; Goiti ignoring the first warning; Goiti being ill; the "Galo" of Parañaque story; the burning of the city/San Agustin church. No quotes are used in the script.
