# Facts — Episode 4: Ang Aso ni Pepe (Usman's back story; Calamba, June 1869) (checked 2026-11-12)

**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, the medallion, the time jump, the talking dog, the tattoos and "M" are invented. **Also fiction: that Indio's Usman is the same dog as the boy Rizal's Usman, and the farewell at the gate** (no source describes how the boy said goodbye to his dog). The script says so in its fairness beat and the captions repeat it. Indio only watches from outside the yard; he never speaks to or touches a real person, and no words are put in a real person's mouth.

**How this was checked:** pages could not be opened directly from this session, so each row rests on web-search summaries of the listed pages, cross-checked. The primary text (Rizal's "Memorias de un estudiante de Manila") was NOT read; the script therefore says "ayon sa mga talambuhay niya", not "isinulat niya".
