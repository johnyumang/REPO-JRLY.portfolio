# Facts — Episode 7: Ang Sulat ni Lolo (Paco, 30 December 1896 to August 1898) (checked 2026-11-12)

**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, his full name José Indalecio Bracken (proposed name, Canon v2), Usman (the talking dog), "M", the Lolo, his five jumps and five marks, the letter and the Lola's photo of it, the box's lining, the lens ("ang lente") and its power, the Lolo talking to the medallion, the bedtime story of twelve datus asleep in bronze (the souls in the medallions are fiction; under Canon v2 each medallion datu is a real person, named with his record when he appears), "ang Huling Tala", and Indio being stranded. Also fiction (series premise): that Indio carries Rizal's blood through the son of José Rizal and Josephine Bracken. Real record: that son (named Francisco in most accounts; some of Rizal's sisters said Peter) was born prematurely in Dapitan (late 1895 in some accounts) and died within hours or was stillborn; Rizal has no known surviving descendants (see the "walang kilalang inapo" row below).

**Real people:** Rizal is shown only as an unmarked grave seen from far away; Narcisa Rizal only from behind and far away, through the lens. Neither gets a word, a thought or an action that is not in the record below. The only quoted lines are the Lolo's letter (fiction). No body, no blood, no burial shown up close.

**Witness rule:** the lens only shows; Indio and Usman watch from a distance and change nothing.

**How this was checked:** en.wikipedia.org pages were opened (raw) on 2026-11-12; inquirer.net, joserizal.com, atlasobscura.com, geronimocristobal.com, philstar.com and intramuros.gov.ph were seen only as web-search summaries, marked [summary only]. ## Could not verify (kept out of the script or hedged)
- The vehicle: only Craig 1913 names it (a wagon); single source, so it is scenery (spoken "karwahe", drawn as a plain horse-drawn wagon). The hour of the burial and the number of guards: never stated.
- How many days Narcisa searched (two and four are both given): the script says no number ("bumilis ang mga araw").
- Who placed the RPJ marker (Narcisa through the caretaker, or the family's friends) and whether anyone was paid: listed as disputed; the script names no one.
- The wording of the Paco Park marker: never quoted.
- The paper in the shoe: listed as weak.
- That the remains went to Narcisa's house in Binondo and to the Rizal Monument in December 1912: true per several sources, but left out of the script for length.
- Continuity checked against Episodes 1–6: Ep 3 (the empty box that fits the medallion; Kawit 12 June 1898; the woman carrying the child with the medallion), Ep 4 (Lola: Lolo left "one word, Calamba"; at the end she has read "ang lumang sulat ni Lolo": the letter was under the box's lining, so she found it after), Ep 5 (five tattoos, M's five fingers), Ep 6 (stranded at Bagumbayan, medallion taken, Usman stays). Indio has five tattoos and gets none here. Ep 3 gave Indio the empty box; this episode says he left it with the Lola ("kahong iniwan ko kay Lola"), so she could find the letter under its lining. The Lolo made five jumps and met M on his fifth; Indio reached this morning on his sixth (unfinished) jump, so the letter no longer says "rin".
