# Facts — Episode 50 (Season 5 closing): Ang Dulo (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, "Ang Dulo"; about 2 minutes, made as its own episode because Kabanata 49 and "Ang Dulo" do not fit in one 5-minute video)

**What is true:** Abel was a keeper of sheep (Genesis 4:2). The Bible names no child of Abel: Cain killed him, and Seth was given "in place of Abel" (Genesis 4:25). The Pugad Lawin Shrine in Bahay Toro, Quezon City, has a historical marker (Episode 1's facts).
**Fiction, on purpose:** the world where the first killing never happened, the shepherd Abel alive with a baby, the baby being Indio (the series' fiction; the text names no child of Abel), the restored Philippines, Indio and M and their child, Tala, Inay, Papa, the dog, and Indio himself. The "tiyak, pinagtatalunan, kathang-isip" lines under every marker are the series' wish, not a real practice.
**Restraint:** God is given no words; nothing is put in Abel's mouth (he does not speak). Everyone is clothed. Abel is drawn as a generic young shepherd seen from the side or behind, never as a devotional likeness.
**Speakers:** INDIO only.
**Ending rule:** the episode ends exactly on "Ako 'to si Indio." The comment question comes before the last scene; there is no follow line after the last sentence.
**Label for captions:** "Ang tagpo ay kathang-isip ng serye; ang sanggunian ay ayon sa Bibliya (Genesis 4:2, 4:25). Walang anak si Abel sa Bibliya."
