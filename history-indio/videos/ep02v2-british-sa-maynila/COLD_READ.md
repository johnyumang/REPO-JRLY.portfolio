# Cold-reader test — Episode 2, version 2

**The test.** A reader who had seen nothing of the series (not even Episode 1) read the master script once,
comment lines removed, and retold it in five sentences.

**Result: passed.** The retelling, in the reader's words:

> 1. Si Indio, bagong delivery rider, ay naghatid ng pancit sa Intramuros para sa isang "M" pero walang tao roon.
> 2. Nang basahin niya nang malakas ang karatula, uminit ang medalyon niya at napunta siya sa Maynila noong
>    Oktubre 1762, habang binobomba ng mga Ingles ang pader.
> 3. Sinubukan niyang balaan ang guwardiyang Kastila pero itinulak lang siya at tinawag na "indio," tapos
>    bumigay ang pader.
> 4. Ibinigay niya ang pancit sa mga daga, na nagturo ng lumang imburnal, at nailabas niya si Sepa, ang lola
>    nito, mga kapitbahay at pati ang guwardiya, labing-apat lahat.
> 5. Sumuko ang Maynila, bumalik siya sa kasalukuyan na may pangalawang guhit sa braso, at nag-tip si M na may
>    mensaheng "Basahin mo nang malakas ang susunod."

Want, fall, comeback: he wanted to deliver his first order, then to warn people; he lost when the guard shoved
him and the wall fell anyway; he came back by giving the pancit to the rats and leading fourteen people out.

Questions that would bring the reader back: who is M and how does she know; what are the marks on his arm.

Best moment: "Paano mo nalaman ang pangalan ko?" / "Hindi pala pangalan. Tawag pala 'yon sa aming lahat," paid
off by Sepa's "'Yung pangalan, ha. Hindi 'yung tawag" and the guard's silent bow; then the pancit poured out.
Jokes that landed: the name gag; "Sundan mo ang daga, señor"; "Kinain po ng daga, noong 1762."

## What confused the reader, and what was changed after the test

| Finding | Change made |
|---|---|
| Who or what is Usman? He speaks with no introduction for someone who missed Episode 1 | A picture line (the black dog with the red kerchief steps out of the light) and Indio's greeting: "Usman! Ang asong madaldal." |
| "Oidor" is an unknown word | "May isang hukom na nakaalis bago bumagsak ang pader." (Anda was an oidor, a judge of the Audiencia; the trivia names him) |
| Does Sepa's tapis come back with him? | The return's picture line and Indio's line now say so: "At wala na akong pancit. Tapis na lang." |
| "Fast food na pala ng mga ninuno natin" fell flat | "Madaling lutuin, mahirap tiisin. Bawal ko kasing tikman." |
| The "late" joke twice | Said once in the hook; the second is now "Puwede ka palang mahuli kahit sa nakaraan." |

Left as it is, on purpose: "Top Rider: R.B." (a plant of the series; it is a picture only); the marks and the
hearing of animals (the series' open questions, each with one line here); "Bumigay na ang pader!" twice (the
hook is a flash-forward of scene 3, as the owner's rule asks). The reader found the present-day opening the
slowest part: it is held under 40 seconds and the pancit line is the owner's "today-thing".

A ten-year-old, per the reader: follows the rats, the pancit and the escape. A grandmother: follows the story
and likes the lola and the name; the app and the tip need clear, large on-screen text (shots 7, 41 to 43).

Not done: a second cold read of the corrected text.
