| The old records we found call Halia a rite (the video says "Sa mga lumang talang nahanap namin", not "oldest": Lisboa's Vocabulario de la lengua Bicol, compiled in the early 1600s, may have an older entry "halea"; not verified), not a goddess: Fr. | Later records say the rite was held to frighten off the Bakunawa (a Bikol dictionary of 1971, as quoted: "HALYA a ritual offered to the GUGURANG ... to frighten off the BAKUNAWA") | quoted by secondary sources; whether Castaño himself made the link is not confirmed, so the video says "ayon sa mga tala" | https://aswangproject.com/haliya , https://stephanie-gancayco-9jrs.squarespace.com/article/2017/1/12/haliya-full-moon-in-cancer |
