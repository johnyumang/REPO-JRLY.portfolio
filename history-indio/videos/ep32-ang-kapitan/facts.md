# Facts — Episode 32 (Season 4 Episode 1): Ang Kapitan (checked 2026-11-12; script version 1 from story version 2)

**What is true here:** the office of punong barangay and its minimum age in the law, the word *barangay* and its origin in *balangay*, and Juan de Plasencia's 1589 account of Tagalog customs, together with the modern debate about it. Everything in the plot of the barangay (the outgoing captain, the contractor, the flood wall and its papers) is invented.
