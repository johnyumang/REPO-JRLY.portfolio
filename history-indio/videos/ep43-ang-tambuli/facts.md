# Facts — Episode 43 (Season 5 Episode 2): Ang Tambuli (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 43)

**What is true:** The Butuan boats are the oldest known balangay: about eleven lashed-lug plank boats found or reported from 1976 at Sitio Ambangan, Libertad, Butuan (Agusan del Norte), first by people searching for alluvial gold near the Masao River; only some were excavated or examined by the National Museum (Lacsina: seven examined, three recovered), and samples from some of them are carbon-dated 689 to 988 CE. The boats were built by joining carved planks edge to edge with wooden dowels (pins) and fibre lashings. The word *barangay* is commonly said to come from *balangay*; how literally the unit of people followed the boat is debated. Butuan sent missions to the Northern Song court (1001–1011); in 1007 an envoy under the ruler Kiling (*Qiling* in the *Song Shi*) asked for the same rank as the envoys of Champa. The tambuli is a carabao-horn trumpet once blown to call people together. The tikbalang, kapre, siyokoy, kappa, nahual, Kraken and yeti are folklore (kuwentong-bayan).
**Label for captions (Season 5 rule):** "Totoo ang mga balangay ng Butuan; kuwento ng serye ang gunaw, ang digmaan, ang mga arko at ang pagtawag ng tambuli."
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio as President, Usman, M (Mikaela), Totoy (Emmanuel Abuyog) as captain, Tay Sebyo, Lolo Isidro, Bea, JM, Ma'am Celia, Pipay, Nanay Pacing, Nong Doming, Bapa Camar, Maestra Adela, Albo, Tala's kin, the arks, the flood and the war, the tambuli's power, the key and its five grooves, the hologram and the ka-Indio counter, the diwa of the twelve datu (light only, no words), and every creature as an ally.
