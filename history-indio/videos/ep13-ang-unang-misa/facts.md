# Facts — Episode 13 (Season 2 Episode 5): Ang Unang Misa (written from Kabanata 13, story version 1; checked 2026-11-12)

**What is true:** the first documented Mass in the Philippines, Easter Sunday 31 March 1521, by Fr Pedro de Valderrama, at the place Pigafetta calls Mazaua (Limasawa according to the NHCP; Butuan by the older belief, still disputed); Rajah Kolambu and his brother Rajah Siagu of Butuan (per Pigafetta); a blood compact and gifts reported; the marker on the hill (1950/1951). The Bakunawa as Visayan folklore, and the recorded custom of beating bells and pots during eclipses.
**Witness and respect:** the Mass is shown only from afar and treated with respect; Indio's joking and drowsiness are shown as his failing (Usman pulls him away), not as comedy about the Mass. Kolambu speaks only as the diwa (two invented lines, labelled).
**The fall (by suggestion only):** drink at noon, strangers on the yacht, nights without sleep; nothing explicit.
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio, M, Usman, the yacht and the guests, the medallion, the diwa's voice, Ang Kalakal and its limit, the fisherman, the eclipse of that night and the Bakunawa's appearance, the hologram. Usman is not in the present-day yacht scenes.
**The find:** the medallion of Rajah Kolambu, "Ang Kalakal" (dim). **Power used on screen:** the trade with the fisherman, and the true worth of the yacht, the drink and the guests. **Not recorded:** the Bakunawa (his first failure).
