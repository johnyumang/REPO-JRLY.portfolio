# Facts — Episode 45 (Season 5 Episode 4): Mula Mactan hanggang Sulu (written 2026-11-12 from story version 2, Kabanata 45)

**What is true:** On the morning of 27 April 1521, on the shore of Mactan, the warriors of Lapulapu defeated Magellan's landing party and Magellan was killed; Pigafetta has him killed by many warriors, so that Lapulapu killed him with his own hand is legend. Pigafetta's ruler of Mazaua in March 1521 is Rajah Kolambu (with his brother Siagu); the NHCP identifies Mazaua with Limasawa, and a Butuan identification is still argued. The blood compact (Sandugo) of Datu Sikatuna and Miguel López de Legazpi on Bohol is dated 16 March 1565. Rajah Tupas of Cebu signed the Treaty of Cebu with Legazpi on 4 June 1565 (some sources give 3 July), after Legazpi's attack on Sugbu. In 1639, when the Spanish entered Lanao, Sultan Kudarat of Maguindanao went to the Maranao datus; his famous speech is known only as reported by a Spanish source. Paduka Pahala (Paduka Batara), East King of Sulu, sailed to the Ming court in 1417, died at Dezhou in Shandong on the way home, and his tomb is still kept there. The sarimanok is the legendary bird of the Maranao.
**Label for captions (Season 5 rule):** "Totoo ang Mactan (1521), ang sandugo ng Bohol at ang kasunduan ng Cebu (1565), ang talumpati ni Kudarat (1639, ayon sa ulat ng Espanyol) at ang libingan ni Paduka Pahala sa Dezhou; kuwento ng serye ang gunaw, ang labanan at ang mga diwa."
**Fiction, on purpose:** Indio as President, Usman, M, the diwa of Lapulapu, Tupas, Sikatuna, Kolambu, Kudarat and Paduka Pahala and their powers, Kudarat's diwa line (the medallion exception, labelled in 62), Noy Kulas, Kalawang (Rufino Paredes) and the Alcaín Builders workers, Tubal, Tala on Tubal's boat, Totoy's bread, the multiplied bread, the Kampilan, the Busog and its twelve arrows, the Binti ng Atlantis, the Bakunawa as a mount, the sky battlefield, the flood and the war. Noy Kulas's line "Ayaw pag-atras" (Cebuano: "Don't retreat") is invented for an invented man.
**Usman:** present. **Power used:** Ang Kalasag, Ang Piging, Ang Sandugo, Ang Kalakal, Ang Tinig, Ang Daan Pauwi, the Kampilan, the Busog. **Knowledge:** 100%.

## Changes from the story chapter, and why
- Mactan: the script never says Lapulapu or his people "killed" anyone; it names the battle and, in the fairness beat, Pigafetta's report and the legend, as season2/research.md asks.
- Cebu: "sa mga simbahang puno ng tao" was dropped (length); the Galilee note of the story ("the series does not compare this small power with what Indio witnessed in Galilee") is kept by NOT making any comparison in the script.
- Limasawa: the story puts Kolambu "at Limasawa"; the script keeps the stop there and states the Mazaua dispute in the fairness beat.
- Kudarat's diwa speaks the story's three short sentences; nothing of the 1639 speech is put in Kudarat's mouth, and the real speech is named only as "ayon sa ulat ng isang Espanyol".
- The Busog is not said to come from Sakai in the line, to keep it short (Episode 16 gave it).
- Paduka Pahala's line "ang bahay ay kung saan may naghihintay sa'yo" is given to Indio as his own realisation, not to the diwa, to keep the number of designed voices down (no PADUKA line needed).
