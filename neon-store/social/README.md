# Social & ad crops

The ready-made crops live in `images/social/`. They are real photos (see `CREDITS.md`), cropped to the sizes ad platforms expect. This folder holds the notes. Put your finished ad creatives here too, e.g. `social/drop-01/`.

## Rules that keep ads approved and readable

- **Safe zones on 9:16:** keep text and logos out of the top 14% (about 270 px) and the bottom 20% (about 380 px). The app UI covers those areas.
- **Text:** keep overlay text short and under about 20% of the frame. Use the site fonts (Orbitron headline, Inter body) in cyan `#00F0FF` or magenta `#FF2BD6` on the dark parts of the photo.
- **One message per ad:** "New drop", "Free shipping over [THRESHOLD]" or a single product, not all three.
- **Match the landing page:** link sleeve ads to `shop.html?category=sleeves`, tops ads to `?category=tops` and leggings ads to `?category=leggings`, so the first thing people see matches the ad.

## Which crop for which campaign

- **Awareness (cold audiences):** 9:16 and 4:5 on-model crops, plus `images/hero/` wides for YouTube and display.
- **Retargeting (site visitors):** 1:1 product flat-lay and fabric (`square_texture_black_fabric_01.jpg`) with a short product line.
- **Launch countdown:** `square_neon_tube_lines_01.jpg` as a background for "Drop 01 — [DATE]" text.

## Before you spend money on ads

- The stock photos set the mood but **do not show your product**. No free photos of compression arm sleeves exist, so shoot your own sleeves, tops and leggings under neon and replace these crops.
- Some stock photos show other companies' logos on clothing or gear (a branded top in `athlete_man_compression_top_01`, branded items in the activewear flat-lay). Do not use those in paid ads. Platforms and brand owners can flag them. Crop the logos out or replace those photos with your own shoot.
- Keep exported creatives at 1080 px wide, JPEG quality 80–85, sRGB.
