## Free hosting decision (afternoon)
- Phone-only and nomad, so hosting on a PC is out. Render/Koyeb free tiers have 512MB, which is too little for Medusa. Hugging Face Docker Spaces now need a paid plan.
- **Chosen: Oracle Cloud Always Free (ARM, 24GB).** Card used for verification only. Kit is in `ops/deploy/oracle/`.
- Found and fixed two production-only problems: `medusa build` failed because the seed script imports `../content`, and Postgres timed out because production mode expects SSL (`?sslmode=disable` on the internal network).
- Email: no Google Workspace. Plan is Cloudflare Email Routing + Gmail "Send mail as" + Brevo SMTP, all free once a domain exists. Self-hosted open-source mail (Stalwart, Mailcow) was rejected for now: needs a server, port 25 is often blocked, and mail lands in spam.
- Order tracking: no WordPress. Medusa fulfilments carry the courier tracking number, plus a "Track my order" page.
