02 — Systems

Automations I built

AI agents that do the owner's daily checks across WooCommerce, Shopify and every marketplace. At peak the brand held #1 on 9–11 of 14 Amazon US money terms, tracked hour by hour.

01

Rank Tracker

Hourly Amazon organic rank ticker for the US and five European markets, tracking about 14 money keywords plus hero products. Captures top-10 results screenshots, flags up / down / flat moves and raises RED alerts, then sends the owner a daily rank card. A sister ticker tracks sponsored (ads) placement.

Amazon US · UK · DE · FR · ES · IT
02

Daily Sales Report

One card a day with every channel's sales: Amazon, Walmart, Etsy, Allegro, Mercado Libre, Shopify and WooCommerce. Auto-posted to the owner's group chat daily, so nobody has to open seven dashboards.

All 7 sales channels
03

Promos & Coupon Alarm

Daily promotions and coupons report across marketplaces, with an expiry alarm that flags coupons nearing expiry.

Amazon · Walmart · Shopify · WooCommerce
04

Stock Sync

Keeps inventory aligned across channels from one source, so a sale on one storefront updates the others and nothing oversells. A pixel watchdog also checks that the WooCommerce store's tracking stays live.

Shopify · WooCommerce · Marketplaces
05

Google AI Search Desk

Checks whether the brand appears in Google AI Mode and AI Overviews for “best …” buying searches, marks each term present or absent, and lists what to fix to get cited.

Google AI Mode · AI Overviews

Results

  • About 16,600 units sold over 15 months on about 430K page views
  • Growth came from conversion: page views rose only 7–10% YoY while units rose 45–57%
  • High-five-figure peak monthly sales
  • #1 on 9–11 of 14 core US search terms (2026)

What I did

  • Listings & SEO: titles, bullets, backend keywords, images, variations
  • PPC & coupons: Sponsored Products and Brands, promos timed with ad pushes
  • Buy Box & health: compliance, appeals, pricing guardrails
  • FBA restocks and 3PL prep to avoid stock-outs; hourly rank tracking

Source: Seller Central Business Reports. Index and conversion figures come from units and page views. No revenue is shown.
