# Episode 15 facts: the Vincent Black Shadow, 1948

Checked on 2026-11-20. B = https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Black_Shadow · F = https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollie_Free.

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Builder, place, year | Vincent H.R.D., Great North Road works, Stevenage, Hertfordshire; announced February 1948, produced until 1955 | B |
| Engine | air-cooled "998 cc (60.9 cu in) 50° four-stroke OHV V-twin", 84 x 90 mm, compression 7.3:1, 55 bhp at 5,500 rpm, dry sump | B |
| What was new | the engine is a stressed member, so no downtube cradle is needed; Series B/C: steel box upper frame member that doubles as oil tank | B |
| Frame | as above; Series D (1955): tubular member and a separate oil tank | B |
| Front suspension | Series B: Brampton girder fork; Series C onward: Girdraulic blade-type girder with hydraulic damper | B |
| Rear suspension | cantilever; Series D travel increased about 30% to 6 in (152 mm) | B |
| Brakes | dual 7 in (180 mm) single-leading-shoe drums, front and rear (the drums are not interchangeable) | B |
| Wheels and tyres | 3 x 20 front, 3.50 x 19 rear | B |
| Size | 458 lb (207.7 kg) dry, 500 lb (226.8 kg) wet; wheelbase 56.5 in (1,435 mm); gearbox four-speed, unit-constructed with the engine | B |
| Top speed | 125 mph (estimated) | B |
| Rider moment | Rollie Free, 13 September 1948, Bonneville Salt Flats, Utah: 148.6 mph on the first run, then "150.313 mph (241.905 km/h)" on the final run, lying prone on the machine "only [in] a bathing suit"; F: "raised the American motorcycle speed record" | B, F |
| Photo | taken "from a car driven parallel to his run"; "one of the most famous photographs in motorcycling history"; nickname "the bathing suit bike" | B, F |
| What came next | May 1952, Montlhéry, France: Vincent attempted endurance and speed records; Series D in 1955, the year the company stopped motorcycle production | B |

## Where sources disagree / not used

- Which bike Free rode: B lists bike 1B/900 as a Black Shadow but says the factory later called it a Black Lightning; F says it is "debated" and a Black Lightning is more likely, and that it was a works-modified machine. The video says "a Vincent" or "a Vincent V-twin", never settled as Black Shadow.
- Top speed of the road bike: B gives 125 mph (infobox, intro) but 122 mph in Motor Cycling's May 1948 road test of the prototype.
- Free's speeds: B also mentions an unofficial 138 mph test at Rosamond Dry Lake; not used.
- Cliff Brown's claim of a 12.75:1 compression engine for 1B/900 is disputed by Denis Minett; not used.
- Free's previous record holder and speed: not on the page. F's death year (1984 vs "1980 deaths") and the "rumored" $1.1 million sale: not used.
- Free's bike rode "lying prone on the fender" per B; the F page says only "prone". Swimsuit is "bathing suit" in the text.
