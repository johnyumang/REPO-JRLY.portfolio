1948: the fastest way to wear a bathing suit. 🩳

The Vincent Black Shadow was announced in February 1948 by Vincent H.R.D. in Stevenage, England. Its air-cooled 998 cc V-twin makes 55 bhp, and it is a stressed part of the frame, so there is no downtube cradle at all. It weighs 207.7 kg dry.

On 13 September 1948, Rollie Free took a Vincent onto the Bonneville Salt Flats in Utah. He lay flat on the machine wearing only a bathing suit and reached 150.313 mph, raising the American motorcycle speed record.

The photo is one of the most famous in motorcycling. Was it a Black Shadow or a Black Lightning? The sources differ.
