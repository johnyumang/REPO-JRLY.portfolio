## Where sources disagree / not used

- D'Ascanio's role: V says Piaggio contracted him to redesign the scooter; D says he designed it (first for Innocenti); a V reference says he "would move on to assist Piaggio with the Vespa in 1948". Video says "designed by" only with care: "D'Ascanio, an aircraft engineer, is credited".
- Predecessor MP5 "Paperino": V says designed 1944 and also "made in 1945 at Biella".
- D itself is inconsistent (mechanical vs aeronautical engineer; "helped" vs "designed"). Not used.
- Not stated / not used: weight, wheelbase, top speed, brakes, tyre size (none given); the Rome and Milan events have no exact dates; the page has no 1946 production count. Many launch statements are tagged "citation needed" in V.
