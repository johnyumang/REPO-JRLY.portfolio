1946: the scooter that looked like a wasp. 🐝

Piaggio built the Vespa 98 in Pontedera, Italy, with aeronautical engineer Corradino D'Ascanio credited with the redesign. No tube frame: a painted, pressed-steel body that carries the load, one of the first vehicles built this way.

A 98 cc two-stroke engine sits enclosed, right next to the rear wheel and drives it directly, with a three-speed twistgrip gear change. The wheels are only 8 inches, and the rear suspension is rigid.

Enrico Piaggio saw the prototype and said "Sembra una vespa!" ("It looks like a wasp!"). The first 13 appeared in spring 1946.
