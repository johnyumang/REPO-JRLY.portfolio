1980: the bike that started the adventure class. 🏜️

BMW Motorrad built the R 80 G/S in Berlin, Germany. It was "the first large displacement multisport bike on the market": a 797.5 cc flat-twin engine making 50 hp, a 21-inch front wheel and a 5-speed shaft drive instead of a chain.

Its trick was the "monolever": a single-sided rear swingarm joined with the drive shaft. Wet weight: 186 kg.

In 1981 Hubert Auriol won the Paris-Dakar motorcycle class on an R 80 G/S, finishing in Dakar on 20 January. He won again in 1983.
