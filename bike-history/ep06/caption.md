1885: a motorcycle made of wood. 🔥

Gottlieb Daimler and Wilhelm Maybach built the Reitwagen ("riding car") in a garden workshop in Cannstatt, Germany. Wooden beams for a frame, wooden wheels with iron tyres, two little side wheels to keep it upright, and a 264 cc one-cylinder engine making half a horsepower.

It is widely recognized as the first petrol motorcycle.

Its first trip, in November 1885, went from Cannstatt to Untertürkheim. The engine's hot ignition tube sat right under the seat... and the seat caught fire.

Who rode it first? Daimler's son Paul, or Maybach: the sources disagree.
