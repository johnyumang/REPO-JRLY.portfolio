1958: the most produced motor vehicle ever. 🌏

The Super Cub debuted in 1958, ten years after Honda was founded. It has a 49 cc air-cooled four-stroke single, a step-through pressed-steel frame, 17-inch wire wheels and drum brakes.

The trick: no clutch lever. A centrifugal clutch and a foot-operated gear change made it easy to ride.

It is called the most produced motor vehicle in history: over 60 million by 2008, and 100 million by 2017.
