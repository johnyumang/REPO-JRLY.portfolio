28 May 1907: the first Isle of Man TT. 🏝️

Riders lapped the St John's Short Course ten times, with separate classes for single-cylinder and twin-cylinder machines.

The twin-cylinder class went to Rem Fowler, a toolmaker by trade, riding a Norton with a Peugeot engine. His winning speed was about 36 mph, and his fastest lap was 42.91 mph, a new record.

Norton itself began in Birmingham in 1898 and was already fitting bought-in engines by 1902. Its own engines followed, with production models in 1908.
