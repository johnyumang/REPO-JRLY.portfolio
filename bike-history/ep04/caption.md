1903: put an engine on a bicycle. 🏍️

In Milwaukee, William Harley and the Davidson brothers built a motorcycle in a 10 by 15 foot shed in the family's backyard.

Their first engine was too weak to climb hills without pedalling. The next one had one cylinder, 405 cc, in a loop frame, with a leather belt to the rear wheel.

8 September 1904, State Fair Park, Milwaukee: Edward Hildebrand races it and finishes fourth.

The oldest Harley-Davidson known today sits in a glass case at their museum in Milwaukee.
