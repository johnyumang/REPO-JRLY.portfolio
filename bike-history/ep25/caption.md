1992: the bike that looked like the future. 🥇

Designer Mike Burrows drew it, and Lotus Engineering developed it as the LotusSport Pursuit Bike, the Lotus 108. Its frame was a carbon composite monocoque with an aerofoil cross-section, moulded from carbon fibre.

At the 1992 Barcelona Olympics, Chris Boardman rode it to gold in the 4000 m individual pursuit, catching world champion Jens Lehmann in the final. He also set a world record of 4:24.496 on the way.

Afterwards Lotus offered twelve factory replicas at £15,000 each.
