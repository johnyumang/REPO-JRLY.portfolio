# Episode 25 facts: the Lotus 108 (LotusSport Pursuit Bike), 1992

Checked on 2026-11-20. L = https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotus_108 · B = https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Boardman.
Only the first 100,000 characters of B were read.

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who built it | Norfolk-based designer Mike Burrows advanced the design; Lotus Engineering developed and marketed it. Rudy Thomann took the design to Lotus, Roger Becker backed it, Richard Hill refined it in wind-tunnel tests | L |
| When | by February 1992 Lotus had acquired the rights and marketed it as the LotusSport Pursuit Bike | L |
| What was new | frame with "an advanced aerofoil cross-section using a carbon composite monocoque"; monocoques were not new, but Burrows improved design and build with carbon fibre mouldings | L |
| Frame detail | described as a "uni-axle" design by Lotus Engineering | B |
| Wheels, brakes, drive, weight | not stated on either page | L, B |
| Rider moment | Chris Boardman won the 4000 m individual pursuit at the 1992 Barcelona Olympics, catching world champion Jens Lehmann in the final. Date given: 27 July 1992 (record table), Velodrom d'Horta, Barcelona | L, B |
| Times | world record 4 minutes 24.496 seconds "in the quarter-finals" (L); B's table lists 4:27.357 and 4:24.496, both dated 27 July 1992 | L, B |
| Numbers built | sixteen Type 108s built; twelve factory replicas offered at £15,000 each after the Olympics | L |
| What came next | publicity led Lotus to plan a production version, the Lotus Type 110 | L |

## Where sources disagree / not used

- Final time and the day of the final were not confirmed: B's table dates both times 27 July 1992 without saying which was the final; L puts the 4:24.496 record in the quarter-finals. Say only "1992 Barcelona Olympics" and "set a world record on the way".
- Names: "Lotus Type 108" vs "LotusSport Pursuit Bicycle": a naming difference only. Wikipedia title is Lotus 108.
- Not used: Shaun/Sean Wallace spelling, auction values ($200,000 unsourced, in dollars vs a pound price), museum location of Boardman's bike (conflicting), the hour records (B does not say which bike).
- Not confirmed: wheel type, weight, tyre sizes, brakes, drive.
