## Where sources disagree / not used
- "World's first production motorcycle" is the page's claim; the Reitwagen (ep06) came earlier as a one-off. Use "one of the first" or "first production" with care.
- Dates: infobox production 1894-1897, but the factory is said to have closed in 1919; not reconciled. Not used.
- Could not confirm that "Motorrad" was a name first used here; the page only says they built a "Motorrad".
- Not found: wheelbase, price, a first rider, any race.
