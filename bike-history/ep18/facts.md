## Where sources disagree / not used

- Introduction date: Tokyo Show November 1968 vs market launch 1969. The video says "1969" for production and "shown in 1968".
- Power 68 hp at 8500 vs 67 hp at 8000 rpm; top speed 125 vs "124 mph (200 km/h)" (Cycle World): the video says "about 200 km/h".
- Displacement: 736 cc here, 748 cc for later DOHC models; the video uses 736 cc.
- Mann's bike was the CR750 racer, not the road CB750; the exact day of the race is not given ("March 1970").
- Not on the page: Bol d'Or, wheel size apart from tyres, the Z1. Torque 60 N.m at 7000 rpm not used.
