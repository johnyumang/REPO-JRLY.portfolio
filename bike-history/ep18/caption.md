1969: the bike that gave us the word "superbike". 🚀

Honda showed the CB750 at the Tokyo Show in November 1968 and sold it in 1969. A transverse straight-four engine of 736 cc, a front disc brake, an electric starter and a double cradle frame, for US$1,495.

It weighs 218 kg dry, has a top speed of about 200 km/h, and is the first motorcycle called a "superbike".

In March 1970, Dick Mann won the Daytona 200 on a race version of it, the CR750, after Honda's three works bikes had all retired.
