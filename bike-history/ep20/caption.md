1975: the two-stroke that beat the four-strokes. 🏁

The Yamaha factory's 500 cc racer, the 0W23, is a liquid-cooled two-stroke four-cylinder of 494.7 cc, built around a chromoly frame.

Giacomo Agostini signed with Yamaha for 1974 after leaving MV Agusta, where he had won 13 world titles. In 1975 he rode this Yamaha to his fifteenth and final world championship.

It was the first time a two-stroke had won the premier 500 cc class.

Two years later the power valve arrived, and from 1978 Kenny Roberts won three titles in a row.
