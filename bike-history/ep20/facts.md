## Where sources disagree / not used

- Displacement: the 1975 engine is 494.7 cc (OW23); the video must not say "498 cc", which belongs to later OW60/OW61 engines.
- Agostini's 1975 title race: A says he "finished in second place to clinch" the title; the date, circuit and round are not on the pages I read. A also says Yamaha withdrew its road-racing teams after 1975 yet he rode Yamaha in 1977 (unresolved): not used.
- Roberts' first title: Y's 1977 entry says "first of three championships" on the 0W35K, but lists 1978-1980.
- Model code written "0W" with a zero in Y.
- Not on the pages: brakes, 1975 weight, power of the 1975 bike, wheelbase. Y does not say Agostini was the first two-stroke champion; only A does.
- Daytona 1974 was on the TZ750, not the YZR500, so it must not be told as a YZR500 win.
