Bicycles finally get pedals. 🚲

The 1860s, France. Put pedals and cranks right on the front wheel: one turn of the pedals, one turn of the wheel. Pierre Michaux's company made these velocipedes in large numbers.

Who added the pedals first? Michaux, his son Ernest, or Pierre Lallement. Historians still argue.

Wooden wheels, iron tyres, an iron frame. People called it the "boneshaker".

31 May 1868, Saint-Cloud near Paris: James Moore wins one of the first bicycle races, 1,200 metres to a fountain and back.
