1885: the bike you ride today is born. 🚲

John Kemp Starley's Rover safety bicycle, made in Coventry, England. The big change: a chain drives the rear wheel, so there is no need for a giant front wheel. Two wheels of about the same size, and the rider sits low between them, so far fewer falls over the handlebars.

The first Rovers steered through rods; then the bars turned the fork directly.

It is usually called the first recognizably modern bicycle. And in Polish, a bicycle is still a "rower", after the Rover.
