- "900" in model names (Z1-900, 900 Z1, 900 S4) vs a 903 cc engine; the video says "903 cc, called the 900".
- Date: text says 1972 but a cited article is titled "1973 Kawasaki Z1": the video says "launched 1972", sales years 1972-75.
- Power "82 PS (81 hp)" vs "82 bhp"; top speed "(210 km/h)" in text vs 209-212: the video says "about 210 km/h".
- The "fastest production bike" claim ends 1975; the 1983 return of the title is "citation needed": not used.
- Not on the page: quarter-mile time, race wins at Daytona 200 or Bol d'Or, Z1 launch city. The 24-hour record year and rider names are only partly stated (the record is dated 1972 in the summary).
