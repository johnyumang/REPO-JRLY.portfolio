## Where sources disagree / not used
- Date: October 1887 (D, first tyre developed) vs 1888 (T, "made in 1888"); the patent date, 7 December 1888, is clear. The video should say "1888" with the patent, not "invented in 1888".
- Thomson's date: 1845 (R, T: "appears to be" a standard pneumatic tyre, hedged), 1846 France, 1847 US (D gives 1847 only). Say "in the 1840s".
- Invalidity date: D says two years after the patent (about 1890); T says he was made aware in September 1890 and the patent was declared invalid in 1892. Not used as a date.
- "Invented" (D lead) vs "developed" (D body). Say "developed".
- D: commercial production "began in late 1890 in Belfast" but also "soon had them made commercially in Scotland". Not used.
- Hume's Liverpool date not stated; H infobox implies 3 of 4 wins, text says "all but one". The total of 7 wins is our inference: not used.
- Not found: Dunlop's own tyre construction (T does not mention canvas for Dunlop), tricycle wheel sizes, weight, wheelbase.
