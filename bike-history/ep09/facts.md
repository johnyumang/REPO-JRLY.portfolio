## Where sources disagree / not used
- Founding: I says Hendee Manufacturing was founded "in 1897"; I infobox says 1901; G says incorporated 1898; O/G say Hendee met Hedstrom in January 1900 and signed a partnership in January 1901; I says Hedstrom joined in 1900. Name "Indian" adopted "from 1898 onward" (I).
- Company name: "Indian Motocycle Company" vs "Manufacturing Company"; G says Hendee Manufacturing until renamed in 1923.
- Aurora engines: until 1906 vs about 1901 to 1907.
- 56 mph record: only I says it; no place or date within 1903.
- Not found: 1901 displacement, weight, wheelbase, tyres, brakes, any race win in 1901-1902.
