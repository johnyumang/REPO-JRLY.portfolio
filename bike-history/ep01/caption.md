The first bicycle had NO pedals. 🚲

Germany, 1817. Karl Drais built a wooden "running machine": you sat on it and pushed the ground with your feet.

On 12 June 1817 he rode it about 7 km out of Mannheim and back, in a little over an hour. No horse needed.

It caught on, until riders took to the smooth pavements and cities banned it.

Episode 1 of the history of the bike, from 1817 to today, one machine per episode.
Next: someone finally adds pedals.
